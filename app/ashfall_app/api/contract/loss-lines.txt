LOSS LINES
  A fight card may tell its lost fights from pools of its own (docs/design/week-four-plan.md, item 1): the lines the
  chronicle labels them with, the card's words for a lost fight, and a note on the journal's day page for each whole
  lost fight. Nothing here changes a number: the lost tally, the odds and the wasted inputs are FIGHTS' as built, and
  the engine is unchanged. Which card tells its losses this way is said by authored fields, never by a card's id or
  name. Fits into Combat (a FightDef's lost tally), Chronicle and unwrap, Knowledge (the composer reads the save's
  history) and Catalogue (the fields).

  In the chapter files, a card's `fight` table may carry these four fields, all of them or none:
    loss_pool       [key, ...], at least one: the line keys the chronicle words this card's lost fights from, in
                    place of lost_line_keys. Log lines like lost_line_keys: each needs its [line_kinds] entry.
    loss_notes      [key, ...], at least one, no key twice: the journal notes written for its lost fights (below,
                    "The composer"). Text keys, held to the text checks like any (no digit).
    on_loss_key     a text key: the card's words for one lost fight.
    loss_after      a FeatureId: the card may not be unlocked before this feature is.

  The opening's checks. Each refusal fails the load naming the card as "card <id>" (a standing action as "action
  <id>"), never text:
    - some of the four fields without the others: refused.
    - any of them on a standing action's fight: refused, naming the action.
    - on a card on a townsperson's lane (a ruled lane): refused.
    - on a card that a town card names in twin_of: refused.
    - on a card whose only_player is not "self", absent included: refused.
    - with loses or wounds non-empty: refused (a lost fight here costs only the attempt's inputs and adds no level).
    - with lost_line_keys non-empty: refused (one pool words the lost fights).
    - loss_notes holding a key twice: refused.
    - a loss_pool key, loss_notes key or on_loss_key not in text: refused, naming the key; a loss_pool key with no
      line kind: refused by the line kinds check, naming the key.
    - loss_after naming no feature: refused. A card that can be unlocked before loss_after's feature: refused. It can
      be when the start unlocks it, or when some outcome that unlocks it (a trigger's, a card's or its finish's, an
      answer's) does not have an outcome unlocking the feature among the outcomes that must have happened before it
      (the chain of waits unlock_order_checks.py reads). A card's requires does not count: it holds the card unready,
      not hidden.

  The wire: FightInfo gains three fields.
    loss_pool       [str] | null
    loss_notes      [str] | null
    on_loss_key     str | null
  On a card carrying the fields, all three are set, as authored and in authored order, and lost_line_keys is [].
  On every other fight, a card's or a standing action's, all three are null. loss_after is the loader's alone and is
  not served.

  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - on a card whose fight has on_loss_key, draw that key's words as its on-loss line, once, under its makes, in place
    of ui.fight.on_loss and ui.fight.on_wound (such a fight has no loses and no wounds). On every other fight the
    on-loss line is FIGHTS' as before.
  - in the chronicle, word each lost entry of a runner whose fight has loss_pool from loss_pool, exactly as FIGHTS
    words a lost entry from lost_line_keys: the k-th lost fight (k from 0, the runner's first lost fight ever) reads pool[bag[k mod n]], the same seeded bag keyed by
    the save, the runner, "lost" and floor(k / n), with pool = loss_pool and n its size. Which entries of a window read
    as lost, the won entries and the summary line (ui.fight.since) are FIGHTS' unchanged. A runner whose fight has no
    loss_pool words its lost entries as FIGHTS says (its lost_line_keys, else ui.fight.lost), never from any loss_pool.
  - show a lost fight's aftermath in the scene only as a scene layer the art adds (tools/scene), keyed by its own
    layer id (a transient id) and shown by the manifest's existing when and until conditions (a store such as the
    tally, a feature). Nothing on the wire names a pose.
  - draw a written day's loss and fold notes (below) on its days page like any day note, in their hand, in page order;
    a fold note's count drawn as digits beside its words in the UI font, as an entry's numbers are, never inside the
    words.
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT:
  - total a runner's lost fights anywhere beyond View.fights' lost: never add up fold counts or loss notes across
    days, or count them; a fold's count is drawn on its own day's note only.
  - word a lost entry of a runner with loss_pool from lost_line_keys or ui.fight.lost, or draw a loss_pool line
    anywhere but that runner's lost entries.
  - compose a loss or fold note, or draw one a served day does not carry.

  The composer (saves/journal_composer/losses.py, beside saves/journal.py's contract, whose statements hold except
  where these say otherwise). A loss runner is a card whose fight carries loss_notes. For each, the composer is given
  its id, its pool (loss_notes, authored order) and `lost`: its lost tally floored to whole fights at the day's end,
  game second (day + 1) * 86400, read off the save advanced to that second (FIGHTS invariant 81: the same however the
  steps fell). Its keys are picked by the composer's own hashed draws (Chance), never by the engine.
    L1. Only a day composed with its end (ended, and not broken off into a gap: a non-null gap, as saves/journal.py's
        contract sets it) gets loss notes, written at its end
        after the corrections, runners in id order. A day that broke off gets none; what it owed is owed to the next
        day that ends.
    L2. A runner's told count is the number of its loss notes plus the sum of its fold notes' counts, over every
        stored day before this one. owed = lost - told; at 0 or below nothing is written.
    L3. owed >= 1: one note, slot "loss", subject the runner's id, its key by L5. owed >= 2: after it, one note, slot
        "fold", subject the runner's id, key ui.journal.fold, count owed - 1. So each whole lost fight is told once,
        as a loss note or inside a fold's count, and a day holds at most one loss note and one fold note per runner,
        however long the absence that wrote it.
    L4. Loss and fold notes are outside the budget, are not tellings (w(S) is unchanged), are no subject's must, show
        no subject and hold no mark; the notes before them are those the day has without them. A day whose only notes
        are these is written.
    L5. The key. drawn: every note of the stored days and of the page so far, oldest first in page order, of any
        subject, whose slot is not "fold" and whose key is in the pool, so a trigger subject's note from the pool
        counts as a draw, and so does a loss note written earlier at this same day's end for a runner before this one
        in id order when its key is in this pool. size = the pool's size, r = len(drawn) // size, used = the keys in drawn[r * size:]. The
        candidates are the pool's keys not in used; when used is empty, drawn is not and size >= 2, the last drawn
        key is not a candidate (no key twice in a row). The key is the candidate whose SHA-256 of the UTF-8 bytes of
        f"{save_id}|journal|loss|{runner}|{r}|{key}" is smallest, compared as bytes. Each round of size draws uses
        every key once.
    L6. Equal arguments give an equal day (statement 1); a day composed again, or in more steps, keeps the loss and
        fold notes it had (statement 22).
  JournalDay's DayNote: slot gains "loss" | "fold"; DayNote gains count: int | null, at least 1 on a fold note and null
  on every other. A loss or fold note's subject is the runner's card id, its hand "pencil", its sketch null. The
  stored row's note gains "count" on a fold note.

  New text keys (Content.text_entries): ui.journal.fold   a fold note's words; its count is drawn beside them

  Where to test it (the seams; item 2's tests call these and nothing private):
  - The loader: opening.schema.load_opening(OPENING, (path,)) on a chapter file merged onto the opening; a refusal
    raises ValueError whose message begins "card <id>: " ("action <id>: ").
  - The wire: api.service.Game(opening, ruleset).content(), CardInfo.fight; openapi.json names the three fields.
  - The composer: saves.journal.compose_day(save_id, day, lines, composed, subjects, habits, ended=True,
    losses=()), losses a tuple of saves.journal.Loss(runner: str, keys: tuple[str, ...], lost: int); Note and
    ComposedDay as statement 1's, Note gaining count: int | None = None (set on a fold note only); SlotName gains
    "loss" and "fold". The day's lines and subjects may be empty: loss notes need neither.
  - The page: game/fights.ts lostPool(fight: FightInfo | null): readonly string[], the pool a runner's lost entries
    are worded from (loss_pool when set, else lost_line_keys, [] for null), which the chronicle's poolLine draws
    from; ui/ledger/fightpanel.ts FightPanel(catalog, info), whose .fight-loss node holds the on-loss line.

  Rejected, and why:
  - An event or timer in the engine per lost fight: an instant per fight (engine design/fights.txt, "FIGHTS", REJECTED).
  - A loss that takes stores or levels beyond the attempt's inputs, or adds a wounded store: the content's guardrail,
    held by the loader's loses and wounds refusal.
  - Telling keyed on the card's id or name: no behaviour from names; the fields say it.
  - A note per lost fight with no fold: a long absence would write a page of them.
  - Loss notes at the moment of each loss: the engine has no per-fight instant, and a note placed by when a step
    happened to look would differ between two ways of stepping the same day; the day's end is the same for both.
  - The folded fights drawing keys too: a draw nobody reads would only move the bag.
  - A fold count in the note's words ("three more"): the journal never writes numbers into its notes.

  Acceptance (item 1; item 2's blind tests read this section alone):
  1. A fixture chapter with a pooled card (the four fields) and a plain fight card loads; Content serves the pooled
     card's three fields as authored and the plain card's as null.
  2. Planted, each fails the load naming the card (the action): the fields on a standing fight; on a ruled lane's
     card; on a card with a town twin; with only_player "fight"; with only_player absent; three of the four; with a
     wound; with lost_line_keys; a loss_notes key twice; loss_after naming no feature; the card unlocked by a trigger
     that does not wait on the feature; the card in the start. Control: the fixture loads.
  3. The chronicle words the pooled card's lost entries only from loss_pool and the plain card's never from it.
  4. Composer: a day with k whole fights owed writes one loss note and, for k >= 2, one fold note of count k - 1;
     composing the same day again gives the same notes; a day broken off carries its owed fights to the next day that
     ends; across rounds no key comes twice in a row and each round uses every key; a trigger subject's note from
     the pool is the first draw.
  5. Nothing on the page totals the losses beyond the served lost.
