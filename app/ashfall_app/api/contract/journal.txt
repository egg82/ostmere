JOURNAL
  The book the player carries, written in: one page per thing known, holding the notes written about it and, for a
  thing fought, its learned numbers (decisions.md, "What you learn is written in a journal", "Blind until learned,
  perfect once known"). It is a reading of what the wire already carries: an entry and each of its notes are unlocks,
  and the numbers are View.fights. View gains nothing. The page draws the journal from Content.journal,
  view.unlocked and view.fights, and keeps what the player has read in AccountPresentation. Page design:
  docs/design/opening-screen/journal.md, "The journal". Fits into Unlocks, Knowledge (as a page reading), Lessons and the
  page shell; not a system (one caller, nothing to swap). The daily journal's written days are the one part the wire
  carries anew, on Step and Opened (below, "The written days").

  The book is in sections, a closed set, and each page is in one:
    days                the daily journal: the written days the server composes from the save (below, "The written
                        days"), never entries. Its pages are the page's own: days run on down a page until the next
                        would not fit.
    creatures           the things fought, with their numbers.
    places              places, events and expeditions.
  Within creatures and places, pages run in the order they are written. The content keeps that order: a section's
  pages are declared (chapters in load order) in the order their first notes are written on every route, so content
  order is writing order and nothing on the wire carries a time.

  In the chapter files:
    [journal]           feature: a FeatureId with a label_key (the book the player carries). Declared once, in the
                        chapter that holds the first entry or an earlier one; Content.journal is null until then.
    [[entries]]         id (an EntryId), title_key, section, sketch, hand, runners
      title_key         a text key naming the page in the contents list, short and in the book's hand; required on every
                        entry. It names only what the page itself shows. Days pages have none: they are headed by their day.
      section           "creatures" or "places"
      sketch            optional: the entry's drawing, an id matching [a-z0-9_]+, the image
                        /assets/journal/<sketch>.png. A page with none is laid out as text alone, which is valid.
      hand              "ink" or "pencil": how the entry's notes are written where a note does not say
      runners           the fight cards and standing fights whose numbers the page shows, in this order; [] for none
    [[notes]]           id (a NoteId), entry, gate, requires, hand, sketch
      entry             the entry the note is written on, declared in this chapter or an earlier one
      gate, requires    when it is written, as an offer's: the loader makes the trigger "note_" + id, whose outcome
                        unlocks the note and, for the entry's first note, the entry. Every other note's trigger also
                        requires the entry unlocked, so a later note is a later outcome (or the first's own instant),
                        never a note on a page that is not there.
      hand              optional: the entry's hand when absent
      sketch            optional: a small drawing beside the note, an id as for an entry
    [text] <note id>    the note's words: a note's id is its text key (there is no text_key field)
  An entry's first note is the first [[notes]] naming it in the chapter that declares the entry. A note's outcome is
  only those unlocks: it writes no line and adds to no store.

  The opening's checks (each naming ids, never text):
    - a note whose entry is not declared in its chapter or an earlier one: refused, naming the note and the entry.
    - an entry no [[notes]] in its own chapter names (no first note): refused, naming the entry.
    - an entry with no title_key, or whose title_key is not in text: refused, naming the entry.
    - a runner that is neither a card with a fight nor a standing action with a fight: refused, naming the entry and
      the runner.
    - a sketch id outside [a-z0-9_]+ (it is a path): refused, naming the entry or note.
    - an entry whose section is "days": refused, naming the section (the days pages are the written days).
    - an entry or a note while no chapter so far declares [journal]; [journal] declared twice; its feature unknown or
      without a label_key: refused.
    - a note with no [text] entry, or whose text holds a digit: refused by the existing text checks (Text and
      numbers). A note writes its numbers as words ("two dozen"); the exact numbers are served and drawn below it.
    - entry and note ids are content ids like any other, and content may rename or remove one: a save keeps the unlock
      it had (no manifest declares them), and the trigger the loader made for a removed note is dropped with it (engine
      engine/design/, "Rebase").

  Engine: UnlockKind gains entry (its id an EntryId) and note (a NoteId), ordinary kinds (not PRESENT_ONLY); engine
  design/core.txt, "Unlocks", lists them. The engine never reads them. Gates read
  them as any unlock (subject "unlocked" with unlock_kind entry or note), so a gate on several entries is one Unlocked
  condition each, and no count store mirrors them.

  Content.journal: JournalInfo {feature, entries: [EntryInfo {entry, title_key, section, sketch, runners: [str], notes:
  [NoteInfo {note, hand, sketch}]}]} | null. entries in content order (chapters in load order), each entry's notes in
  content order; within one section, content order is writing order. Section is a closed enum, "creatures" |
  "places". EntryInfo.sketch is null for a page with no drawing.
  NoteInfo.hand is resolved (the entry's where the note names none), so EntryInfo carries no hand and the page applies
  no default. NoteInfo.sketch is null for a note with none. A note's words are Content.text_entries[note]. Hand is a
  closed enum, "ink" | "pencil": each hand is a style the page owns, so adding one is page work as well as content.
  The sketch images live on the assets volume (engineering.md, bulk assets) at /assets/journal/<sketch>.png: ink on
  transparent, drawn by docs/research/sketch-test/process.md. An entry's sketch is 1200 by 1200 (the method's 600 by
  600 page at twice), holding the figure, its studies, a crossed-out try and the labels pointing into it, and no
  caption or free notes: those are the entry's notes. A note's sketch is at most 600 by 600. The words drawn in a
  sketch are art: no text entry holds them and no check reads them.

  AccountPresentation gains journal_read: [Name] (at most MAX_LIST): the notes on pages the player has turned to.
  Empty on a new account. A note is unread while view.unlocked holds it and journal_read does not; read is kept for
  the account, as ledger_lines is, so a note read in one game is read in the next.

  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - draw Content.journal.feature as the Journal button, in the book's place with the chips, once view.unlocked holds
    that feature and the book has a page (a written day, or an entry view.unlocked holds); until then the book is
    plain text, as any feature with a label_key. The button's word is the book's label_key. It carries the quiet dot
    while anything written is unread (a note, or a written day after newest_day_read), and at no other time.
  - open the Journal sheet from it: the days pages, then one page per entry view.unlocked holds, creatures then
    places, each section's entry pages in Content.journal.entries order. It opens on the first page holding something
    unread, else the page last shown in this page session, else the first. Prev and Next turn one page at a time,
    across sections.
  - fill the days pages with the written days in day order: each day's notes in page order under its day's heading
    (ui.journal.day {n}, n the game day plus one), in the note's hand and with its sketch beside it, days running on
    down a page; a day begins on the page in hand if its heading and first note fit, and a note that does not fit
    begins a new page, headed again by its day. Whether a note fits is a fixed measure of its words (rows of the hand
    at the desktop page's width), never the window, so the same days are the same pages on every device and width,
    and a page, once a later one is begun, never changes. A day served while its page is open joins it, or grows on
    it, and stays unread.
  - draw one bookmark per section that has a page, in the same order, worded by its text key (ui.journal.days,
    ui.journal.creatures, ui.journal.places); a section with no page written has no bookmark. Turning to a bookmark
    opens that section's first page holding something unread, else its latest page. The bookmark of the open page's
    section is marked as the current one.
  - turn Prev and Next within the open page's section, worded by the neighbouring section's bookmark where the turn
    crosses into it, and list a section's pages by title on request (ui.journal.contents): a days page by its first
    day, an entry's page by its first note's words.
  - on a page, draw the entry's sketch and each of its notes view.unlocked holds, in content order and in its hand,
    with the note's sketch beside it. A note not unlocked is not drawn and keeps no room. A page whose sketch is null
    is laid out as text alone: no empty room is kept for a drawing, nothing is loaded and nothing is reported.
  - run the journal's lesson's numbers step only on a page that shows numbers: on a page with none, the lesson ends
    (kept) after its notes step, and the numbers step runs as a lesson of its own the first time a page with numbers
    is open. A step that waits on numbers never holds the lessons after it.
  - under the notes, draw for each of EntryInfo.runners that view.fights holds, in runners order, the runner's label
    (its card's label_key, or its standing action's) and its numbers exactly as the fight card words them (FIGHTS):
    with fought 0, ui.fight.odds_unknown and ui.fight.vs with ui.fight.threat_unknown, and no tally; with fought
    above 0, the odds (ui.fight.odds, else ui.fight.odds_pct), ui.fight.vs with both values, and ui.fight.tally
    {fought}{lost}. Known means fought above 0, read off the view and stored nowhere (systems.md, Knowledge). A runner
    view.fights does not hold (not unlocked) draws nothing. The numbers are redrawn at each view while the sheet is
    open; a strength's entries do not open here (the fight card's do).
  - count the notes of each page the player turns to (opening counts as turning to the page it opens on) as read,
    and send them once, as the sheet closes: one PUT /api/me/presentation whose journal_read gains exactly those
    notes, every other field as kept, and, when the newest day on the days pages turned to is later than
    newest_day_read, one PUT /api/saves/{id}/presentation with that day. A note unlocked while its page is open stays
    unread until the page is turned to again.
  - load a sketch when its page is drawn; one that fails to load leaves its room empty (no broken-image mark, no
    stand-in drawing) and is reported to /api/client-errors with the sketch id.
  - mark the pages (docs/design/journal.md, "Marks on the page"): on every page, one or two wear marks chosen and
    placed by draws keyed by the save and the page's id; on a days page, each of its days' marks, at most two a
    page, a variant chosen and placed by draws keyed by the save, the day and the kind. The art is
    /assets/journal/marks/ and its manifest.json; a mark is kept only where its box crosses no line of words, no day
    heading and no drawing, else the next draw is tried, and one that finds no room is not drawn. A manifest or
    image that fails to load draws no marks and is reported to /api/client-errors once.
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT:
  - send anything because of the sheet before it closes: opening it and turning its pages cause no decision,
    advance, dry run, forecast or presentation write (the session's own advances at view.next_event_at go on as
    ever). The read marks wait for the close.
  - draw a figure for a runner whose fought is 0, take an entry's numbers from anywhere but view.fights, or draw an
    entry or note view.unlocked does not hold.
  - write a note's words anywhere but its page: the story strip and the chronicle carry lines, not notes.
  - draw a mark over the words, or a mark a served day does not carry (wear aside).

  The written days (docs/design/journal.md): the main character's daily journal, which the server composes from the
  save's history, never the page. Game day d is game seconds [d * 86400, (d + 1) * 86400). A day's page fills as the
  day goes: each step composes the day still being written and each day it begins, oldest first, in its transaction,
  and a save that has none stored (one begun before the journal was kept) composes every day it has begun on its next
  step. A note is decided from the lines up to it, so a day comes out the same however the steps fell. A note once
  stored never changes or goes: a step only adds notes and marks after those a day has, and a later change to the
  habits or the slots applies from the next note composed.
    Step.journal_days    [JournalDay]: each written day this step added notes to, whole (every note it has), oldest
                         first. Empty on most steps.
    Opened.journal_days  [JournalDay]: every written day stored before the open, oldest first, the day in progress with
                         the notes it had then. The open's step carries the days it added to, so the two together,
                         each day taken as the copy with more notes, are every written day.
    JournalDay {day, day_notes: [DayNote {subject, slot, key, hand, sketch}], marks}: a day the journal wrote, its notes in
                         page order (at least one); a later copy of the same day begins with the notes and marks of an
                         earlier one. A day not written (a gap or a blank) is stored and never served.
                         key is the note's text key (Content.text_entries); slot is a closed enum, "first" | "repeat" |
                         "catchup" | "mention" | "late" | "misremember" | "correction"; hand is the slot's own, else
                         "ink"; sketch the slot's, else null (/assets/journal/<sketch>.png). subject is the content's
                         subject id. marks: the marks the day leaves on its page, each a closed enum, "blood" | "soot"
                         | "water" | "mud" | "smudge", each at most once, in the order drawn, stored with the day
                         (saves/journal.py, statement 21); empty for none.
  SavePresentation gains newest_day_read: int | null, the newest written day whose page the player has turned to,
  null on a new save; a written day after it is unread. It only grows: a PUT whose value is lower, or null, leaves the
  kept one. The days are the save's own diary, so how far into them the player has read is kept per save, where the
  book's notes, which every game shares, are read per account (journal_read).
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT:
  - compose a day, or draw a written day neither Opened nor a Step carried.

  New text keys (Content.text_entries): ui.journal.heading | ui.journal.prev | ui.journal.next   the sheet's heading
                                    and its page turns
                                    ui.journal.contents   the button that lists the open section's pages
                                    ui.journal.day {n}   a day's heading on a days page

  Rejected, and why (the journal on the wire):
  - A journal object or a known set on the View: each fact is already there (view.unlocked, view.fights), and the
    engine has no use for a known set until a fork reads one (systems.md, Knowledge). A second home would drift.
  - A `journal` feature beside the book: the page draws the journal from its first entry ("The screen arrives when it
    means something"); a feature would be a second switch for what the entry unlock already says.
  - A count store of entries for gates: one Unlocked condition per entry says the same with nothing to keep in step.
  - Notes inside [[entries]]: a later chapter writes notes on an earlier chapter's entry, and chapters merge on with
    new ids only, so a note is its own table naming its entry.
  - Read marks per save, or in the browser: what the player has read is theirs across games, and a browser store is
    lost on another device. Written at the close rather than per page so reading sends nothing.
  - Every word in the sketch (process.md's notes files): notes grow as the player learns and pass the content's text
    checks, so they are text; the sketch keeps only the words that point into the drawing.
  - A note that strikes an earlier one, and a contents page: nothing in the first entries needs either.
  - A written-at time on the wire to order a section's entry pages: content order is writing order, which the played
    routes hold, so a time would be a second home for the same fact.
  - The days pages' breaks on the wire, or paged by the window: the page's measure is fixed, so the pages follow from
    the days alone, and a break the window chose would differ between a phone and a desktop.
  - The days not written on the wire, with why: the page draws nothing for them, and a gap's cause is the composer's.
  - Every written day on every Step: Opened carries the stored ones once, and a step carries only those it composed.
  - A day's hand left for the page to default: as NoteInfo.hand, the server resolves it and the page applies none.

  Acceptance (the first slice is done when each passes; server tests under app/tests, client tests under
  app/client/test, as their neighbours):
  1. A fixture chapter with [journal], one entry and two notes loads: Content.journal holds the entry with both
     notes in order and each hand resolved; the loader made note_<id> for each; the first's outcome unlocks the
     entry and the note, and the second's requires the entry unlocked.
  2. Planted, each fails the load and names its ids: a note on an entry no chapter declares; a note on an entry a
     later chapter declares; an entry with no note in its chapter; a runner with no fight; a sketch id holding "/";
     an entry with no [journal]; [journal] twice; [journal] on a feature with no label_key; a note text holding a
     digit. Control: the same fixture without the plant loads.
  3. On fixture rules, a save reaching a first note's gate holds the entry and note unlocks from that instant; a
     later note whose gate holds first is not unlocked until the entry is, then lands at that instant.
  4. A changed journal: a save keeps the entry and note unlocks it had across a rebase into rules that rename them.
  5. Wire: Content.journal is null for rules with no [journal] and served in content order otherwise; openapi.json
     names JournalInfo, EntryInfo, NoteInfo and the Hand enum as components; the field-names test holds; journal_read
     round-trips through PUT /api/me/presentation, refused past MAX_LIST or with a name a Name refuses.
  6. The Journal button is absent with no entry unlocked (the book drawn as plain text) and present with one.
  7. A runner with fought 0 draws ui.fight.odds_unknown and ui.fight.threat_unknown and no digit in its odds, threat
     or tally; one with fought above 0 draws the served odds as oddsOf words them, both values and the tally; a
     runner not in view.fights draws nothing.
  8. With the clock held (no next_event_at passes), opening the sheet and turning to every page sends no request;
     closing sends exactly one PUT /api/me/presentation, whose body equals the kept presentation but for
     journal_read gaining exactly the shown pages' notes; no request under /api/saves/ is sent from open to close.
  9. The button's dot shows while a note is unread and not otherwise; the sheet opens on the first entry with an
     unread note; after a close with every unread page shown the dot is gone; a note unlocked while its page is open
     keeps the dot after the close.
  10. The layout's draws for an entry are equal across two saves and two page loads; over a fixed list of ids at
      least two pages differ; adding a later note leaves every earlier note's draws unchanged.
  11. The journal's lesson starts on the button's first showing, walks its steps, and is kept in the save; it
      never returns once kept.
  12. Renders at 390x844 (portrait page) and 3840x2160 and 1280x800 (spread), with one entry fought and one not, a
      pencil note and a note sketch, looked at (headless-render); the page is the game's one dark theme
      (color-scheme: dark), so the renders are repeated with the browser set to prefer light and must not differ.
      tests/client/test_client_contrast.py passes with the hand's colours on the parchment.
  13. Each sketch is looked at, by process.md's method, before its entry ships.
