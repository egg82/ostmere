A FEATURE'S CARD
  A FeatureInfo with a feature_card is shown as that card once, when the page first sees the feature unlocked: a View
  after one without it, live or on a return (Opened.was without it, the step with it). It waits until what arrived with
  that View has been revealed and its lines read, then docks at the panel's foot (along the bottom edge on a phone),
  with nothing dimming the scene: the title, each of line_keys, and one button (button_key) that puts it away. Nothing
  else waits on it, and play goes on under it. A page whose first View already holds the feature (a reopen, a redraw
  for a deploy) never shows it, so a card put away stays away and nothing is kept for it. The content's end card is one.
