GOAL STINTS (docs/design/worth-a-change.md, "What to add first", item 1; engine DESIGN.txt, "Queue mix")
  A goal stint is a queue entry {verb: switch_mix {mix_id}, until: a GoalRow's until}: "run {mix} until {goal}". The
  server holds no stint type: set_queue takes it as any entry (a hands mix the save holds, at most
  Content.limits.max_queue entries; a town mix is 422 invalid_decision), and every rule below holds for any queue
  entry whose verb is a switch_mix, whatever its until.
  - Controlling: the first entry of view.queue whose verb is not dead (a switch_mix to a mix in view.greyed_mix_ids, a
    tap_card of a completed card, which leaves view.queue at that instant) controls. While a switch_mix entry controls and no order's mix runs (view.mix_order
    null), view.running_mix_id is that entry's mix_id; view.active_mix_id, the player's own mix, is never written by
    the queue. An order whose mix runs takes the hands over the stint (view.mix_order set), and the stint's mix runs
    again when that order lets go.
  - Popping: the controlling entry leaves view.queue at the first instant its until holds, also while an order's mix
    runs, and at once when it already holds as the entry comes to control (a set_queue whose stint's goal is already
    met pops it in that same decision). The next live entry then controls: its mix runs if it is a switch_mix; else
    view.running_mix_id is view.active_mix_id again (the order's mix aside) and a tap_card entry taps when it can.
    A goal stint on a GoalRow whose lands_at is t, set while the rates as set stay unchanged until t, pops at exactly
    t.
  - The player's switch_mix while a stint's mix runs changes view.active_mix_id alone: the stint's mix keeps running
    and the switched-to mix runs once the queue gives the hands back. delete_mix of the active mix leaves
    active_mix_id null and the stint's mix running; delete_mix of a mix a queue entry names is 422 (as before).
  - Unreachable: an until that never holds (its goal row's lands_at null: nothing moves the goal at the rates as set,
    the store falls, or a store target over its cap) is not an error, and nothing in the server passes the entry over,
    pops it or removes it: it controls and its mix runs until the until holds (the rates change and the goal lands)
    or the player sends set_queue without it, and every entry behind it waits. A skill that has reached its phase's
    top cannot reach a higher counted level until the player's prestige click, so a skill_level until above that level
    does not hold before the click. A stint whose mix greys is dead: passed over, the entry behind it controlling, and
    kept, except that it leaves view.queue at the instant its until holds and cannot fall (an at_least on a skill_level,
    as every level row's until is); a dead stint on a store until is kept even while that until holds (engine
    DESIGN.txt, "Greyed mix").
  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - in the mix editor, offer ui.stint.run under the goal rows, never while Save is unavailable, the queue is at
    max_queue, or the editor makes a mix for a rule. Pressed, it lists each row of the edit's dry run whose lands_at
    is neither null (an Unreachable stint would never pop, and would hold every entry behind it) nor the dry run's
    `at` (a met goal would pop at once): the goal (ui.goal.store, ui.goal.level) and its time left in the dry run,
    never sorted or marked as better; under a store's row (one that lands: the store rises), after
    ui.stint.or_until, the named shares of that store's cap (a quarter, half, three quarters, full) its whole items
    have not reached, each an until at_least {store_value, value = release = b, both flags true} with no time (none
    is served for it). Picking one sends save_mix (with saveDecisions' renames) when the edit differs from the saved
    mix, then set_queue with view.queue and the stint {switch_mix {mix id}, until: the row's until or the share's} at
    its end, and closes the editor.
  - in Next (the Rules tab's queue), word a switch_mix entry ui.stint.entry {mix} {goal}: the goal from the row of
    view.goal_rows whose until equals the entry's (kind, subject, value, release), else from the until itself (a store
    at value, a share by ui.goal.store_share {store}{n}, a skill at the counted level). A share until never matches a
    row. On the controlling entry while its mix runs, add the row's time left
    (ui.goal.in, or ui.goal.never for a null lands_at) and its line, as the editors' rows word it (ui.goal.limited, the
    store's label or ui.goal.xp). Nothing else: no warning, offer or removal for a goal that never lands.
  - in Next, offer ui.queue.plan (while the queue has room and a saved hands mix is not greyed or a card would take
    When ready): pressed, it lists first each card the queue takes (one not on a ruled lane, after the end, with room
    under its cap for one more run than those planned): steps and action cards, ready now ones and a card a lane runs
    included, worded ui.queue.when_ready, whose pick appends one run of it; a card's own When ready, which sends the
    same entry, is offered on a step only (not an action card), not queued already and not the card its lane runs, while
    it cannot be tapped (unready, or ready on a lane whose running_card is another card). Then each
    step that would take it but for its blocker (no ready time kept,
    or a store the view has not unlocked), not pickable, worded as the card draws its blocker's need (ui.card.needs,
    ui.card.opens, a skill level or step; a step whose card draws nothing for its blocker is left out); then the saved
    mixes. A mix picked is worded ui.stint.which {mix}, with the goals of the dry run of switch_mix to it (the live
    view's when it already runs) listed as the mix editor lists them. Picking a goal sends set_queue with view.queue
    and the stint at its end. New text keys: ui.queue.plan | ui.stint.which {mix}.
