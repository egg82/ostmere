TEXT AND NUMBERS (one rule)
  A number lives once, in the rules or the view; text never repeats it. Text entries hold words and named slots
  ({name}), never a digit (the opening's checks fail one). The page fills a slot only from the served value named
  below, formatted this way and no other:
    {n} as an amount   whole items: floor(quantity / Scales.quantity_per_item)
    {n} as a rate      items per minute: rate / Scales.rate_per_item_hour / 60, toward zero, no trailing zeros, two decimals at most (more only under 0.01, down to the first digit), sign unless zero
    {have}             a store's amount now in whole items, sampled from the segment beside it
    {cap}              a store's cap in whole items
    {h} {m} {s}        a duration in seconds, rounded up to the second: ui.duration.h on a whole hour, ui.duration.hm
                       from 1 h, ui.duration.ms from 1 min, else ui.duration.s; {d} is a whole duration formatted that way
    {m}                a rule's other level, in whole items (as {n})
    {store} {card} {skill} {chip}   the label of that store, card, skill or chip
    {<lane>}           a person's name: the text of that lane's LaneInfo.name_key (the slot is the lane id, "{n2}"),
                       filled in every text entry, labels and lines alike; in a line (a key of Content.line_infos) it is
                       lowercased, as the lines' voice writes every word. A slot naming a lane with no name_key fails
                       the opening's checks; a slot's name may hold a digit, the text around it may not
    {found}            the amount a find found, in whole items (as {n} as an amount), filled in the one line its FindInfo
                       {line_key, store, quantity} (Content.find_infos) names; the opening's checks hold that line to
                       exactly one {found} and no other text to any
    {mix}              a mix's id, as it is; for a rule that taps a card, that card's label
    {pct} {pct_b}      the first and second of a served change_bp (LevelPays, CampLevel, CardInfo, ChipInfo: a modifier's
                       signed change in basis points, +1000 for a tenth more; a CardInfo's include the entries it holds
                       while it runs, at its full share, then those of the standing action its content names as
                       upside_of, the runner whose held entries its upside describes: a recipe whose make another
                       runner eats names that eater, and the page draws the eater's change on the recipe; then, for a
                       card whose content gives it a reach, those of the triggers on the store it fills; where a
                       person's share of the card is chosen, the page draws each change at a CardInfo.kept slot as
                       floor(amount_bp * share / Scales.bp), the engine's rounding, and the rest as served; a card's face
                       draws them at its Card.share_bp in the running mix, and all at the full share while that is 0) as a signed
                       percent: change / Scales.bp * 100
                       toward zero, no trailing zeros, two decimals at most (a change that is not zero never reads as
                       zero), sign unless zero ("+10%", "-33.33%"). The text names what changes and carries no direction
                       word the sign already says; a text holds exactly as many of these slots as its change_bp has values
    {gain} {gain_b}    the first and second of an AnswerInfo's gains, each in whole items (as {n} as an amount), in an
                       answer's cost_key text only. {found} is a find line's alone, so an answer's amount has no
                       slot there; the text names the store itself
    {minutes}          how long a transfer code works, in whole minutes: floor(Content.transfer_code_s / 60)
    {number} {level}   a save's SaveInfo.number and camp_level, as they are
    {max} {days}       Games.max_saves (on a join's refusal, MergeSummary.max_saves) and Games.kept_days, as they are
    {started} {played} {until} {seen}   SaveInfo.started_at, played_at, DeletedSave.purge_at and Device.last_seen_at as a date in the browser's
                       own locale and zone: Intl.DateTimeFormat with day numeric and month short
  A text holds only the slots above that its key's kind is filled with (a person's, {found} on a find line, {pct} where a
  change is served, a ui key's own); any other fails the opening's checks, naming the key and the slot.

  A content order card marked `default` is not served: the loader makes it a trigger that installs its order, on a ruled
  lane's card, once its offered_after (and, with after_end, the end) is unlocked (RuleInstall.when); the player edits or
  removes it as any rule. Order cards carry no text: `when` is an at_most on the card's store, worded as a store rule (above) or, for a
  card with a `chip`, as that chip, and `then` taps a card, worded by its label. Content order cards only tap cards; a mix is the player's, so no order card names one.
