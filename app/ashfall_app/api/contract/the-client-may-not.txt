THE CLIENT MAY NOT
  - simulate: no rule, rate, cap, completion or unlock is computed on the page (a mix bar's modulo is sampling, not
    a completion: the count is Card.completions). Past next_event_at, ask again.
  - decide what exists, readiness, chip state or what a line says: all of it is in the view or the content. When
    something that exists is first drawn follows the rules above (a chip's offered_after, the Camp bar's first level,
    a ledger line's first meaning).
  - write a number or an id into text other than through a slot: no text entry holds one. A mix's id is the
    player's name for it and is shown as it is.
  - send a decision it thinks will fail to save a request; the server's 422 is the answer.
