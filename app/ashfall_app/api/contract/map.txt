MAP
  The world map (docs/decisions.md, "A world map"): a page of the Book showing where the town is and where the places
  found so far lie from it, drawn from engraved stamps placed by code. A place is content; whether it is revealed is
  view.unlocked's (its reveal unlock), so View gains nothing and no decision changes. Fits into Town map and world map
  (its first caller: a module until a second caller or variant), the page shell (a Book page), Unlocks and Lessons.
  What stands where inside the town is not this section.

  In the chapter files:
    [[places]]          id, stamp, at, reveal, label_key
      id                a PlaceId, a content id like any other, unique among places
      stamp             the kind of stamp drawn for it, a closed set the page owns art for: "village" | "town" |
                        "city" | "fort" | "stronghold" | "hill_village" | "crossing" | "mountain" | "range" |
                        "forest" | "tree"
      at                [east, north]: whole cells from the town, which stands at [0, 0], north up; each from
                        -MAP_REACH to MAP_REACH (8). Coarse on purpose: the content says roughly where, the page
                        places the art.
      reveal            {unlock_kind, id}: the unlock that reveals it, written as an aim's `after`. unlock_kind is
                        one of feature, card, skill, store, chip, action (some outcome or the start adds it) or
                        entry (a journal page, which its first note writes).
      label_key         optional: the place's name, a text key; absent for a place drawn unnamed (a forest, a range).

  The opening's checks (each naming the place, never text):
    - a place with no reveal, or a stamp outside the set: refused by the shape.
    - a reveal of another unlock kind; a reveal naming an id its kind does not know; a reveal of a kind other than
      entry that no outcome and not the start adds (nothing would ever reveal it): refused, naming the place.
    - two places on one cell, in one chapter or across chapters, or a place on the town's cell [0, 0]: refused,
      naming both places (the one place).
    - an at outside the reach: refused.
    - a label_key that is not in text: refused by the text checks.

  Content.places: [PlaceInfo {place, stamp, east, north, reveal: Unlock, label_key}], every place in content order
  (chapters in load order); empty when no chapter declares one. stamp is a closed enum, Stamp. label_key is null for
  an unnamed place.

  The stamps: /assets/art/map/manifest.json, {stamp: [{src, w, h}]}, each image ink on transparent at
  /assets/art/map/<src>, w by h pixels at the sheet's base scale. Made by
  tools/mapstamps.py from the Widman 2.1 PNG pack (docs/research/art/art-sourcing.md, CC0, outside the repo) and seeded
  with the art kind (engineering.md, bulk assets); never in git or the image.

  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - offer the Map as the Book's last page, its button worded ui.book.map, while view.unlocked holds the reveal of at
    least one place; until then it has no button, and nothing of it is drawn or fetched.
  - draw the sheet, north up: the town's stamp at [0, 0], in the warm ink of what is the player's own, labelled with
    the town's shown name (THE TOWN'S MIX: ui.town.title or its later label); then each revealed place's stamp at its
    cell, in the cool ink of what was found. The frame holds every revealed cell and the town with a cell of margin,
    at least two cells each way from the town, widened along the longer side to fill the sheet.
  - choose each place's art from its stamp kind's variants by a shuffle bag keyed by the save and the kind, walked
    over every place of the kind in content order, revealed or not, so a place keeps its art as others are revealed,
    and no variant repeats on a sheet until every variant of the kind is used. The town is a village: the first draw
    of that bag is its art, the village places' after it.
  - put each label in clear space beside its place, with a dark halo: the first of the spots tried (right, left,
    below, above, then the same further out) whose box crosses no stamp, no other label and not the sheet's edge. A
    label that finds no such spot is not drawn on the sheet.
  - list the revealed places that have a label, in content order after the town, beside the sheet on a desktop and
    under it on a phone; a tap on a row marks its place on the sheet until another row is tapped or the page is left.
  - dot the Book's tab when a place is revealed after the first view, until the Book is opened.
  - teach the page once, the first time its button is on show: a lesson on the button itself (ui.lesson.map_open),
    then on the sheet (ui.lesson.map_places); kept in the save as other lessons are.
  - load the manifest when the page is first offered; a manifest or image that fails to load leaves the sheet's
    stamps undrawn (the labels and the list stay) and is reported to /api/client-errors once.
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT:
  - draw, label or list a place view.unlocked does not reveal, or say how many places are still to find.
  - draw two stamps whose boxes cross, or a label over a stamp or another label.
  - send anything because of the page: opening it, and tapping rows, cause no decision, advance, dry run or
    presentation write (the lesson's own keep aside).
  - put a place's name only on hover: every label drawn is text on the sheet or in the list.

  Rejected, and why:
  - A map feature to switch the page on: the first revealed place already says it ("the screen arrives when it means
    something"), as the journal's first entry does the book's.
  - Pixel or fractional positions: the content would be laying out art; a coarse cell keeps placement in code and
    makes "two places on one spot" a check.
  - The revealed places on the View: each reveal is already in view.unlocked.
  - The variant chosen in content: the pool and the no-repeat rule are the page's (decisions.md, "A world map").
  - Filler terrain between places drawn by code: geography the content has not written; the sheet shows only what
    is found.
  - Any unlock kind as a reveal: offers, answers and joins are added by triggers the loader makes or by other
    outcome parts, so "something reveals it" could not be checked from the content alone; an outcome there unlocks a
    feature instead.

  Text keys (Content.text_entries): ui.book.map                  the Map page's button in the Book's row
                                    ui.map.heading               the list of places' heading
                                    ui.lesson.map_open | ui.lesson.map_places   the map's lesson

  Acceptance (server tests under app/tests, client tests under app/client/test, as their neighbours):
  1. A fixture chapter with two places, one labelled and one not, loads: Content.places holds both in content order
     with their stamp, east, north, reveal and label_key (null for the unnamed one).
  2. Planted, each fails the load and names the place: no reveal; a stamp outside the set; a reveal of kind offer;
     a reveal of an unknown feature; a reveal of a feature nothing unlocks; two places on one cell in one chapter;
     two on one cell across two chapters; a place at [0, 0]; an at past the reach; a label_key not in text. Control:
     the fixture without the plant loads, and a reveal of a journal entry loads.
  3. The shipped content's places are served in content order (day nineteen's first sheet); openapi.json names
     PlaceInfo and the Stamp enum as components; the field-names test holds.
  4. With no place revealed the Book has no Map button and no request goes to /assets/art/map/; once one is revealed
     the button shows, last in the row, and the dot is on the Book's tab.
  5. Stamp choices are equal across two calls for one save; a place keeps its variant when another place of its kind
     is revealed; with no more places of a kind than its variants, no two share one; past that, none repeats before
     every variant has been used.
  6. Over a fixed set of sheets (a phone's and a desktop's size; neighbouring places, long labels, places at the
     reach), no two stamp boxes cross, no label box crosses a stamp box, another label or the sheet's edge, and each
     stamp lies inside the sheet; a label with no spot is left off the sheet while its place is still listed.
  7. The map's lesson starts on the Map button's first showing, walks its two steps, is kept in the save, and never
     returns once kept.
  8. Renders at 390x844 and 3840x2160 of a fixture with places revealed, looked at (headless-render), repeated with
     the browser set to prefer light (the game's one dark theme) and not differing; no name is reachable only by
     hover.
  9. The stamps made by tools/mapstamps.py are looked at, on the sheet, before they ship.
