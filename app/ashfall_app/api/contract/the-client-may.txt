THE CLIENT MAY
  - on load, ask GET /api/me: with a save, open the first (no front page); without, show the front page, whose
    Begin is POST /api/saves. Draw `was` and `history` at once, then `step` as a return (what arrived while away is
    revealed in turn). A Step whose rules_digest differs from the content the page holds means a deploy: fetch the
    content again and redraw.
  - keep the save a tab plays in its address as #game=ID: on load, open the save it names when it is in /me's saves,
    else the first. Switching opens another and writes its id there; New game is POST /api/saves, drawn as Begin
    draws a new game, and writes its id. A deleted save is never opened; a 404 on the open save starts again from
    GET /api/me.
  - on load, read a join link's fragment (#join=CODE) and remove it from the address bar before anything else runs
    (analytics included), then put the code in the join box: a link never joins by itself, the player's tap does.
    With a save on this browser, open it as usual with the join box shown over it. On confirm_merge, ask the player
    whether to move this browser's games or join without moving them, and send the same join again with merge true or
    keep true only on their tap. After a keep that answers `kept`, show its words (or, with none, say that the words
    D already had are what bring those games back) before reloading. After a
    join answers 200, reload and start again from GET /api/me.
  - reload itself when a Step's build, or /page-config.json's version asked every few minutes while idle, is not the
    build it booted with: at a quiet moment (no input for twenty seconds, no sheet open), behind a fade to its
    background, backing off a build that is still served after a reload. The game resumes from the server.
  - send one request at a time per save, and on 409 stale draw the Step it carries and carry on from it.
  - ask a request again, with backoff and the quiet "reconnecting" state, when it got no answer or a 5xx that the app
    did not answer (no X-Ashfall-Server: app: a gateway's, while a deploy or a pod restart leaves no ready app), without
    bound. A 5xx that carries X-Ashfall-Server: app and no ApiError body is the app's own failure and may repeat on every
    try: ask it again at most 3 times, then refuse it like any refused request, so the save's queue goes on.
  - on a Step with catching_up_to, ask advance again at once, keeping each slice's lines, and draw them as one Step
    once catching_up_to is null; hold the player's decisions and the forecast ask until then. On 409 catching_up,
    carry on catching up from its `step`, then send the decision again. While catching up, say so plainly where the
    reconnecting state shows, with how far through the time away it is if shown at all (view.at against
    catching_up_to).
  - sample any Segment at its clock: value = start_value + rate*(t-start), clamped at limit; divide by
    Scales.quantity_per_item to show items. A store's Segment is exact at its start; a card's per-completion amounts
    move a store by the card's work, a slope the integer rate rounds down, so between events the sample may trail the
    engine by under one unit per second, and it is exact again at next_event_at. Bars: Card.bar against bar_total;
    skills: SkillBar.xp.
  - show what the view says is there: only unlocked stores, skills, cards, chips, and the lanes in view.lanes (free
    ones too, labelled by LaneInfo). A chip whose ChipInfo has an offered_after is drawn only once view.unlocked
    holds it (its cure is on screen). A chip with a cost_key draws that text with its word, wherever it is drawn.
    A chip whose ChipInfo.chore is true and that no OrderCard names carries the hint dot while it is drawn and no
    rule is set on its store and test: no card is offered for it, and its rule is the player's to write.
    Just above the art, one line: the aim, the text of the last of Content.aim_infos whose `after` view.unlocked
    holds (the first has none, so there is always one); no line once that aim's `ends_after` (when set) holds too: its
    step is done, and the next step is the wait line's or a goal's. Content.line_infos lists every key a log line can carry
    (any LogLine.key, and end_line_keys), each once, so the page can keep the strip's room for the tallest line of a
    kind the strip shows (story, tap, body) before any arrives.
  - draw one card panel from view.cards and a Completed tab from view.completed; a card moves between them only
    when the view says so. Each card is drawn the same whether tapped, mixed or idle: its completions against its
    cap ("3 of 5"; no cap, the count alone), its upside_key (also under its name in the town editor), its share and run
    in the running mix, and its one bar: Card.bar sampled
    and taken modulo bar_total, whoever runs it (Card.by: tap, mix, or null and still). Card.completes_at is a tap's
    landing, drawn as its countdown; a share's is null: a rule that takes the hands yields the mix, so no landing is
    claimed for it.
    A card is ready iff blocker is null, else show blocker.ready_at as a countdown (null: no countdown), except while
    an at_most order on the blocker's store is on (view.orders_on) and its release + 1 is below the blocker's value:
    that order lets go first, so the time cannot be kept and no countdown shows. It can be tapped iff it is ready and
    its lane's LaneBar has no running_card; its time is Card.tap_s. A need on a store the view has not unlocked is
    drawn by what the store is: a tally (StoreInfo.tally), as ui.card.needs like any store; a clock with a part of
    the day (StoreInfo.clock) as when it opens, ui.card.opens with {when} from ui.when.* (below), until its sample
    reaches the quantity; any other (a countdown the player never sees) not at all, so the card's countdown is all of
    it. A blocker on a store the view has not unlocked never takes When ready.
    On a Step whose failed_tap names a card, pulse that card red and mark what its blocker names, and show no text for it: the need of its
    store (its have/need or when it opens; for a countdown the player never sees, the countdown), skill level or step;
    a blocker with nothing of its own on the card (a time) marks the countdown, and with no countdown either, the card
    alone. Every require that can refuse a tap is one the card draws (tests/opening/test_requires_shown.py).
  - draw a time for a skill's next level, a store's "full in" or its "empty in" only from the forecast it holds
    (FORECAST below), never from SkillBar.next_level_at, LedgerLine.fills_at or empties_at, which hold only until the
    next event.
  - draw a card in view.completed as retired, not done, when view.unlocked holds it as a `retired` unlock (a newer
    card replaced it); in the mix editor it is struck through until removed.
  - list a card's Card.needs on it, ready or not, each as ui.card.needs (what one completion takes, the same while
    its bar runs; for a card paused dry, also the store it ran out of at the level it resumes at, which a tap waits
    for), and draw one short (the ledger's red) while the card is unready (blocker not null) and its
    segment's sample is below its quantity. A need whose Need.gate is set draws the gate in its place, never naming
    its store, whether or not the view has unlocked the store: a skill_level gate as ui.card.needs_skill and a
    card_count one as ui.card.needs_step, read as below. The server sets it while the need is short, nothing makes the
    store, and a trigger that grants it has not fired, to the first such trigger's condition a need row can show. A
    need whose Need.spent is set (short, no card or standing in play can make the store again, every trigger granting
    it fired) draws as ui.card.needs_spent instead of ui.card.needs. A tap
    of a short need's row lights its Need.makers (the cards in view.cards and standing actions in view.standing that
    make it: their cards and the ledger lines they feed) until the next press, adds no words and does not tap the card.
    List each of CardInfo.skill_needs too, as ui.card.needs_skill, short while that
    skill's SkillBar.level is below its level (a skill not in view.skills reads as level one), and each of
    CardInfo.step_needs as ui.card.needs_step, short while that card's completions (view.cards or view.completed;
    absent, none) are below its count.
    A card with CardInfo.reach also draws, even while it works, its store's amount (the ledger line in view.ledger, sampled)
    against the first reach stop above it as ui.card.reach, and none once every stop is passed: a rising line joins the
    ledger only once full, so the card is where the player reads how far the next reach is.
  - show on each skill row its next level and what it pays, from SkillInfo.levels (the first entry above
    SkillBar.level); a pays_key is words, "?" for a story unlock. Its {pct} slot (and {pct_b}) is LevelPays.change_bp.
  - when a skill's shown level (SkillPrestige.level_counted, else SkillBar.level) rises from one view to the next,
    show ui.card.levelled for 2 s on each card in view.cards that a level passed names in LevelPays.speeds and whose
    Card.speed rose: {skill} the skill's label, {n} the new level, {before} and {after} one whole completion's time

  - draw the Camp bar from the ledger line of Content.camp.store, which is never drawn as a ledger line: its
    level is how many of camp.camp_levels' `at` the line's sample has reached, its next marker the next level's
    pays_key (its {from} and {to} slots filled from pays_from and pays_to, {pct} from change_bp) in ui.camp.pays, shown only once

    It shows once view.unlocked holds that store and its first level is reached, and is not tappable.
  - fill a text entry's slots, and only that, and format numbers by the one rule below (Text and numbers).
  - offer to add to a mix any card in view.cards whose CardInfo.lane is "hands"; show a mix by its id (its name).
    A mix in view.greyed_mix_ids (it holds a completed or retired card) is drawn grey and offers only edit
    (save_mix without those cards) and delete_mix.
    A retired card's CardInfo.replaced_by, when set, is the card that takes its place: a lesson can say to add it.
  - draw the hands from view.running_mix_id, the mix that runs now. view.active_mix_id is the player's own mix; while
    view.mix_order is not null, orders[mix_order]'s mix runs over it, and the player's own comes back once that

  - say why a rule is off from view.order_states[i], never from its own reading of the stores:
    waiting (its condition does not hold), outranked (its condition holds and an earlier rule has the hands or the
