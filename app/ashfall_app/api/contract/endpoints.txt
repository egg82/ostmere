  Decisions (discriminated by `kind`): tap_card (a tap, a craft, a story step), switch_mix, save_mix, delete_mix,
  set_orders, set_queue, answer_offer (OFFERS below), prestige, undo_pick and move_pick (PRESTIGE PHASES). Orders and queue
  entries take switch_mix, tap_card and answer_offer. A `running` subject is taken in exactly one shape: an order (in
  `when` or `unless`) whose every action is a tap_card of a card on one lane other than the hands, watching a card
  of a different lane (the hands' or another person's). Its verb cannot start or stop the card it watches, and its tap
  runs on when it lets go, so the instant settles. Every other `running` condition is 422 invalid_decision: a queue
  entry's, a mix switch's or an answer's, a hands tap's, a rule tapping on two lanes, a watched card on the rule's own
  lane, or a standing action watched. Why: two mix orders on a running card flip each other forever within one instant
  (engine DESIGN.txt, "Order mix"). A view carries `running` subjects, in blockers, rules and saves.
  Share levels: at_least and at_most carry value_of_cap and release_of_cap (bool, false when left out, always
  served). True says that level is basis points, 0..10000, of the watched store's cap at the moment the condition is
  read, not a quantity, so the rule keeps up as the cap grows (engine DESIGN.txt, "SHARE LEVELS": the store's whole
  items are compared with the exact share of the cap's whole items, and a band of one item is kept). Only an order's
  `when` and a queue entry's `until` on a store_value hold one; set_orders or set_queue with a share level on any
  other subject, outside 0..10000, or with two share levels on the wrong side of each other is 422 invalid_decision.
  A share level and a quantity are never compared for which side the release is on. Content's conditions (chips,
  goals, blockers, order cards, goal rows) are always quantities.
  Rule guards and fallbacks: an Order is {when, then, unless, or_else}; `unless` (conditions, at most
  3) and `or_else` (order verbs, at most 2) may be left out of a request and read as empty, and are
  always served. The rule's actions are `then` followed by `or_else`: it runs the first that can act. Each `unless` entry
  is the condition that blocks the rule, read as a `when` is (the same refusals, share levels included): while any holds, the rule is paused, and it resumes when the
  last lets go. All actions are one kind (tap_card or switch_mix), taps on one lane, mixes of one layer; a rule that
  answers an offer takes no or_else; a guard or an action listed twice is refused. Every refusal is 422
  invalid_decision, a list past its limit included. A rule with neither list is the rule it always was,
  in a request, in a save and in a rule_run's `order`, which equals its rule in View.orders.
  View carries, beside orders_on (still the `when` latch alone): order_states[i] may be `paused`;
  order_guards[i][j] {on, lets_go} for guard j of orders[i], `on` its latch and `lets_go` the quantity of the watched
  store it lets go at as the engine reads it now (a share level read against the cap now), null off a store;
  order_doing[i], the index in the rule's actions (then, then or_else) of the one it runs now, else null.
  Next's row: an Order may carry below_next (false when left out, always served). The rules with it are the ones under
  Next, the queue's row in the hands' list; set_orders makes every rule after the first with it carry it too, so the
  rules under Next are a run to the end and a rule appended lands there. A rule's latch is kept when only below_next
  changes, and a rule_run's `order` never carries it. Rules above Next outrank a ready queue entry as every rule does
  with none set (Next at the bottom, as in every save from before it); rules under it wait while the controlling
  entry can act or its goal stint runs, and act again when it pops. An entry that cannot act holds nothing, so the
  rules under Next run, while the entries behind it in the queue still wait. order_states[i] is `behind_next` for a
  hands rule under Next whose condition holds while the queue has the hands (engine DESIGN.txt, "NEXT'S ROW").
  SaveInfo {save_id, number, decisions, game_time, ended_at, started_at, played_at, camp_level}: game_time is the
  save's game time when last written; the rest are in GAMES ON ONE ACCOUNT. A save id is the account's own: any other
  answers 404 not_found, exactly as a save that does not exist, and so does a deleted save.
  AccountPresentation {rules_opened: bool, devices_opened: bool, reminder_mentioned: bool, ledger_lines: [str], mix_stopped: [str], switch_offered: [OfferedSwitch {chip, mix_id}], journal_read: [str]}, all false
  and empty on a new account (devices_opened: the "Another device" sheet has been opened; reminder_mentioned: the one

  SavePresentation {rule_seen: bool, mix_offered: [str], lines_touched: [str], lane_tasks_seen: [str], lessons: [str], lesson_ends: [LessonEnd {lesson, skipped, step, longest_wait_s}], visits: [Visit {left, back}], chapter: Chapter | null, newest_day_read: int | null},

  ended on, the longest the player stayed on one step in seconds), logged once as a lesson.end line with the save's id,
  when a PUT first adds it to what the save keeps; visits: each return after at least half an hour away, the game
  second the player last saw the save (`left`, one entry per value) and the one the return came to (`back`): the

  found against what the player left: each store whose whole items moved (quantities), each skill that rose and the
  Camp's level, each rule that came on (times: how often, 1 where the page could not count) or only let go while away;

  newest_day_read: JOURNAL, "The written days"). A PUT from a page of the build before, which carries no visits or
  chapter, leaves them as stored; a chapter replaces the stored one only when its `left` is later, and never past the
  byte limit below.
  Content.line_infos names each log line's kind, authored in the content's `[line_kinds]` beside its text and

  Beat lines: LogLine.opens_beat is true on a story beat's first line (a beat of content/beats.yaml, weekly and season

  only; the page never takes a beat from a key or a line's words. The end's first line is Content.end_line_keys[0].
  AccountSettings {lessons: bool}, true on a new account: lessons is false once the player turns lessons off in every
  game of the account; the page then starts no lesson and shows no hint that only a lesson gives (a guided offer is its

  Account {presentation: AccountPresentation, settings: AccountSettings, has_words: bool, words_due: bool}: has_words,
  the account has recovery words; words_due, it has none, holds a decision on a save, and was made at least 3 days ago
  (the page marks the way to make them with a dot).
  A presentation only grows, so a PUT is merged into what the account (or the save) keeps, in one write under its row lock:
  a flag is set if either has it, a growing number (newest_day_read) is the higher of the two, a list is the kept
  entries then the sent ones not yet there (a lesson_end is the same entry as another naming the same lesson; the kept

  whose copy is older than what is kept (a second tab or device) so takes nothing away, and takes the answer as its
  new copy. The lesson id in a lesson.end line is the page's own id for a lesson, logged as sent like a card id.
