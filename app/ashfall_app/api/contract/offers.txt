OFFERS
  Someone asks the player something, with two or more answers; it waits until answered and never expires (engine
  design/townsfolk.txt, "Offers"). Content.offer_infos: [OfferInfo {offer, text_key, kind, line_keys, answers: [AnswerInfo {answer,
  label_key, cost_key, change_bp, gains: [AnswerGain {store, quantity}], role}], askers, cause, waiting_key, expiry_key,
  permanence_key}] in content order, kind the group of offers that ask the same question and role what
  an answer means in it (both null on an offer outside any kind; a kinded offer has a role on every answer, and every
  offer of a kind has the same roles), line_keys the lines written when the offer shows. Content.offer_kinds:
  [OfferKindInfo {kind, roles: [KindRole {role, label_key}]}], one per kind in content order, a label for each role
  written for whoever asks: the words of the rule that answers the kind (the content refuses a kind without one for
  each of its roles). askers: the ruled lanes whose
  people ask (empty when no one person does). cause: what led to the offer, a LineCause (Line causes): its gate;
  null for a gate the page cannot word (Line causes: a store never shown, a lane's rules), and an earlier answer the
  offer requires is never its cause. The offer's lines carry the same cause. The three *_key fields are text the content
  writes only where this offer differs from the usual, null otherwise: waiting_key, what waiting costs (usually
  nothing: an open offer pauses only itself); expiry_key, when it goes (usually never; the content refuses one while
  the engine has no offer expiry); permanence_key, what the answer settles for good, for a choice to weigh. View: offers [Offer
  {offer, answer, answers: [OfferAnswer {answer, card, takes_s, shifts}]}], every offer shown so far, in content order:
  answer null while it is open, else the answer given; per answer, the card it sets running (null for none) and
  takes_s, the seconds one completion of that card would take started now (null without a card), and shifts: one entry
  per AnswerInfo.change_bp, in its order, AnswerShift {now_bp, after_bp}, the total change on that change's target now
  and once the answer is given (signed basis points, so +15% is 1500); null where the entries of one change aim at
  targets standing differently; empty once answered. The page fills a cost text's {pct} and {pct_b} with "now → after"
  (ui.ask.shift {before}{after}) where a shift is given, else with the change itself, and computes no total.
  AnswerInfo.change_bp is the answer's modifiers as signed changes in basis points, each distinct value once in
  authored order, as LevelPays.change_bp; AnswerInfo.gains the stores its outcome puts in, one entry per authored
  store line in authored order, quantity in quantity units. Both are empty for an answer without any.
  Unlock categories gain offer (shown), answered (answered, by any answer; its id is the offer's) and answer (its id
  is the answer given); gates read them as any unlock.
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/decisions with answer_offer {offer, answer}: advance to now, then answer. The answer's
  lines are in Step.lines at once; what it starts or sets is in the view. 422 invalid_decision, nothing written, when
  the answer is not one of the offer's, the offer is not shown, or it is already answered (a rule may have answered it
  since the page's last view: the page then asks advance and draws that). An order or queue entry may hold
  answer_offer: an order answers at the first instant its `when` holds while the offer is open, a queue entry when it
  is the controlling entry while the offer is open (it is gone once the offer is answered), so an offer can arrive
  already answered on a return; set_orders or set_queue with an answer not of its offer is 422.
  An order may also hold answer_kind {offer_kind, role} (never a decision, never a queue entry's: both 422): at each
  instant its `when` holds it answers the first open offer of the kind with that offer's answer of the role, so one
  rule answers every offer of the kind as each shows. It is never passed over. set_orders with a kind no offer
  declares, or a role the kind does not have, is 422 invalid_decision and writes nothing. The wire's field is
  offer_kind because `kind` is the verb's tag.
  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - draw the open offers (view.offers entries whose answer is null) as one Asks list: the first open one in content
    order shown at a fixed height with a count of the rest, which opens the whole list. An offer is revealed as an
    arrival: after the last of the Step's lines whose key is in its OfferInfo.line_keys, or at once when this Step
    holds none of them (it showed before the page's last view). An ask holds its askers' lane labels,
    OfferInfo.text_key, its cause worded as a line's, the *_key texts it has, and one control per answer: its label_key
    and its cost_key with {d} filled from takes_s (a cost text with {d} left out while takes_s is null; one without it,
    the effect of an answer that starts nothing and installs a modifier, drawn as it stands), {pct} and {pct_b} from
    AnswerInfo.change_bp and {gain} and {gain_b} from AnswerInfo.gains. An answer with change_bp also shows each figure
    it moves, before → after, read from a dry run of that answer_offer against the view (a store's net rate or cap, a
    card's time), never computed by the page. A tap sends answer_offer. An ask has no dismiss and stays until
    answered.
  - draw no row for an offer that arrives already answered: its lines say what happened.
  - list the answer order in the Rules tab: every always rule (after_time 0) that answers an offer, in list order, under
    ui.rule.answers, each as ui.rule.answer with no arrow.
  - build one answer rule per offer kind, in the Rules tab's answers section, once an offer of that kind has been
    answered in the view (an open offer is not enough) and while one of the kind is still to answer (an offer of
    Content.offer_infos the view does not hold answered, open or not yet shown): a row with that offer's question (set
    apart from an open offer's), ui.rule.answered {answer} with the label of the answer that offer was given, the line
    ui.rule.answer_next (the buttons answer the kind's later offers), and one button per role,
    each worded by the kind's own label for the role (Content.offer_kinds). A tap sets the kind's rule to that role
    (an always rule, appended; a rule already set for the kind is changed in place), and Remove takes it away. The
    rule reads as ui.rule.answer with the kind's label for the role; an answer_offer rule reads with that answer's own
    label, which was written for that one ask.
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT build an answer_offer rule (no editor offers one; the order is set by a story moment or a tool),
  nor offer the answer rule for a kind before an offer of it has been answered, or once every offer of it has been (the
  rule would wait for an ask that never comes).

  New text keys (Content.text_entries): ui.lane.idle | ui.lane.change   a lane row's free state and its Change
                                    ui.rule.always {card}   a lane's always rule
                                    ui.rule.answer {answer} | ui.rule.answers | ui.rule.answer_next   a rule or queue entry that answers an
                                    offer (the answer's label), and the Rules tab's heading over such rules
                                    ui.rule.answered {answer}   the answer rule builder's word of the answer given
                                    ui.queue.running   a Next entry whose tap a lane already runs
                                    ui.queue.move   a Next entry's ≡, for screen readers: it drags, or moves by the arrow keys
