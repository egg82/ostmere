LEAVES
  A person may leave the town at a story moment (engine DESIGN.txt, "LEAVES"), for good. Unlock categories gain left
  (id a lane): that lane's person has left. The people present (THE TOWN'S MIX) are the ruled lanes whose joined
  unlock view.unlocked holds and whose left unlock it does not.
  At that moment, in the same Step: the lane is gone from view.lanes; its cards are in neither view.cards nor
  view.completed; their shares are gone from every mix in view.mixes (no mix greys, and nobody else's
  shares change); the rules that tapped its cards are gone from view.orders, each kept in rule_runs with ended left
  (RULE RUNS); the standing actions the moment stops are gone from view.standing. A task the person had under way
  ends unfinished and pays nothing. The moment's lines say what happened. Nothing on Content changes but the unlock
  category; no decision changes, and a decision naming a card of a lane that has left is refused as for a retired
  card (tap_card from the player: 422).
  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - draw the town strip, the Rules tab's lanes and the scene without the person from the Step that holds the left
    unlock, after the moment's lines have been revealed.
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT:
  - keep a row, a rule or an idle remainder for a person who has left, or draw their cards in the Completed tab.
