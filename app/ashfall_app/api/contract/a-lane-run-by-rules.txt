A LANE RUN BY RULES
  A townsperson who works is a lane whose LaneInfo.ruled is true: its task is set by the player's rules, never by
  their tap (engine design/townsfolk.txt, "Ruled lane"). Content: LaneInfo {lane, label_key, ruled, later_labels: [LaneLabel
  {after: Unlock, label_key}], name_key}, name_key the person's name or null (Text and numbers, {<lane>}); the lane's label is the label_key of the last of later_labels whose `after`
  view.unlocked holds, else label_key (for every lane, ruled or not). View: LaneBar {lane, running_card,
  completes_at, order}, `order` the index into view.orders of the rule whose task the lane runs now: the first i
  with view.orders_on[i] true, view.order_states[i] not passed_over, and a tap_card of running_card; null while the
  lane is free or no rule taps what it runs. completes_at is when the running tap ends; null while the lane is free
  and while it repeats its task (a rule that would start the same card again at each completion: engine design/repeating-taps.txt,
  "REPEATING TAPS"), when the running card's Card.bar has no limit and is sampled modulo bar_total as a mix bar is.
  - tap_card from the player of an uncapped card whose CardInfo.lane is a ruled lane: 422 invalid_decision, nothing
    written. Rules may tap it; a queue entry may not (set_queue with one is 422: the queue is the hands' plan). A
    capped card there (a one-off) the player taps as on the hands: one completion, the lane's LaneBar holding it
    (order null, completes_at its landing) and the person's shares yielded until it lands, no rule made; refused
    while the lane is busy, as any tap.
  - The lane's always rule: an order {when: after_time {time: 0}, then: tap_card c}, c a card on that lane. It is on
    from the moment it is set and never lets go. Rules on a lane act first match: at each of the lane's landings the
    first rule that is on and whose card is ready starts it, so a rule below the always rule acts only while the
    always rule's card cannot.
  - A story moment may set a rule (an installed rule, engine design/townsfolk.txt, "Installed rule"), and only one shape: an
    always rule on a ruled lane, which the page draws and edits as below. It arrives in view.orders at the top or
    the end, like a rule the player set, and is theirs to edit, move or remove; once removed that moment never sets
    it again. rule_runs lists it from the moment it arrives, on at once (also when it arrives at a load that carried
    the save to new content). A rule whose one-off task has completed stays in view.orders as passed_over until the
    player removes it.
  - An answer may also put a one-off of the hands at the front of view.queue, as the page's own When ready entry
    (tap_card c until c's card_count reaches its next completion): drawn as Next like any entry, removable the same
    way, gone once c completes.
  - A tap_card entry is one run of its card: tap_card c until c's card_count reaches one past the runs planned before
    it (c's completions, plus 1 while a lane's running_card is c, or the until of c's last entry in view.queue if
    higher), so an entry for a card a lane runs now adds a run after that tap. More runs are more entries. An entry is
    checked when its turn comes: one whose card cannot run then waits (engine design/cards.txt, "Never ready"), and one whose
    card reached its cap leaves view.queue at that instant. Each entry's runs are its until less the runs planned
    before it, the tap under way included.
  - In Next, a tap_card entry that adds no run after the tap a lane runs (its card some view.lanes entry's
    running_card, its until at most that card's completions + 1) is drawn with ui.queue.running in place of Remove: it
    stays in view.queue until that tap completes, and set_queue without it would not stop the work. Each other row of
    one card is worded ui.queue.once {card}, or ui.queue.runs {card}{n} for n runs: entries of one card one after
    another are one row, whose Remove (ui.queue.remove_one when the row holds more than one) sends the queue without
    its last entry, and whose ≡ (aria ui.queue.move_one) moves that one entry before the row above or after the row
    below.
  - The other Next entries may be reordered: one set_queue with the same entries in the new order, the entries a lane
    runs at their own indices; each card's tap_card untils are given back to its entries in the new order, least
    first, and a Remove gives the card's entries left its untils less the highest, so the runs left still count from
    the tap under way.
  THE CLIENT MAY, for each lane in view.lanes whose LaneInfo.ruled is true:
  - draw it as a row under the hands strip, the hands strip's shape: its label, what it does (the label of
    running_card, or ui.lane.idle while free), the running card's bar (its Card in view.cards, sampled as any bar),
    and ui.lane.change, which opens the person's page: their cards in view.cards on that lane drawn as the card panel
    draws a card, the rules that tap them (each opening the rule editor), and, once they have joined the town, their
    part of the town's mix as a share bar, a tap on an uncapped card joining it to the mix or taking it out and a tap on a
    capped one sending tap_card for its next completion. While a one-off holds the lane (its LaneBar's
    running_card a capped card, whoever started it), the share bar, on the page and on the person's Town row, is that
    card alone with its time left and ui.hands.then {mix} naming the split they go back to, as the hands strip draws a
    tap; the card on the page counts down and fills its bar as the card panel's does. The rule editor's simple view scoped to a lane picks the task: a card in
    view.cards on that lane. Saving sends set_orders with that rule's tap_card changed in place (or a new always rule
    appended).
  - on the page of a lane whose LaneInfo.station is true (a thing, not a person: it never joins), send set_orders with
    the lane's first always rule's tap_card changed to a task the player taps, or a new always rule appended when it
    has none; draw the task that rule taps as pressed.
  - never draw a card on a ruled lane in the card panel or the Completed tab; such cards are offered only as that
    lane's tasks.
  - in the Rules tab, list the rules that tap a card on that lane under the lane's label, in their view.orders order;
    word an always rule as ui.rule.always {card}. A new rule on that lane (from the editor or an order card's
    Accept) goes immediately before the lane's first always rule, or at the end when it has none, so it can act.
  - say a lane rule is outranked with ui.rule.outranked, {rule} the words of orders[LaneBar.order]'s condition, or,
    when that rule is an always rule, its ui.rule.always words.
