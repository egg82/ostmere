THE TOWN'S MIX
  Once people join, the townsfolk's lanes are run by one mix whose shares are people's time: each share is a part of
  one person's time, the person being the lane its card is on (engine design/towns-mix.txt, "THE TOWN'S MIX"). It is an
  ordinary saved mix in view.mixes; which one runs is set by a rule, never by the player's switch.
  Content: game_mix_ids, the mix ids the content's joins make: the game's own mixes, worded by the town's name and
  changed on a person's page; every other saved mix is the player's. limits.reserved_mix_prefix ("town:"): a new
  mix id beginning so is the game's. Nothing new on LaneInfo or CardInfo. Unlock categories gain joined (id a lane):
  that lane's person has joined. The people present are the lanes in content order whose LaneInfo.ruled is true and whose joined unlock
  view.unlocked holds, and whose left unlock it does not (LEAVES).
  View: running_town_mix_id (the town mix running now: the first rule that is on with a switch_mix of a town mix that
  isn't greyed; null when none runs) and town_mix_order (the index into orders of that rule; null when none).
  A mix is a town mix when every card it holds, nested mixes flattened, has a CardInfo.lane that is ruled; a hands
  mix when every card is on hands; a mix with no card is a town mix when a join makes it or it was a town mix before it emptied, else a hands mix. running_mix_id and mix_order read only hands
  mixes, as before. A card of the running town mix has its Card.share_bp and Card.run as a hands mix card has, and
  Card.by is mix, or yielded while a rule's tap on its lane runs (its LaneBar.running_card is then that tap's card;
  while the lane runs only shares, running_card is null and completes_at null). A share of a card whose lane has not
  joined, or that is locked, idles.
  Decisions:
  - save_mix of a town mix: 422 invalid_decision, nothing written, when it holds cards of both kinds of mix, a card
    whose lane has not joined, a card with a cap, or one person's shares summing above 10000; for a new mix id
    beginning "town:" (those are the game's; the one it made may be edited); and, for any mix, under the id of a saved
    mix of the other kind (a hands mix's cards under a town mix's id, or the reverse).
  - switch_mix from the player of a town mix: 422 (set by rules). set_queue with a switch_mix of a town mix: 422 (the
    queue is the hands' plan). set_orders accepts one: that is the town's rule.
  - The town's always rule: an order {when: after_time {time: 0}, then: switch_mix m}, m a town mix. When the first
    person joins, the game makes the town's mix and appends this rule (rule_runs lists it, on at once), and each
    joining person's current always task becomes their whole share in it (their always rules on uncapped cards leave
    view.orders; their other rules stay where they were and still act, as taps that take the whole of that person
    while they run). The rule and the mix are the player's to edit from then on.
  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - draw the town as one strip under the hands strip, in place of the joined lanes' rows (a ruled lane not joined
    keeps its row, A LANE RUN BY RULES): one row per person present, their lane's label, and per card of
    running_town_mix_id on that lane its label, share and bar (sampled as any mix bar), with the person's idle
    remainder shown as idle; a person tapping by a rule shows that tap as their lane row does, shares yielded.
  - set a person's split on that person's page, on running_town_mix_id (else on the mix of the town's first always rule
    not passed_over, else on a new always rule's new mix): one split per person, each a part of 100%, a tap on a card in
    view.cards on that person's lane without a cap joining it to the split or taking it out, a capped one never held by
    the mix. Each change sends save_mix of that mix; a new mix sends save_mix then set_orders with the town's always
    rule pointed at it.
  - teach it once, the first time the strip shows, with a lesson on the strip and the person's page
    (decisions.md, "Lessons"), skippable and never repeated in a save, kept in the save as other lessons are.
  - draw no town rule in the Rules tab and offer no new one: the town's always rule runs the container and is listed
    nowhere. A greyed town mix is drawn as the hands' greyed mix is: the strip says it stopped and which card retired.
  - name the town everywhere by one name: its tab, its view's title, the Rules page's switch and heading, and a mix in
    Content.game_mix_ids. The name is the text of the label_key of the last of Content.town_later_labels
    ([LaneLabel], as a lane's later_labels) whose `after` view.unlocked holds, else ui.town.title.
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT:
  - list a town mix in the Mixes list or open it in the mix editor, offer switch_mix of one there, or put one in the
    queue. A name typed in the mix editor that begins limits.reserved_mix_prefix, or is another saved mix's, is
    refused where it is typed.
  - build an always rule (after_time 0, tap_card) on a joined lane: it would take that person whole for good.
  - add shares across people, move a share from one person to another, or show the split as anything but each
    person's own parts of 100% (the game never redistributes a share).

  Rejected, and why (the town's mix on the wire):
  - A town object on the view (people, shares, running state) beside view.mixes and view.cards: a second home for
    facts the mix, the cards and the unlocks already carry.
  - A `person` flag on LaneInfo: who has joined changes with the story, which is what the unlock set says.

  New text keys (Content.text_entries): ui.town.title   the strip's heading
                                    ui.town.idle {person}   a person's unshared remainder
                                    ui.mix.name_reserved {prefix} | ui.mix.name_taken   the mix editor's name refused
  The lesson's lines are content text keys like the other lessons'.
