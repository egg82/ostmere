PEOPLE (docs/decisions.md, "People, as built")
  How a person's joining and leaving is written: LaneInfo.told, line or mention for a ruled lane, null for any other
  lane. A line person's joining (a counted join aside: they came earlier) and leaving are told by the story lines of
  the outcome that moves them; each such LineInfo has person true. A mention person has no line of their own: each
  outcome that joins or leaves one writes a mention line, key "people.<way>.<lane>", text empty, kind story, its
  LineInfo's mention {lane, way: joined | left}. Mention lines are in the save's log like any line, at the second the
  person came or went.
  Content.people {came, went}: each a list of bands {at_least, keys}, rising from 1. The page words each game day's
  mention lines (day: the line's game second over 86400) as one line per way: the band of the highest at_least not over
  how many lanes came (or went) that day, and of its keys the one a shuffle bag keyed by the save, the way and the band
  draws for that day.
  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - draw a day's mention lines as that one line in the strip and the chronicle, at the second of the day's first,
    reworded when a later Step adds to the same day; and gather the person and mention lines of a return into the
    "Since you left" chapter's people section.
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT:
  - draw a mention line by itself, or name the person a mention counts.
