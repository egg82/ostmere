GAME API: the contract for the page. Models: models.py, saves_models.py, devices_models.py and error_models.py
(generate the client's types from openapi.json). Every path is under /api.

The server holds the game. An account is a browser (a session cookie); it owns saves; a save is one game, written by
the server on every decision and advance, so the page never holds the only copy and keeps nothing itself. The
server's clock decides game time: a save's game time is the wall seconds since it began (never before the time it was
last written at), so the time away runs whether or not a page is open.

Every answer that moves a game is a Step:
  save_id        the save it belongs to.
  decisions      how many decisions the save holds after this Step: send it as `after` with the next decision.
  rules_digest   the rules the save is now under; equal to Content.rules_digest.
  build          the server's build (/page-config.json's version).
  view           what to draw now (View), read off the engine at view.at, the server's game time for the save.
  lines          log lines the game wrote since the last time it was written, in (at, seq) order; (at, seq) is
                 unique within one Step, and a later Step's lines follow an earlier one's. Text: Content.text_entries.
  repeats        what the player keeps doing by hand, counted from the save's decision log (REPEATS below).
  rule_runs      what the rules did lately (RULE RUNS below).
  catching_up_to null, or the game time this request was advancing to when its time ran out short of it: view.at is
                 behind now; ask advance again (CATCHING UP below).
Every response, errors included, carries the header X-Ashfall-Build: the build that answered it. While a deploy
rolls, two builds answer at once. X-Ashfall-Server says which server answered (app, or web for the page's files).

ACCOUNTS AND SESSIONS
  The session is the cookie __Host-ashfall_session (Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=None, Partitioned, Path=/, Max-Age 400
  days). The page never reads it; the browser sends it. Partitioned keeps one cookie per top-level site, so the game
  embedded in another site's page has an account of its own there, apart from the one at this site; a browser that
  refuses cookies in a cross-site frame (Safari) keeps no session there at all. Writes are held to this site by the
  Origin check below, not by SameSite. It is renewed (cookie and last use) at most once a day, by a POST or PUT
  that succeeds (never by a GET, nor by a refused write), so a browser that plays at least once in 400 days keeps
  its account. A renewal that fails is logged (session.renew_failed) and never changes the answer to the write. An account keeps at most 20 sessions; making
  another drops the one unused longest. An account is made only by POST /api/saves from a browser with no live
  session; no GET makes or changes one. An account with no decision on any save, unused for 30 days, is deleted.
  Every POST and PUT under /api must come from this site: an https Origin naming the request's Host (the gateway and
  the tunnel keep the browser's), or, with no Origin, a Sec-Fetch-Site of same-origin or none; a write with neither
  header, or with any other, is refused (403 cross_site). A body, when there is one, must be application/json (415
  not_json), and at most 262144 bytes (413 too_large, refused before it is parsed, whether the length is declared or
  the body streamed). The client address the account limit counts is the connection's as uvicorn's --proxy-headers leaves it
  (X-Forwarded-For from a trusted proxy).

ENDPOINTS
  GET  /api/content                   Content. Static: text, labels, cards, Scales, limits. Fetch once per rules_digest. ETag names the build and the
                                      rules; If-None-Match with it is a 304.
  GET  /api/me                        Me {account: Account | null, saves: [SaveInfo]}: who this browser is, with its
                                      live saves, the most recently played first. Never makes an account.
  POST /api/saves                     NewSave {save: SaveInfo, presentation: SavePresentation, step: Step}: a new
                                      save at game time 0, its first view and lines, and nothing kept about its
                                      teaching. Makes the account (and sets the cookie) when the browser has none,
                                      under a limit (429 rate_limited). On an account, under the limits of "Games on
                                      one account" (409 too_many_saves, 429 rate_limited); the other saves are kept.
  GET  /api/saves                     Games: the account's saves, live and deleted (GAMES ON ONE ACCOUNT). 401
                                      no_account without an account.
  A {save_id} below is 1 to 2^53; anything else is 422 invalid_decision before any lookup. In range but not this
  account's is 404 not_found.
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/delete    Games: the save deleted (GAMES ON ONE ACCOUNT).
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/restore   Games: a deleted save live again (GAMES ON ONE ACCOUNT).
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/open      Opened {save, was, history, presentation, step}: the save as the player last saw
                                      it (`was`, the View at the time it was last written), its last 200 lines before
                                      this call (`history`, PastLine {n, at, key}, oldest first), what the page keeps
                                      about its teaching (`presentation`, SavePresentation below), and `step`, the save
                                      advanced to now with the lines that arrived while away. Writes like an advance.
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/advance   Step: a plain advance to now. Call it when the clock passes
                                      view.next_event_at, and on the page being shown again.
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/decisions DecisionRequest {decision, after} -> Step: advance to now, then apply the
                                      decision. `after` is the Step.decisions the page last saw (409 stale if the
                                      save has had another decision since). A tap_card of a card that is not ready
                                      is a failed tap: it takes no time, changes nothing and emits no line, and
                                      Step.failed_tap names the card (null on every other Step).
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/forecast  ForecastAnswer {decisions, forecast}: the save advanced to now and what lands
                                      from there if the player changes nothing (FORECAST below).
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/dry-run   DryRunRequest {decisions} -> View: what the save would show after these
                                      decisions, applied in order now. Nothing is written (the mix editor's "after").
  GET  /api/saves/{save_id}/view      View: the save as last written, at its own time (rebased in memory if the rules
                                      changed since), not advanced to now. Nothing is written: no played_at change, no
                                      decision counted. Never 409 catching_up. 404 for a save the account does not
                                      own; 503 save_unavailable for one this build cannot read. The front page's
                                      picture of each save's camp, before any game is opened.
  PUT  /api/me/presentation           AccountPresentation -> Account: what the page keeps about the player across
                                      saves (the Rules tab opened, the ledger lines shown). Adds to what is kept,
                                      never takes away (below); the answer's presentation is what is kept now. 401
                                      without an account.
  PUT  /api/me/settings               AccountSettings -> Account: the player's choices for every game of the account,
                                      replaced whole (below). 401 without an account.
  PUT  /api/saves/{save_id}/presentation  SavePresentation -> SavePresentation: what the page keeps about one save's
                                      teaching (below). Adds to what the save keeps, never takes away, exactly as
                                      the account's presentation does; the answer is what is kept now. 404 for a save
                                      the account does not own or has deleted.
  POST /api/feedback                  FeedbackRequest {text, save_id?} -> 204: a tester's words. `text` is trimmed and
                                      must then be 1 to 2000 characters (422 invalid_decision). `save_id` is 1 to 2^53
                                      (past that, 422 invalid_decision) and is kept only
                                      when this browser's account owns that save; with it the server also records the
                                      save's game time when last written and the key of its newest log line, beside the
                                      build and release that answered and the account id. No account is needed (the
                                      browser's client address is what is counted either way). At most 5 an hour
                                      from one client address, 5 from one account, 15 from one IPv6 /48 and 50 an hour
                                      in all, counted across pods; a limit that cannot be counted refuses (429
                                      rate_limited).
                                      Rows older than 180 days are deleted as new ones come in. The text is stored
                                      and never logged. The owner reads them with `python -m ashfall_app.feedback list`.
  POST /api/me/transfer-code          TransferCode: a code that joins another browser to this account (below,
                                      "Moving to another device"). 401 no_account without an account.
  POST /api/me/words                  RecoveryWords: new recovery words for this account. 401 no_account without
                                      an account.
  POST /api/join                      JoinRequest {typed, merge, keep} -> Joined {moved, account, kept}: this browser
                                      joins the account a code or recovery words name.
  GET /api/me/devices                 Devices: the account's browsers (below, "Your devices"). 401 no_account.
  DELETE /api/me/devices/{device_id}  Devices: signs that browser out. 401 no_account; 404 not_found.
  PUT /api/reminders                  ReminderOn -> Reminder: reminders on for this browser, or its keys refreshed
                                      (below, "Reminders"). 401 no_account; 404 not_found; 422 invalid_decision.
  DELETE /api/reminders               ReminderOff -> 204: reminders off for this browser. 401 no_account.
  Decisions (discriminated by `kind`): tap_card (a tap, a craft, a story step), switch_mix, save_mix, delete_mix,
  set_orders, set_queue, answer_offer (OFFERS below), prestige, undo_pick and move_pick (PRESTIGE PHASES). Orders and queue
  entries take switch_mix, tap_card and answer_offer. Their conditions take no `running`
  subject (the page never builds one): set_orders or set_queue with one is 422 invalid_decision. A view still
  carries `running` subjects, in blockers and in saves that hold them.
  SaveInfo {save_id, number, decisions, game_time, ended_at, started_at, played_at, camp_level}: game_time is the
  save's game time when last written; the rest are in GAMES ON ONE ACCOUNT. A save id is the account's own: any other
  answers 404 not_found, exactly as a save that does not exist, and so does a deleted save.
  AccountPresentation {rules_opened: bool, devices_opened: bool, reminder_mentioned: bool, ledger_lines: [str], mix_stopped: [str], switch_offered: [OfferedSwitch {chip, mix_id}], journal_read: [str]}, all false
  and empty on a new account (devices_opened: the "Another device" sheet has been opened; reminder_mentioned: the one
  row pointing at the reminders setting has been drawn, so it is never drawn again; ledger_lines: the stores whose
  ledger line the page has shown; mix_stopped: kept for old accounts, no longer written (the stopped mix's fix
  is gone); switch_offered: the repeated switches (a RepeatedSwitch's chip and mix_id) whose chip mark was touched;
  journal_read: the notes on journal pages the player has turned to). An account's stored presentation may still hold
  the lesson fields it kept before lessons were per save; they are not served, and a PUT from a page of the build
  before (carrying rule_seen, mix_offered, lessons or lesson_ends) is accepted with those fields dropped, never stored.
  SavePresentation {rule_seen: bool, mix_offered: [str], lessons: [str], lesson_ends: [LessonEnd {lesson, skipped, step, longest_wait_s}], visits: [Visit {left, back}], chapter: Chapter | null},
  all false and empty on a new save and on every save made before this field: what gates a lesson or a lesson's hint
  is kept per save, so a new game teaches everything again from each lesson's own moment (rule_seen: a rule has been
  seen on and then off in this save, which opens writing rules; mix_offered: the cards whose "your mix could take it"
  dot was touched; lessons: the lessons done or skipped; lesson_ends: how each ended (skipped or done, the step index it
  ended on, the longest the player stayed on one step in seconds), logged once as a lesson.end line with the save's id,
  when a PUT first adds it to what the save keeps; visits: each return after at least half an hour away, the game
  second the player last saw the save (`left`, one entry per value) and the one the return came to (`back`): the
  chronicle's stretches begin at each `left`; chapter: the newest visit's figures, Chapter {left, back, stores: [Figure
  {id, was, now}], levels: [Figure], camp: Figure | null, rules: [RuleChange {order, came_on, times}]}, what the return
  found against what the player left: each store whose whole items moved (quantities), each skill that rose and the
  Camp's level, each rule that came on (times: how often, 1 where the page could not count) or only let go while away;
  the page takes it once, as the visit is kept, and draws it as the chronicle's "Since you left" chapter). A PUT from a
  page of the build before, which carries no visits or chapter, leaves them as stored; a chapter replaces the stored one
  only when its `left` is later, and never past the byte limit below.
  Content.line_infos names each log line's kind, authored in the content's `[line_kinds]` beside its text and
  checked for every key a line can carry: story, tap, find, level, body or fight (opening/line_kinds.py). The page sets
  a line by its kind and never by its key or words.
  Line causes: each LineInfo also carries cause, what writes the line, read from where the content authors its key
  (api/line_causes.py): runner {runner}, a card's line, outcome or finish, or a fight's pool on a card or standing
  action; when {when: Condition}, what a beat, an offer's arrival or a chip waited on; skill {skill}, a line several
  levels of one skill share (a phase's pool), so which level is not known; camp {level}, a Camp level from 1; answer
  {offer}, the player's answer to that offer (an offer's own lines take the offer's cause: OFFERS). Null for the end's
  lines and a beat waiting on a lane's rules. A key is written in one place or by one skill's levels; any other
  sharing fails the content's load. The page words a cause under its line in the chronicle and never parses a line's
  words for one.
  AccountSettings {lessons: bool}, true on a new account: lessons is false once the player turns lessons off in every
  game of the account; the page then starts no lesson and shows no hint that only a lesson gives (a guided offer is its
  one tap), and on again, each save's lessons go on from what it keeps. A PUT replaces the settings whole.
  Account {presentation: AccountPresentation, settings: AccountSettings, has_words: bool, words_due: bool}: has_words,
  the account has recovery words; words_due, it has none, holds a decision on a save, and was made at least 3 days ago
  (the page marks the way to make them with a dot).
  A presentation only grows, so a PUT is merged into what the account (or the save) keeps, in one write under its row lock:
  a flag is set if either has it, a list is the kept entries then the sent ones not yet there (a lesson_end is the
  same entry as another naming the same lesson; the kept one stays), and a list stops growing at 512 entries, and every list at the byte limit (REQUEST LIMITS). A page
  whose copy is older than what is kept (a second tab or device) so takes nothing away, and takes the answer as its
  new copy. The lesson id in a lesson.end line is the page's own id for a lesson, logged as sent like a card id.

CATCHING UP
  A request advances a save for at most a few seconds of the server's time, so a long absence comes back in slices:
  open, advance and decisions each advance one slice, write it, and answer it. A Step whose
  catching_up_to is set stopped short (view.at is the last instant it resolved, before catching_up_to); its lines
  are that slice's, and the next advance's lines follow them, so the slices' lines in order are what one advance to
  now would have brought, and the save they end at is the same. A decision whose advance stops short is not applied:
  409 catching_up, with the slice written as an advance in `step`. 
  A forecast or a dry run that would stop short replays for the whole request budget, keeps none of it and writes
  nothing (holding no database connection while it replays): 409 catching_up, whose `step` is the save as last written
  (no lines); the page asks an advance, which is behind.

REPEATS
  Step.repeats {taps: [RepeatedTap {card, count}], switches: [RepeatedSwitch {chip, mix_id, count}]}: what the player
  keeps doing by hand, counted by the server from the save's decision log as of view.at; each entry is there only once
  its count reaches opening.toml's [repeat_offers], in the order its first counted decision was made.
    taps       a tap_card of a hands-lane card while the hands ran a mix (View.running_mix_id) that did not hold it,
               nested mixes flattened, counted per card over the last hand_taps_within_s game seconds; listed from
               hand_taps.
    switches   a switch_mix the player made while one or more chips showed (View.chips), unless the hands already ran
               that mix, counted once per (chip, mix_id) over the last switches_within_s game seconds; listed from
               switches.
  Each decision's context (the mix the hands ran, whether a tap's card was in it, the chips shown) is kept in the log
  as it is made, so the counts need no replay and a save rebuilt from its log counts the same. Decisions logged by a
  build before this field count for nothing. The page offers each repeat once (AccountPresentation.mix_offered,
  switch_offered); what it offers is the page's (docs/design/opening-screen.md, the hands strip).

RULE RUNS
  Step.rule_runs [RuleRun {order, starts, on_at, on_level, off_at, off_level, ended}]: what the rules did lately, one
  entry per rule the save has now (`order` equals one of View.orders) or the game ended (below), the most recent change
  first, at most 8. A rule is
  known by its condition and verb, since rules have no id: moving it keeps its entry; editing it makes another rule,
  whose entry starts the next time it comes on, and the old entry goes, as a removed rule's does.
    starts     the times it came on (its latch turned true) since its entry began.
    on_at      game time of its last start; on_level the amount of the store its condition reads then (quantity
               units), null for a condition on no store.
    off_at     game time of its last let-go, null until it has let go; off_level as on_level. A let-go before on_at
               belongs to an earlier run.
  Kept by the server across advances and decisions, so a rule that comes on and lets go while no page is open is there
  on the next Step. A set_orders keeps the on or off of every rule it moves or keeps, so a rule running inside its
  band goes on running; a new or edited rule starts from its `when` alone. Saves not written since this field start
  empty.
  Ended by the game: RuleRun gains ended, null, or season (a season's turn removed the rule: engine DESIGN.txt,
  "Retired chores") or left (its person left the town: LEAVES). An ended entry is kept after its rule leaves
  View.orders, with off_at the time it was removed and off_level as for a let-go, until 8 newer entries push it out;
  it matches no rule in View.orders. A rule that never came on before it was removed gets an entry too, with starts 0
  and on_at and on_level null (on_at is null only there). If the player sets an equal rule again, its next start
  begins a fresh entry, ended null. Entries the server kept before this field read ended null.
  THE CLIENT MAY word an ended entry in the Rules tab's Lately block as ui.rule.ended_season {rule} or
  ui.rule.ended_left {rule}{person} ({person} the name_key of the lane of the card the rule taps), and draw it in
  the order rule_runs gives. It never offers to edit an ended entry: the rule is gone.
  New text keys (Content.text_entries): ui.rule.ended_season {rule} | ui.rule.ended_left {rule}{person}   a rule the
                                    game removed, at a season's turn or when its person left
  Ended stint: a goal stint (GOAL STINTS) that left view.queue with its until holding gets an entry with ended "stint",
  order {when: the stint's until, then: its switch_mix}, on_at the instant it came to control (the first queue entry not
  dead, its mix running; null when its goal already held then), off_at the instant it left. starts is 1 (0 with no
  on_at); the levels are null. A stint the player removed before its goal held makes no entry. The entry is kept like
  an ended rule's (8, newest first) and matches no rule in View.orders. The server keeps a stint that has begun to run
  in the same record, off the wire until it ends.
  THE CLIENT MAY word an ended stint in Lately as a line ui.stint.entry {mix} {goal} (the mix of `then`, the goal of
  `when`, as Next words it) and under it the facts, joined by " · ": ui.stint.ran {d} (off_at - on_at, only with an
  on_at) and ui.stint.landed {ago} (frame time - off_at).
  New text keys (Content.text_entries): ui.stint.ran {d} | ui.stint.landed {ago}   an ended stint's facts.
  Made: RuleRun gains made [Amount {store, quantity}], [] on every entry but an ended stint's. On one, what the save
  gained while it ran: for each store that a card in the stint's mix (at a share above 0) makes, by its makes or its
  outcome's stores, both at the instant the stint began to run and at the instant it popped, that store's amount at
  the pop less its amount at the start, in quantity units. Net, not gross: what other work took from the store or its
  cap threw away meanwhile is not counted back, and work on other lanes in the window is counted in, so it is the
  save's gain in those stores, never a claim about what the mix alone did. Only gains of at least one whole item
  (QUANTITY_SCALE) are listed, the goal's store first (the store the stint's until reads, when it reads one), then the
  largest gains, ties in content store order, at most 2. An ended stint with no on_at has made []. Entries the server
  kept before this field read [].
  THE CLIENT MAY add the made fact last, after ui.stint.landed: ui.stint.made {n}{store} for one entry, and
  ui.stint.made_two {n}{store}{m}{store_b} for two, {n} and {m} in whole items rounded down, {store} and {store_b}
  the stores' labels as they stand in a sentence; nothing when made is [].
  New text keys (Content.text_entries): ui.stint.made {n}{store} | ui.stint.made_two {n}{store}{m}{store_b}   what
                                    an ended stint's save gained while it ran.

GAMES ON ONE ACCOUNT
  An account holds several saves, each its own game; the page plays one at a time and keeps the others.
  GET /api/saves -> Games {saves, deleted, max_saves, kept_days}
    saves          the live saves, the most recently played first (as GET /api/me).
    deleted        DeletedSave {save: SaveInfo, deleted_at, purge_at}: the deleted saves still held, the most recently
                   deleted first. purge_at is deleted_at + kept_days; from then on the save may be gone for good.
    max_saves      5: the most live saves an account holds. Deleted saves do not count. GET /api/me carries it too.
    kept_days      30: how long a deleted save is held. GET /api/me carries it too.
  SaveInfo's fields beyond the save's clock:
    number         the account's count of its games: 1 for the first begun, and for each later one the highest number
                   the account holds (deleted saves included) plus one. Null for a save begun by the build before
                   numbers, until it is next written.
    started_at     the wall time it was begun; played_at the wall time it was last written (ISO 8601, UTC).
    camp_level     how many Camp levels (Content.camp.camp_levels) the save had landed when last written; null for one
                   not written since the build before this field.
  POST /api/saves on an account makes another save and changes no other. 409 too_many_saves when the account already
    holds max_saves live saves; at most 10 a day (24 hours) per account beyond its first save (which the limit on
    new accounts counts), past which 429 rate_limited with Retry-After; a limit that cannot be counted refuses. Either refusal makes nothing.
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/delete -> Games. The save is deleted at once: gone from GET /api/me and Games.saves, held in
    Games.deleted, and every call on it (open, advance, decisions, forecast, dry-run, feedback's save_id) answers as
    for a save that does not exist until it is restored. 409 last_save when it is the account's only live save; 404
    not_found when the account has no live save of that id. A page that has it open learns on its next call.
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/restore -> Games. A held deleted save is live again, as it was; its clock never stopped, so
    opening it brings the time away since. 409 too_many_saves at max_saves; 404 not_found when the account holds no
    deleted save of that id (never deleted, or gone for good).
  A deleted save is deleted for good, with its lines, decision log and snapshots, only after purge_at, a small batch
  at a time by the next delete or new game on any account; never by the request that deleted it. Making, deleting and
  restoring saves of one account run one after another on any pod (each holds the account), so max_saves and the last
  live save hold under any race; a delete waits for a call already running on that save. A join moves an account with
  all its saves, deleted ones included, and a merge holds both accounts, so max_saves holds across it too.

MOVING TO ANOTHER DEVICE
  An account moves to another browser by a transfer code (or its QR code) made on a browser that has it, or by its
  recovery words, typed on the other browser (or the code's link opened there). Every answer of the three routes
  below, errors included, carries Cache-Control: no-store. Without a database, or without the server's join key
  (ASHFALL_JOIN_KEY: 32 bytes written as 64 hex characters), all three answer 503 save_unavailable and change nothing.
  POST /api/me/transfer-code -> TransferCode {transfer_code, expires_in_s, join_url, qr}
    transfer_code  eight characters of ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789, shown as two groups of four joined by a dash
                   ("ABCD-EFGH"). It joins one browser, once, within expires_in_s of being made.
    expires_in_s   600, as Content.transfer_code_s says. A code past it finds nothing, as if it had never been made.
    join_url       ASHFALL_PUBLIC_URL (default https://ostmere.yggdrasil.pub) + "/#join=" + transfer_code, never built
                   from the request's Host. The code rides in the fragment, which no request carries.
    qr             join_url as a QR code: one string per row of modules, top row first, each character "1" (dark) or
                   "0" (light), every row as long as there are rows (21 + 4k for some k >= 0), with no quiet zone
                   (the page draws one around it, dark on light).
    Making a code ends the account's previous one, used or not: an account has at most one live code. At most 10
    codes an hour per account (429 rate_limited).
  POST /api/me/words -> RecoveryWords {words, label}
    words          four words of the BIP-39 English word list, lowercase, joined by single dashes. The server keeps
                   only a slow hash and never answers them again. New words replace the old at once: from this answer
                   on the old words find nothing. Words never expire and are not used up by a join.
    label          "Ostmere": the name a browser's password manager files the words under.
    At most 5 sets of words a day per account (429 rate_limited).
  A browser already joined keeps its session: a new code or new words stop the old ones joining anything more, and
  sign no browser out. Ending a browser's session is "Your devices", below.
  POST /api/join  JoinRequest {typed, merge, keep} -> Joined {moved, account, kept}
    typed          at most 200 characters: a transfer code in any case, with or without spaces and dashes, or a whole
                   join_url; or recovery words in any case, separated by any run of spaces, dashes, dots, commas or
                   underscores. Space before and after is ignored.
    merge          false by default; true moves this browser's own games into the other account (below), after the
                   page has asked the player (confirm_merge).
    keep           false by default; true joins T and leaves this browser's own games where they are, on D (below),
                   after the same question. merge and keep together are 422 invalid_decision.
    With T the account `typed` names and D the account of this browser's live session, if it has one:
      - nothing named: no such code (never made, used, expired, or ended by a newer one), no such words (never made,
        or replaced), or `typed` is neither shape -> 404 no_such_code. Nothing changes.
      - no live session: the browser joins T: a new session of T and its cookie (T keeps its 20 sessions used most
        recently). moved 0.
      - D is T: nothing changes, except that a code is used up. moved 0.
      - D holds no decision on any save: D folds into T (below) and D's saves are deleted. moved 0.
      - D holds a decision on a save, and D's live saves and T's together pass max_saves -> 409 too_many_saves, with
        ApiError.merge, `merge` true or false. Nothing changes, and a code is not used up.
      - D holds a decision on a save, merge false -> 409 confirm_merge, with ApiError.merge. Nothing changes, and a
        code is not used up.
      - D holds a decision on a save, merge true: every save of D, deleted ones included, becomes T's, and D folds into
        T. A moved save keeps its id, clock and everything in it but takes a new number: on from the highest number
        T holds (deleted saves included), in D's own order of numbers (unnumbered first). moved: D's saves.
      - D holds a decision on a save, keep true: only the session that asked becomes T's (T keeps its 20 sessions used
        most recently). D, its saves, its other sessions and T's saves are as they were, so D's games are still there
        for any browser that holds a session of D, and for D's recovery words. D is not deleted, and a pruning of
        empty accounts never takes it, since it holds a decision. The cap does not apply, as nothing moves. moved 0.
        D's words: when D has none, the join makes them as POST /api/me/words would (this browser can no longer ask
        for them) and answers them once, in `kept.words`; when D has some, they are left alone and `kept.words` is
        null, since they cannot be shown again. `kept` is null for any other join, and for a keep from a browser
        whose D holds no decision (that D folds into T as above).
    ApiError.merge is a MergeSummary {here, there, there_newest_game_time, max_saves, words_replaced}: D's live saves,
    T's live saves, the game_time of T's most recently played save (null when T has none), Games.max_saves, and
    whether D and T both have recovery words (a merge then ends D's: the page says so in its question).
    D folding into T: every session of D becomes T's (this browser keeps its cookie, which now names T; T keeps its 20
    sessions used most recently), D's recovery words become T's when T has none (else they find nothing from then on),
    T's presentation is kept, and D is deleted, with its code. All of a join is one transaction: everything moves or
    nothing does. Only D's own saves move, D being the account of the session that asked; T's never move to D.
    Kept {saves, words, label}: D's live saves left on D, D's new recovery words or null, and RecoveryWords.label.
    `moved` is how many saves became T's. `account` is T's Account, as GET /api/me now answers it. A code is used up
    only by a 200, never by a refusal.
    Limits, each answered 429 rate_limited with Retry-After (seconds) and changing nothing: at most 10 joins per 15
    minutes from one client address, every join the limit lets through counting, right or wrong (an IPv6 address
    counts by its /64, and its /48 is held to 30 in the same 15 minutes); and at most 300 joins an hour, from every address together, whose code or words (of either
    shape) found nothing: past that, every join is refused until the hour moves on. A limit that cannot be counted
    refuses. Recovery words are slow to check: when the server has been unable to start on them for 3 seconds, 429
    with Retry-After 3.

YOUR DEVICES
  A device is a browser that holds a session of the account (one row per session; a join folds a browser's session
  into the account, so it lists there). Both routes answer Devices {devices: [Device]}, and every answer, errors
  included, carries Cache-Control: no-store. Neither renews a session. Without a database, 503 save_unavailable.
  Device {device_id, label, created_at, last_seen_at, this_device}
    device_id      the session's id, the {device_id} of DELETE. The session's token and its hash are never answered.
    label          browser and system read from the User-Agent when the session was made ("Firefox on Linux",
                   "Safari on iPhone"; "Chrome" or "Linux" alone when only one is told); "A browser" when neither
                   is, and for a session made before labels. The User-Agent itself is not kept.
    created_at     when the browser got its session.
    last_seen_at   when it was last used, stored at most once a day.
    this_device    true for the browser that asked; exactly one row of a list asked by a live session.
  GET /api/me/devices -> Devices: the account's sessions, this browser first, then the rest by last_seen_at, latest
    first. An account keeps at most 20 sessions, so at most 20 rows.
  DELETE /api/me/devices/{device_id} (device_id >= 1) -> Devices: the session ends at once, so that browser's next
    request has no account (GET /api/me answers account null, a route that needs one 401 no_account), and the list
    is what is left. Removing this_device signs the asking browser out too: the answer ends its session cookie
    (Set-Cookie with Max-Age 0) and lists the other rows, none of them this_device. A device_id that is not one of
    this account's sessions, another account's included, answers 404 not_found exactly as one that does not exist,
    and changes nothing. A write, so csrf.py's rules hold (403 cross_site, 415 not_json), and, like any DELETE, it does not
    renew the session. The page offers the removal on every row, this one included, and asks first for this one.

REMINDERS
  Off until the player turns them on. A browser with reminders on is one row, keyed by its Web Push endpoint, and gets
  one notification once it has been away the hours it picked, then none until it comes back. Both routes are writes
  (csrf.py's rules hold), carry the endpoint only in the body, and answer Cache-Control: no-store; without a database,
  503 save_unavailable.
  /page-config.json names `reminders`: {public_key, after_hours: [int]} when the server can send them, null when it
  cannot (the page says reminders are not available here). A page that finds no `reminders` key at all is on a build
  from before reminders and offers no setting.
  ReminderOn {endpoint, p256dh, auth, tz, window: ReminderWindow | null}
    endpoint, p256dh, auth  PushSubscription.toJSON()'s endpoint and keys. The endpoint must be https on a browser push
                   service (FCM, Mozilla, Apple, Windows), or 422.
    tz             the device's IANA time zone, or 422: the window's hours are local to it.
    window         ReminderWindow {after_hours, send_from_hour, send_until_hour}: after_hours one of page-config's
                   reminders.after_hours; the hours 0-23, from up to until, past midnight when from is later, any hour
                   when they are equal. Null refreshes a browser already on (its keys and zone) and answers its window;
                   404 not_found when it is not on, so the page shows the setting off.
  PUT /api/reminders -> Reminder {window}: the browser's row, made or changed; it becomes this account's whichever
    account held it, and counts as a visit.
  DELETE /api/reminders, ReminderOff {endpoint} -> 204, whether or not it was on; another account's row is untouched.
  POST /api/saves/{id}/open and /advance mark the account's browsers visited (at most once per ten minutes); a
  reminder is due from the latest visit.

SAVES ACROSS RELEASES AND PODS
  A save written under older rules (a deploy changed the content) is carried over, never refused: the server rebases
  it when it is next read (engine DESIGN.txt, "Rebase") and answers under the current rules, with any lines new
  content landed at the save's own time first. Each decision, advance and open is one transaction holding the save's
  row, so two requests on one save run one after the other, on any pod. Builds are ordered by content release: a
  number the release lock (opening/released/ids.json) raises by one whenever the content's rules change, so a later
  content always has a higher one, even one that changes only numbers. Each save records the release it was last
  written under. A pod answers 503 save_unavailable (Retry-After) for a save of a later release than its own, and for
  one it cannot rebase, and writes nothing: mid-deploy the next try reaches a pod of the later release. A save of the
  pod's own release or an earlier one is read exactly, or rebased forward, and written. A 4xx or 5xx answer writes
  nothing, except a 409, whose Step is written as an advance; so a decision is applied once, and a 409 or 5xx means
  it was not, bar one 503: a database switchover that loses the COMMIT (below, ERRORS).

REQUEST LIMITS
  A request body is at most 262144 bytes (413 too_large, ERRORS). Within it, a request past a bound below does not fit
  the models: 422 invalid_decision, and nothing is applied.
  A dry run takes at most 8 decisions.
  A string a request stores (a feedback text, a mix's id or name) holds no U+0000: 422 invalid_decision, before any
  limit is counted.
  A save takes at most 120 decisions a minute and 3600 an hour (each is a stored row), counted across pods: past
  either, POST /api/saves/{save_id}/decisions is 429 rate_limited with Retry-After (seconds), and nothing is applied or
  written. Only a decision that would be applied counts: a stale, catching_up or invalid one does not, and neither does
  a refused one. The hardest legitimate play is repeated taps on the shortest card, 20 a minute. The page treats it as
  any refused decision: redraws the save as it has it and goes on advancing it.
  PUT /api/me/presentation and PUT /api/saves/{save_id}/presentation: each list at most 512 items, each string in it (a lesson, a chip, a card) 1 to 64 ASCII
  letters, digits and `_ . : -`, except an offered switch's mix_id, a mix's own name, at most 64 characters. What an
  account (or a save) keeps weighs at most 262144 bytes of JSON: past that a PUT adds nothing more to the lists (answered 200,
  as at 512 entries).
  save_mix: at most 64 components; a save holds at most Content.limits.max_mixes (64) mixes, and a save_mix
  of a new id past that is 422 invalid_decision (replacing a saved mix is always allowed).
  set_orders: at most Content.limits.max_orders (64) orders. set_queue: at most Content.limits.max_queue (64) entries.
  The page's rules editor and "When ready" stop offering another at those, and say so in one plain line (ui.rule.full,
  ui.queue.full); the mix lists and the mix editor stop offering a new mix at max_mixes, the same way (ui.mix.full); its ledger, chip and repeated-switch rule offers stop at the rules' bound, quietly.

ERRORS: body ApiError {code, message, step, merge}. The message is for the console, never the player, and never
holds a code, words or a session. step is null except on stale and catching_up; merge is null except on confirm_merge and on a
join's too_many_saves.
  401 no_account        no live session: the browser has no account (start one with POST /api/saves).
  403 cross_site | 415 not_json   a write from another site, or a body that is not JSON (above).
  413 too_large         a request body over 262144 bytes (above). Nothing was read past the limit or applied.
  404 not_found         no such save on this account; no such device (Your devices).
  404 no_such_code      POST /api/join: the code or words name no account (above).
  409 stale             `after` is not the save's decisions: another page decided since. Nothing of the decision
                        was applied; `step` is the save advanced to now (written as an advance): draw it.
  409 catching_up       the save is still catching up on time away (CATCHING UP): nothing of the decision was applied
                        (a forecast and a dry run write nothing); `step` is the save as this request left it.
  409 confirm_merge     POST /api/join from a browser whose account holds a decision on a save (above).
  409 too_many_saves    POST /api/saves or a restore on an account holding max_saves live saves (Games on one account);
                        POST /api/join whose two accounts' live saves together would pass it (above).
  409 last_save         deleting the account's only live save.
  422 invalid_decision  a busy lane (unless Card.cuts_in), a locked, completed or retired card, a tap_card of a card on a ruled lane (A LANE
                        RUN BY RULES), an answer_offer the offer cannot take (OFFERS), a bad mix, deleting a mix something
                        still names, an order or queue condition watching `running`, a feedback text empty or over 2000 characters, or a request that does not
                        fit the models.
  429 rate_limited      too many new accounts just now: at most 10 an hour from one client address (an IPv6
                        address counts by its /64, and its /48 is held to 30 an hour) and 200 an hour in all; a limit that cannot be counted refuses. On POST /api/saves only when it would make an
                        account; the page shows one plain line and leaves Begin to be tried later.
                        POST /api/feedback answers it too, past its own limits (above); the page says to try later.
                        Also the limits of "Moving to another device", and new games on an account (Games on one
                        account), with Retry-After; and POST /api/saves/{save_id}/decisions past the decisions a
                        save takes (Request limits), with Retry-After.
  503 save_unavailable  retryable (Retry-After, seconds): no database (any endpoint that needs to know the account or
                        a save, Begin included), a save of a later release, a rebase that failed, or the database
                        switching over (its writable member changed or is unreachable). A request that meets the
                        switchover runs once more on a fresh connection, unless a write of its own may already have
                        committed; one not run again, or that meets it again, answers 503 with Retry-After 3. A 503
                        from a lost COMMIT may have taken effect: asking again then answers 409 stale for a decision.
                        The page retries with backoff and a quiet "reconnecting" state, keeping its view.
                        Also the three routes of "Moving to another device" without the join key.

FORECAST
  POST /api/saves/{save_id}/forecast answers ForecastAnswer {decisions, forecast}: the save advanced to now in memory (it
  writes nothing, so it carries no lines; a later advance brings them), and every level-up of every skill and every fill (reaching its cap from below) and emptying of
  each unlocked store that happens if the player changes nothing, by forecast.horizon_at (six game hours on, or less
  where the replay stopped: after 400 instants, or once the request has spent the time an advance may, as a long absence's
  catch-up is cut): orders, the queue and everything running are played out instant by instant as the engine
  would (engine DESIGN.txt, "Forecast"). So a time drawn from it comes true by itself. Nothing past horizon_at is
  known: the page draws no time for it, never an estimate. It is the save's own future while nobody decides, so it
  holds for every later view of the save while Step.decisions is still its `decisions`. It costs hours of replay, so
  it has its own call, kept off every View: the page asks when it holds none that holds (an open, a decision since,
  new rules) or its horizon is under an hour away (or, for one cut short, half its span), and only after a few quiet seconds with no decision of its own and
  at most once a minute, so a run of taps costs one forecast, not one each.
  forecast.tops_out lists, for each skill below its phase's top and not mastered, the instant it reaches that top
  ("tops out in"), by horizon_at; level_ups stop at a phase's top (PRESTIGE PHASES). Empty without prestige.

PRESTIGE PHASES (engine DESIGN.txt, "PRESTIGE PHASES")
  SkillBar.prestige is null when the content has no prestige, and SkillBar is then the curve's alone. Otherwise a
  skill climbs one XP segment through phases; SkillBar.level is its shown level, counting from 1 in each phase and
  stopping at the phase's top, level_xp and next_level_xp the bar's ends within the phase (next_level_xp null at the
  top), and SkillPrestige says, at view.at: the phase; level_counted, the level a skill-level condition reads, which
  never falls at a click; at_top, when the skill waits for the player's click and its XP still accrues for the carry
  window; waiting_levels, the levels a click now would land above the new phase's level 1 (it rises while the carry
  runs, so read it again at the next view); menu_nodes, the nodes the next pick may go into, in track order and the
  same all phase, each with ranks_taken of ranks and its target's before_value and after_value one more rank (at a
  full share: a make per hour, a card's work per second, or a cap, in the engine's units); undo_open, the last pick
  can be taken back; mastered, every node finished, so no click ever waits at its top; pick_owed, an undo took the
  last pick back and the next prestige fills it; topped_at, when the skill reached its current phase's top; picked, the
  nodes its picks hold in click order; and parked, the ranks a move parked, each {node, due}: out of play until phase
  `due` begins.

THE END
  view.ended_at is the game time the end happened: the first instant the content's end feature was unlocked, as the
  engine recorded it (engine DESIGN.txt, "The end"). Null before; once
  set it never clears, so the page never decides the end from live fields. The scene is at dusk while it is set. When
  the page first sees it, it appends Content.end_line_keys to the log as ordinary lines, and play goes on: nothing
  covers the screen. What waits on the end (an order card with after_end) comes once those lines have had twenty
  seconds to be read. A page reopened on a game whose end it had already shown (Opened.was carries ended_at) replays
  the lines only if the end was under thirty game seconds ago; an older end replays nothing, and what waits on it
  comes at once. An end that happened while the page was shut was never shown, so it plays in full. The content
  unlocks the feature a quiet stretch after its last step, so the end lines never land on that step's own; that step
  is a goal the page states (view.goals). all_covered is no part of the end.

GOALS (docs/decisions.md, "Progress is stated, never hinted")
  view.goals is what the open story beats wait on (app/ashfall_app/opening/goals.py: a beat unlocks something, brings
  or takes someone, or tells lines with no find on them): each store (store, target, value), card (card, target; 0 for
  the card running) and skill level (skill, target) an open beat waits on that is in play, a require on an unlock
  standing for what the earliest open trigger giving it waits on; then at most one clock, the soonest wait on the clock
  alone with a time (at), else one stopped on a dry store, with `feeds`, the shown stores whose running dry stops it
  (or any clock landing at that same time).
  The page puts the story mark (the Camp bar's ember ▸, no words) on each such card, ledger line (drawn from then on)
  and skill row, and on nothing else, so a bar without it is progress no beat waits on; a store with no line yet is a
  row in the Camp bar's box (its label, value against target, a bar, "something new"), the cards that make it marked;
  the clock is the wait line, plain text at the top of the deck and no card or control: "Something new", the time left,
  and the store it runs while there is some, or that store alone while it is dry. Nothing names a beat. `at` holds until next_event_at.

GOAL ROWS (docs/design/worth-a-change.md, "The payoff, shown before and after"; docs/research/fun-from-choice.md, proposal 1)
  view.goal_rows is each goal a mix can move: every store and level goal of view.goals, in that order, then each skill
  in view.skills whose next_level_xp is set, at its next shown level (level + 1), skipping one a story level goal
  already holds. A card's count and the clock are never rows, nor is a skill at its top: no next level, and a story
  level goal no XP in the skill's phase reaches. Each row is read at the rates as set: the engine's own
  shares and lines with a tap of the hands set aside, so the hands' mix runs at its shares and a card that yielded to
  the tap runs in full (app/ashfall_app/api/setrates.py). Rates are read at view.at; nothing projects an input running
  dry later, an order taking the hands, or a level speeding the work.
  - lands_at: view.at plus what is left over per_hour, rounded up; view.at when already met; null when per_hour is not
    above 0 or a store goal's target is over its LedgerLine.cap.
  - per_hour: the store's net (unclamped, and a tap of the hands set aside, so it can differ from its LedgerLine), or
    the XP per hour every runner training the skill gives. Each runner runs at its effective share: a paused or spent
    card's share is already handed to the rest of its mix (engine DESIGN.txt, "SPENT"), as is in_per_hour's.
  - limit: the first store, in running order, that a runner moving the goal (a maker of the store, a runner training
    the skill) is paused or spent on, among the stores view.ledger draws, with in_per_hour, its makers' lines alone;
    null when none is, and the goal's own line binds it, and null for a goal already met. Running order is the
    engine's: standing actions in rules order, then the hands' mix in its flattened card order, then the town's mix;
    never ledger order. A fact read off the runs, never a guess at what to change.
  - until: at_least {watched, value, release}, value == release, the condition that holds once the goal lands. A store
    row: watched store_value {store: row.store}, value row.target. A level row: watched skill_level {skill: row.skill},
    value the level counted across prestige phases, (phase - 1) * (top_level - 1) + row.target for the skill's phase
    (row.target in phase 1 and when the rules have no prestige), which is what a skill_level condition reads (engine
    DESIGN.txt, "PRESTIGE PHASES"). While the rates as set hold, the condition is false before lands_at and true from
    lands_at on; it is the `until` of a goal stint (GOAL STINTS below).
  THE CLIENT MAY, in the mix editor and the condition editor, compare a dry run's goal_rows with the live view's by
  (kind, store, skill, target) and list each row whose shown time or limit the edit changes: the goal (ui.goal.store,
  ui.goal.level), the time left now and after (each from its own view's `at`; ui.goal.never for null), and under it
  the after row's line: ui.goal.limited with the limit's store and in_per_hour per minute, else the store's label or
  ui.goal.xp with per_hour per minute. Never better, best, faster or any other verdict.

DRY RULES (docs/decisions.md, "A rule that cannot let go says so"; api/ruledry.py)
  view.order_dry[i] is null or {store, holding} for orders[i]; the server decides, the client only words it. It is set
  only for a rule whose `when` is at_most on a store_value and whose `then` is a switch_mix (a rule releases when the
  store rises above `release`), by the rates as set (GOAL ROWS above):
  - holding true, "can't reach": order_states[i] is on (the rule has the hands with its mix) AND the watched store's
    per_hour as set is not above 0 (the net the goal row of a store reads: GoalRow.per_hour) AND a runner making the
    watched store is paused or spent on a shown store: `store` is the first such, in running order (GoalRow.limit's
    computation, the same function). Nothing releases the rule: no auto-release, no change to a rule's defaults.
  - holding false, "may stop short": the rule's mix, flattened, has a card that makes the watched store and a card that
    costs a shown store that no card of the mix makes; `store` is the first such, in card order. It is read off the mix
    and the rule alone, so a rule that is off, outranked or not yet saved has it, and it never says the rule is stuck.
  - null otherwise, a falling-toward-release (at_least) rule and a skill or count subject included: unproven, not "fine".
  THE CLIENT MAY: on the Rules tab, under a rule with order_dry[i].holding true, one quiet line ui.rule.stuck {n}{store}
  (n the rule's release as the rule's own words give it, store the label); in the condition editor, beside Save, one line
  from a dry run of the decisions Save would send, read at the rule's index in that view's order_dry: ui.rule.stuck when
  holding, else ui.rule.unfed {store}{n}. Save is never blocked and no default changes. The line states a fact; it names
  no fix.

GOAL STINTS (docs/design/worth-a-change.md, "What to add first", item 1; engine DESIGN.txt, "Queue mix")
  A goal stint is a queue entry {verb: switch_mix {mix_id}, until: a GoalRow's until}: "run {mix} until {goal}". The
  server holds no stint type: set_queue takes it as any entry (a hands mix the save holds, at most
  Content.limits.max_queue entries; a town mix is 422 invalid_decision), and every rule below holds for any queue
  entry whose verb is a switch_mix, whatever its until.
  - Controlling: the first entry of view.queue whose verb is not dead (a switch_mix to a mix in view.greyed_mix_ids, a
    tap_card of a completed card) controls. While a switch_mix entry controls and no order's mix runs (view.mix_order

FIGHTS
  A fight is a card the player runs (a hunt) or a standing action that runs on its own (a threat at the stores), with
  odds on it: what a won fight brings, what a lost one costs, and the share lost, set by the side's strength against
  the threat's (engine DESIGN.txt, "FIGHTS"). Nothing on the wire is chance: the odds are exact and every pay is a
  rate. Content.side_infos: [SideInfo {side, label_key}]; Content.threat_infos: [ThreatInfo {threat, label_key}];
  CardInfo and StandingInfo gain fight: FightInfo {side, threat, loses: [Amount], wounds: [Amount], won_line_keys,
  lost_line_keys} or null; the two lists are the runner's pools of lines for one fight won and one lost (keys of
  Content.line_infos), words only.
  View.fights: [Fight {runner, side, threat, power: Strength, threat_strength: Strength, loss_bp, fought, lost}], one
  per unlocked fight card or standing action, cards first, each in content order. Strength {value, sources:
  [StrengthEntry {source, form, amount_bp, share_bp}]}: value is what the engine uses; sources are every source behind
  it, the base first as {source: null, form: "base", amount_bp: the base}, then the sums (add_bp), then the factors
  (mul_bp), each in stack order. share_bp is set only on an entry a card holds while it runs (engine DESIGN.txt,
  "ENTRIES HELD WHILE A CARD RUNS"): source is that card's id, share_bp its share of its lane's time now (10000 for a
  tap, its town or hands mix share otherwise), amount_bp what that share gives (the card's whole amount times
  share_bp / 10000, rounded down); null on every other entry, so a held entry and a permanent one from the same card
  are told apart by it. The page draws a held entry's source label and its share from these fields alone and never
  multiplies a share itself. A standing action may hold one too (engine DESIGN.txt, "HELD SPEED"): its source is the
  action's id (cards and standing actions share one namespace) and its share_bp 10000 while the action runs, the
  entry absent while it is paused. loss_bp is the share of fights
  lost now, 1 to 10000; for a running tap, the share it was started at and pays to its end. fought and lost count since
  the runner first ran; lost <= fought.
  A fight's pay is in the ledger like any line: its makes already scaled by the share won, its loses and wounds by the
  share lost, so the ledger's numbers are the fight's numbers. A card's Card.needs and takes_s are as for any card.
  Content.side_infos and threat_infos carry only the words (the strengths are View.fights). CardInfo.fight and
  StandingInfo.fight name the side and threat and what one lost fight takes (loses) and adds (wounds).
  The feature `combat` (FeatureInfo, a FEATURE unlock) switches the page's fight parts on; a fight runner is only ever
  unlocked with or after it.

JOURNAL
  The book the player carries, written in: one page per thing known, holding the notes written about it and, for a
  thing fought, its learned numbers (decisions.md, "What you learn is written in a journal", "Blind until learned,
  perfect once known"). It is a reading of what the wire already carries: an entry and each of its notes are unlocks,
  and the numbers are View.fights. View gains nothing. The page draws the journal from Content.journal,
  view.unlocked and view.fights, and keeps what the player has read in AccountPresentation. Page design:
  docs/design/opening-screen.md, "The journal". Fits into Unlocks, Knowledge (as a page reading), Lessons and the
  page shell; not a system (one caller, nothing to swap).

  The book is in sections, a closed set, and each page is in one:
    days                the daily journal: the story's bigger beats, retold afterwards in the character's voice (the
                        chronicle stays the live feed). Each note is written on a spine beat and names it.
    creatures           the things fought, with their numbers.
    places              places, events and expeditions.
  Within a section, pages run in the order they are written. The content keeps that order: a section's pages are
  declared (chapters in load order) in the order their first notes are written on every route, so content order is
  writing order and nothing on the wire carries a time.

  In the chapter files:
    [journal]           feature: a FeatureId with a label_key (the book the player carries). Declared once, in the
                        chapter that holds the first entry or an earlier one; Content.journal is null until then.
    [[entries]]         id (an EntryId), section, sketch, hand, runners
      section           "days", "creatures" or "places"
      sketch            optional: the entry's drawing, an id matching [a-z0-9_]+, the image
                        /assets/journal/<sketch>.png. A page with none is laid out as text alone, which is valid.
      hand              "ink" or "pencil": how the entry's notes are written where a note does not say
      runners           the fight cards and standing fights whose numbers the page shows, in this order; [] for none
    [[notes]]           id (a NoteId), entry, gate, requires, hand, sketch, beat
      beat              on a days page, required: the spine beat the note retells, an id of content/beats.yaml's
                        beats (B5). Its gate is that beat's own step, so the note is written at the beat's instant.
                        Refused on a note of any other section. The loader holds its presence and shape; the canon is
                        not shipped, so tests hold that it names a beat in beats.yaml and that, on every played
                        route, the note is written at the instant that beat lands.
      entry             the entry the note is written on, declared in this chapter or an earlier one
      gate, requires    when it is written, as an offer's: the loader makes the trigger "note_" + id, whose outcome
                        unlocks the note and, for the entry's first note, the entry. Every other note's trigger also
                        requires the entry unlocked, so a later note is a later outcome (or the first's own instant),
                        never a note on a page that is not there.
      hand              optional: the entry's hand when absent
      sketch            optional: a small drawing beside the note, an id as for an entry
    [text] <note id>    the note's words: a note's id is its text key (there is no text_key field)
  An entry's first note is the first [[notes]] naming it in the chapter that declares the entry. A note's outcome is
  only those unlocks: it writes no line and adds to no store.

  The opening's checks (each naming ids, never text):
    - a note whose entry is not declared in its chapter or an earlier one: refused, naming the note and the entry.
    - an entry no [[notes]] in its own chapter names (no first note): refused, naming the entry.
    - a runner that is neither a card with a fight nor a standing action with a fight: refused, naming the entry and
      the runner.
    - a sketch id outside [a-z0-9_]+ (it is a path): refused, naming the entry or note.
    - a note on a days page with no beat, a beat outside [A-Za-z0-9-]+, or a beat on a note of another section:
      refused, naming the note.
    - an entry or a note while no chapter so far declares [journal]; [journal] declared twice; its feature unknown or
      without a label_key: refused.
    - a note with no [text] entry, or whose text holds a digit: refused by the existing text checks (Text and
      numbers). A note writes its numbers as words ("two dozen"); the exact numbers are served and drawn below it.
    - entry and note ids are content ids like any other: new ids only across chapters, kept by the release lock, and
      renamed by renames.toml under the id kinds entry and note. A note_ trigger's outcome is locked as any trigger's,
      so a released entry keeps its first note.

  Engine: UnlockKind gains entry (its id an EntryId) and note (a NoteId), ordinary kinds (not PRESENT_ONLY), renamed
  through IdKind ENTRY and NOTE; engine DESIGN.txt, "Unlocks", lists them. The engine never reads them. Gates read
  them as any unlock (subject "unlocked" with unlock_kind entry or note), so a gate on several entries is one Unlocked
  condition each, and no count store mirrors them.

  Content.journal: JournalInfo {feature, entries: [EntryInfo {entry, section, sketch, runners: [str], notes:
  [NoteInfo {note, hand, sketch}]}]} | null. entries in content order (chapters in load order), each entry's notes in
  content order; within one section, content order is writing order. Section is a closed enum, "days" | "creatures"

THE TOWN'S MIX
  Once people join, the townsfolk's lanes are run by one mix whose shares are people's time: each share is a part of
  one person's time, the person being the lane its card is on (engine DESIGN.txt, "THE TOWN'S MIX"). It is an
  ordinary saved mix in view.mixes; which one runs is set by a rule, never by the player's switch.
  Content: nothing new on LaneInfo or CardInfo. Unlock categories gain joined (id a lane): that lane's person has
  joined. The people present are the lanes in content order whose LaneInfo.ruled is true and whose joined unlock
  view.unlocked holds, and whose left unlock it does not (LEAVES).
  View: running_town_mix_id (the town mix running now: the first rule that is on with a switch_mix of a town mix that
  isn't greyed; null when none runs) and town_mix_order (the index into orders of that rule; null when none).
  A mix is a town mix when every card it holds, nested mixes flattened, has a CardInfo.lane that is ruled; a hands
  mix when every card is on hands; a mix with no card is a town mix when a join makes it or it was a town mix before it emptied, else a hands mix. running_mix_id and mix_order read only hands
  mixes, as before. A card of the running town mix has its Card.share_bp and Card.run as a hands mix card has, and
  Card.by is mix, or yielded while a rule's tap on its lane runs (its LaneBar.running_card is then that tap's card;
  while the lane runs only shares, running_card is null and completes_at null). A share of a card whose lane has not
  joined, or that is locked, idles.
  Decisions:
  - save_mix of a town mix: 422 invalid_decision, nothing written, when it holds cards of both kinds of mix, a card
    whose lane has not joined, a card with a cap, or one person's shares summing above 10000; and for a new mix id
    beginning "town:" (those are the game's; the one it made may be edited).
  - switch_mix from the player of a town mix: 422 (set by rules). set_queue with a switch_mix of a town mix: 422 (the
    queue is the hands' plan). set_orders accepts one: that is the town's rule.
  - The town's always rule: an order {when: after_time {time: 0}, then: switch_mix m}, m a town mix. When the first
    person joins, the game makes the town's mix and appends this rule (rule_runs lists it, on at once), and each
    joining person's current always task becomes their whole share in it (their always rules on uncapped cards leave
    view.orders; their other rules stay where they were and still act, as taps that take the whole of that person
    while they run). The rule and the mix are the player's to edit from then on.
  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - draw the town as one strip under the hands strip, in place of the joined lanes' rows (a ruled lane not joined

SPENT
  A mix card stopped on a dry store gives its share to the other cards of the same running mix on the same lane, in
  proportion to their own shares, until it starts again (engine DESIGN.txt, "SPENT"): a store something in play can
  refill takes it back when the store reaches an hour of the card's cost, and a store nothing in play can make again
  (a pool after its season) when an outcome refills it. Both are the same yield; they differ in what brings the card
  back. The share the player set never changes on its own.
  View: Card.run is paused for a card stopped on a store something can refill, and spent (its store will not refill
  by itself) for one stopped on a store nothing can make again; either runs nothing. Card gains spent_store, the
  store a spent card is stopped on (null unless run is spent), and gained: [SpentShare {card, share_bp}], the part
  of its share each stopped card of its mix on its lane gives it now, in mix order (empty when none). Card.share_bp
  stays the share the player set; the card runs at share_bp plus the sum of gained, and a card that gains while it
  is waiting runs its gain as it runs its own share. The split that runs now is each card's share_bp plus
  its gained, a paused or spent card's 0; it differs from the split set exactly while some card of the running mix
  is paused or spent. A ledger line is not starved by a spent card alone (LedgerLine.starved), so a used-up pool does not show as
  a problem.
  Nothing on Content changes and no decision changes: save_mix and the split panel set share_bp only.
  THE CLIENT MUST:
  - while the running mix's split that runs differs from the one set, show it under the mix as a separate "running
    now" split (ui.mix.running_now): each card at share_bp plus gained, a receiving card naming each source with
    ui.mix.from {pct} {card}, a stopped card's part greyed with ui.mix.waiting {card}; and show nothing of it once
    no card of the mix is paused or spent.
  THE CLIENT MAY:
  - draw a spent card's state with ui.mix.spent {store} (spent_store's label).
  - keep the split panel and the mix editor on share_bp: what the player set is what they edit.
  THE CLIENT MAY NOT:
  - add gained into a share the player edits or the bar of the split set, or work out a gain itself.
  - draw a spent card as greyed or as a problem: the mix runs on.

  Rejected, and why (spent on the wire):
  - share_bp served as the effective share: the split panel would show, and save back, a split the player never set.
  - The page finding the spent store from the card's needs and the ledger: a second home for what the engine knows.
  - Marking a card whose share moved only when its store is seasonal: a card that behaves like the others but looks
    different, or the reverse (docs/decisions.md, "A mix keeps going without a dry store").

                                    ui.mix.running_now            the label of the split that runs now
                                    ui.mix.from {pct} {card}      a receiving card's gain, naming its source
                                    ui.mix.waiting {card}         a stopped card's part of the split that runs now
