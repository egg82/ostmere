The Breaking of Ostmere

What the game keeps about you, why, and for how long.

Your game

Playing creates an anonymous account for you. There is no name, email address or password. Your browser gets one cookie, __Host-ashfall_session, holding a random code that tells the server which account is yours. It is the only cookie the game sets, it lasts up to 400 days, and it is renewed (at most once a day) whenever you play. The server keeps only a scrambled copy of that code, so a copy of its database could not be used to sign in as you.

Your saves are kept on the server so your game is there when you come back, on any device you move it to. A save holds the state of your game, when you began and last played, and the lines your game has written. Every move you make is recorded with its game time and the clock time you made it. Moves are kept for 30 days, and so are the daily copies of the save; the save itself and its lines stay as long as the save does.

A game you have not made a single move in, on an account unused for 30 days, is deleted automatically. A game with moves in it is never deleted automatically. If your browser does not come back for 400 days its sign-in lapses, so keep your recovery words if the game matters to you.

The account also remembers a few things about the screen, such as which panels you have opened. Your browser keeps a couple of notes in its session storage so a page can reload cleanly after an update; they go when you close the tab.

Moving to another device

A transfer code is eight characters, works once, and expires after ten minutes. Recovery words are four words, shown once. Both are stored only as scrambled values, so nobody, including whoever runs the game, can read them back. That also means lost recovery words cannot be looked up for you.

Limits on abuse

New accounts, new games, transfer codes and similar are limited so one visitor cannot flood the game. To count them the server keeps a keyed scramble of your IP address (for IPv6, of your whole network), never the address itself. Each entry is dropped once its hour, or less, has passed; old entries are cleared out whenever a new one is counted.

Server logs

The server writes a line for each request: the time, the page asked for, whether it worked, and how long it took. For a move it also notes the account and save numbers, the kind of move, the card it named, and how many seconds after you began and after your last move it came. The logs hold no IP address, no cookie and nothing you typed. They are ordinary server logs, kept for 60 days and then deleted.

Statistics and error reports

The page counts visits with Umami, run by the game's owner on a server of their own: page views and a few events, such as beginning a game, starting, switching, deleting or restoring a game (with the save's number), reaching a milestone, and reaching the end. Ids and counts only, never what you read or typed. The page asks Umami to respect your browser's Do Not Track setting. If you block it, nothing about playing changes.

The page also reports to the game's own server when something breaks (the error's message, where in the code it was, which build you run) and when you leave: how long you played, the game clock, the last card you tapped, your number of moves, and whether you reached the end. These go into the server logs above, not to Umami.

Feedback you write

When you send feedback, the game keeps your words (up to 2000 characters) with the build you run and, if the save is yours, your account and save numbers, the game time, and which line your game had written last. The owner reads it. It is kept for 180 days and then deleted. The text is never written to the logs.

Who else carries the game

The game's traffic passes through Cloudflare, so Cloudflare sees your IP address and what you ask for, as it does for any site it serves. There are no ads, nothing is sold or shared, and the game loads no fonts or scripts from anyone else; its only outside call is to the statistics tool above.

Having your data removed

In the game you can delete a game (the last one cannot be deleted). It is held for 30 days in case you change your mind, then removed for good, with its moves and daily copies. The removal happens the next time any player deletes a game or starts one, so it can come a little after the 30 days. For anything else, such as removing your account or a feedback message, use the Feedback button in the game and say what you want removed.
