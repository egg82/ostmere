ENGINE DESIGN: the single home of the engine's semantics, and the invariants contract tests check. Contract:
docs/systems.md, "The engine". Code docstrings summarise and point at the part file by the lead word of a CHOICES entry.

One file per part, in design/. A later part wins where it disagrees with an earlier one ("Where this part and an entry
above disagree, this part holds"), and invariants are numbered across the parts. Concatenate the files below, in this
order, to read it as one document.
