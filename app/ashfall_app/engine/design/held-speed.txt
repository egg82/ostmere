HELD SPEED, AND ENTRIES HELD BY STANDING ACTIONS (a joiner who is fed works faster: stew earns its place as a bonus
while fed, 2026-11-10). Where this part and ENTRIES HELD WHILE A CARD RUNS disagree, this part holds; everything
there not named here is unchanged. Examples name ids only; which runners, lanes and amounts is content's. Blind tests
are written from this part, that part and api/contract/, against app/tests/engine_contract/heldspeed/MUTANTS_heldspeed.txt.

FIT PASS
- The need: while a joiner's eating (a standing action) runs, that joiner's work runs faster; while it is paused on a
  dry store, it does not. An instance of Modifiers held while a runner runs, which ENTRIES HELD WHILE A CARD RUNS
  already is, aimed at a target the stack already has: LaneSpeed (Speed), which scopes an entry to every line on one
  lane, so to one joiner. Two of that part's refusals are lifted, each for a reason its text did not weigh (its
  REJECTED entries carry a dated pointer here). No new system, Save field, verb or event kind.
- How games do it: RimWorld's "ate a fine meal" and Don't Starve's food buffs are a bonus held while fed and gone when
  the larder is empty, never a penalty for plain food. Nystrom's Component again (the entries are an optional part of
  a StandingDef that its own machinery never reads).

CHOICES (held speed)
- Shape: a second dedicated type, HeldSpeed(lane: LaneId, amount_bp: int), exported beside WhileRunning. A runner's
  while_running is tuple[WhileRunning | HeldSpeed, ...]. Each HeldSpeed is an ADD_BP entry on LaneSpeed(lane), sourced
  "held:" + the runner's id, at the runner's held share. Kind, target and source stay fixed by the type: no held entry
  can aim at a rate, a WorkRef, a cap, a threat or HANDS, or be a multiplier.
- Why lane speed is now allowed (lifting part of "Held entries on rates, work, lane speeds or caps"): that refusal's
  reason was HANDS's kept speed. A HANDS tap keeps the speed entries it started under (Speed, LaneState.speed), so a
  speed entry that came and went with someone else's work would make a tap's shown time depend on who else works.
  Every other lane keeps no speed: its lines read the live stack at every instant (Speed, "Other lanes stay live"), as
  a chip aimed at one already does. So a HeldSpeed may name any declared lane and never HANDS (validation), and no
  LaneState.speed ever holds a held entry. The rest of that refusal stands: a card's own effect on rates is its
  lines, so no held entry aims at a rate or a WorkRef, and none at a cap.
- Why standing actions may now hold entries (lifting "while_running on standing actions"): that refusal assumed a
  standing action runs whenever unlocked. It does not while paused on a dry store (Standing, the dry rule), and that
  pause is exactly the state a fed bonus turns on, so its entry is not an outcome landed at the unlock. StandingDef
  gains `while_running: tuple[WhileRunning | HeldSpeed, ...] = ()`, either kind, validated as a card's.
- The held share of a standing action a: h(a) = BP while a runs (unlocked, not stopped by a leave, and holding no
  Pause in save.pauses); else 0. It reads only the unlock set and save.pauses, never a rate, so building the stack
  still never recurses into running(). A card's h(c) is unchanged.
- The dry rule is unchanged and already right for it: it pauses one consumer at a time and rebuilds after each, so a
  paused eater's speed entry has left its lane before the next consumer is picked, and pausing stops at the first
  pause that brings the net to 0 or above. Eaters of one store pause last first (standing in reverse rules order), so
  with a store short of all its eaters the later ones go unfed and the earlier ones stay fed: the bonus is per
  runner. A resume (its Pause's level reached) returns the entry at that instant.
- The entries: while h > 0, for each HeldSpeed(lane, a) in declaration order, Modifier(LaneSpeed(lane), ADD_BP,
  a * h // BP, "held:" + id); WhileRunning entries as before. They join base_stack where held entries already do,
  so every line on that lane (each make, cost and XP of what runs there, and each card's work there) reads them, and
  explain lists them among that line's adds with their "held:" source, aimed at the line itself as it aims every entry
  it lists (rates.aimed_stack).
- Ids: a held source is "held:" + the runner's id, which names one runner because runner ids are already unique
  across cards and standing actions (Validation, "duplicate runner id").
- Validation (InvalidRulesError), per runner, cards and standing actions alike: each WhileRunning's side declared, as
  before; each HeldSpeed's lane one rules.lanes declares (HANDS is never declared, so naming it is refused); amount_bp
  >= 1 on each; no side twice and no lane twice in one runner's while_running. A message names the runner id and the side or lane id; a test may assert both appear, and nothing else of it.
- View: View.held lists standing holders too, after the cards, in rules.standing order, with share_bp BP; HeldView's
  `card` field holds the runner's id (one namespace). The wire's Strength.sources serves a standing holder's side
  entry as a card's (source the runner id, share_bp BP). A HeldSpeed entry is on no strength, so it reaches the wire
  only through the rates and work times it changes, and through CardInfo.change_bp (contract/text-and-numbers.txt, "upside_of").
- Codec: HeldSpeed joins the rules encoding's classes; a StandingDef's `while_running` is left out while empty, as a
  card's is, so rules that declare none keep their digest. No Save field, no manifest change, FORMAT unchanged.

INVARIANTS (held speed; numbered on from 155. Invariants 110-118 hold for standing holders and HeldSpeed as below.)
156. Held exactly while running, standing: for a standing action a with while_running, the effective stack holds,
    per entry of a, exactly one Modifier(target, ADD_BP, amount_bp, "held:" + a) while a is unlocked, not stopped
    and not paused, and none sourced "held:" + a otherwise; target is SideStrength(side) for a WhileRunning and
    LaneSpeed(lane) for a HeldSpeed.
157. Speed scoped to the lane: with a HeldSpeed(l, A) held at share h, every make, cost and XP line of a runner on
    lane l, and every card's work on l, equals the same rules with Modifier(LaneSpeed(l), ADD_BP, A * h // BP) written
    into save.modifiers by hand; no line on another lane, HANDS included, changes.
158. Dry pause drops it: when a standing holder is paused on a dry store at T, its entries leave the stack at T and
    every line on its HeldSpeed lanes is rebuilt at T; they return at the instant its Pause resumes. Of several
    holders eating one store that runs short, the dry rule pauses them in reverse rules order and stops at the first
    pause that brings the store's net to 0 or above, so the earlier ones keep their entries.
159. Never kept: no LaneState.speed of any save holds a held entry, and a HANDS tap's time is unchanged by any
    HeldSpeed.
160. Validation: InvalidRulesError for a HeldSpeed naming HANDS or an undeclared lane, amount_bp below 1, one lane twice
    in one runner, and a standing action's WhileRunning naming an undeclared side or one side twice.
161. Timing does not matter (113's shape) with a standing holder whose store runs dry and refills.
Invariant 114 now reads: "no ThreatStrength explanation and no rate, work or cap Explanation lists a WhileRunning's
entry, and only the lines on a HeldSpeed's lane list that HeldSpeed's entry; s.modifiers ..." and the rest unchanged.

REJECTED, AND WHY (held speed)
- A positive chip "fed" on the eaten store: it bends chips (bad states with cures), and a store-level latch cannot say
  which eater is paused when the store is short of some of them.
- HeldSpeed on HANDS: its taps keep their speed, the original refusal's case, which still holds.
- A multiplier: as for sides, an add scales linearly with the share and has one meaning.
- A held entry on a runner's own rates or work (a RateRef, a WorkRef): a card's own effect on rates is its lines.
- Feeding the speed from the eaten store's level: modifiers never read stores.

KNOWN GAPS (held speed)
- A HeldSpeed on a lane whose card is a fight raises the fight's rate with the lane's speed, as any speed entry there
  does; no content does this yet. Decided by: whoever first writes it.
