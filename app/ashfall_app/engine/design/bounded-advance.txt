FIT PASS
- Nystrom's Game Loop "catch up", with the inner loop that bails after a maximum number of iterations: the game
  slows down instead of locking up. It is advance_logged given a stop, no new type and no Save field; the caller owns
  the budget (the engine reads no clock), and invariant 1 already makes a stop between instants cost nothing.

CHOICES (bounded advance)
- advance_logged(s, r, t, stop) asks stop() after each instant resolves. True while an instant at or before t
  remains ends the call at that instant: Step.save.time is its time (not shifted to t) and Step.stopped_short is
  True. A stop that says True after the last instant at or before t changes nothing: the save is shifted to t as
  without one. advance, decide and decide_logged take no stop.

INVARIANTS (bounded advance; numbered on from 72)
73. Slices: for any stop, repeating advance_logged(s_i, r, t, stop) from each Step's save (encoded and decoded
    between calls or not) until one does not stop short ends at the save of advance_logged(s, r, t); its lines and
    rule_changes, each Step's in turn, are that call's. Each stopped Step resolved at least one instant.
74. Lane rules change only at a decision: advancing any length of time with no verb never fires a trigger on a LaneRules
    condition, and an install of a lane's always rule leaves the count unchanged.
