OR ELSE (a rule's fallbacks: issue #91, docs/design/rule-guards.md, which holds the design, the page's words and the
contract). Where this part and an entry above disagree, this part holds.

FIT PASS
- A fallback is an instance of Standing orders: its verbs are OrderVerbs, read as the order list is read, first that
  can act. A rule with actions a1..an stands for n adjacent rules sharing its `when` and guards, one action each, and
  every reader walks it that way (orders.listed), so evaluation gains no rule of its own. No new event kind, verb or
  Save field; the latch is the rule's, one entry as before.

CHOICES (or else)
- Shape: Order.or_else, a tuple of OrderVerbs; Order.actions is `then` followed by it. Left out of the codec's text
  while empty, so every save and logged SetOrders without one keeps its text: FORMAT is unchanged.
- SetOrders refuses (InvalidVerbError, checks.check_actions) more than MAX_ACTIONS (3) actions, an action listed twice,
  and, with any fallback: a first action that answers an offer, two kinds of verb, taps of cards on two lanes, mixes of
  two layers. Each action passes the checks a `then` passes. A tap of an undeclared card puts no lane in the
  comparison, as a `then` naming one is accepted today.
- Readers walk orders.listed (rule index, action, the rule's latch), rule order then list order: the tap source, the
  running mix of each layer, candidates (so READY falls for every listed tap of a rule that is on), the lane order,
  and the READY watch of a repeating lane (a fallback running as a rate lands once an earlier action can act). Readers
  asking whether any rule names a verb (requeued, the cut-in's _ordered, coverage, DeleteMix) read every action.
- A rule's taps share one lane, so it starts at most one tap an instant, and the loop of "Evaluation applies every verb"
  still ends. Readers that branch on the kind of a rule's verb (the firing cap on a mix rule's LATCH, answers) read
  `then`: every action is that kind.
- order_states: passed over when every action is dead (is_dead); outranked compares the rule's lane (that of its first
  declared tap) or its layer as before. order_doing[i] (View, out of the repr): the index in actions of the card its
  lane runs for it (lane_order) or of the mix that runs by it, else None.
- Taking actions out (installs.without_actions, orders.pruned): a season's retire_orders and a join's removal of the
  lane's always rules take the matching actions out of each rule's list, the rule keeping its place and its latch;
  a rule left with none is removed with its latch. A rule a retirement only trimmed is not logged as ended, nor as
  coming on (rulelog matches it through the same trim). LaneRules counts a rule once if any action taps the lane.
- A rebase takes an action naming a gone id out of `or_else` as out of `then`; the rule goes when none is left.

INVARIANTS (or else; numbered on from 172. listed(s) is orders.listed(s.orders, s.latches).)
173. Unchanged without fallbacks: a save whose orders hold no fallback encodes, evaluates, logs and reads exactly as
     before Order.or_else existed; a logged SetOrders without one keeps its text.
174. Expansion: from the same save, decisions and times, a save holding rule o with actions a1..an and one holding in
     its place the n adjacent rules Order(o.when, ak, o.unless) agree on stores, skills, lanes, cards, pauses, unlocks,
     the queue and the running mix, at instants that do not hit the firing cap, under rules where no trigger reads
     LaneRules.
175. Invariant 57 reads "an order with a true latch has a TapCard" as "a rule that is on lists a tap".

REJECTED, AND WHY (or else)
- Storing which action a rule chose: the choice is a function of the save at that instant (docs/design/rule-guards.md,
  "No memory"); a stored choice would be state with no home and a second "until".
- Expanding the rules into one-action orders inside the save: the latch, the rule log, the page and SetOrders' equality
  all speak of one rule; expansion lives in the reading, not the saved shape.
