LEAVES (a person leaves the town: docs/design/week-three-plan.md, item 4; blind tests from this part and
api/contract/ alone, item 5, against app/tests/engine_contract/MUTANTS_leaves.txt). The inverse of THE TOWN'S MIX's
Join (engine/joins.py). Where this part and an entry above disagree, this part holds. Examples name ids only; who
leaves, when and why is content's.

FIT PASS
- People (a person is a ruled lane, present once joined), Lanes, Mixes, Standing orders and Unlocks, each run
  backwards. Joining is an outcome part landing once with an only-growing fact; leaving is the same: an outcome part
  whose fact, this lane's person has left, is one more only-growing unlock kind. No Save field, verb, event kind or
  system: what follows from the fact (the lane's cards out of play, the listed standings stopped) is derived wherever
  it is read, as retirement is; what the player's plan held (orders, shares) is edited once, as a join edits it.
- Retirement already says "out of play for good, by an only-growing fact"; a leave retires a lane's cards by that one
  predicate, except that it never greys a mix: a person's shares are their own time (THE TOWN'S MIX, "Town share"), so
  removing them changes nobody else's, and greying would stop every lane for one person.
- How games do it: RimWorld and Banished remove a colonist and their work-table rows go with them, the other workers'
  priorities untouched; a departed companion's skills leave the party sheet (Nystrom: Command, the leave a part in the
  log; Type Object, a lane's cards are data that go out of play by a fact, never deleted from the rules).

CHOICES (leaves)
- Shape: Outcome.leaves, a tuple of Leave(lane: LaneId, stops: tuple[ActionId, ...] = ()), exported from the engine
  package beside Join. Allowed only in a TriggerDef's and an AnswerDef's outcome (once-only, as installs and joins).
  It lands after `retire_orders` and before `orders` and `joins`: raises, retire_orders, leaves, orders, joins,
  queues, starts, then lines.
- Left: UnlockKind gains LEFT ("left"), id a LaneId, held as JOINED is (PRESENT_ONLY: written to save.unlocked and
  counted only when present, whatever rules.opening is). Only Outcome.leaves adds it (validation refuses it in any
  Outcome.unlocks and in Rules.opening). A lane's person is present when JOINED is held and LEFT is not; a ruled lane
  with LEFT held is gone, joined or not. Unlocks only grow, so a leave is for good: a person who returns is a new lane
  id.
- Each leave, in tuple order, when Unlock(LEFT, lane) is not yet held, at that instant:
  1. add Unlock(LEFT, lane);
  2. the lane's tap ends: the lane is freed, and the running card's completion is dropped: no push line, no outcome,
     no completion; its inputs taken at the start stay taken, and what its rate form has already paid stays (a store
     is never wound back). The work segment stays where it stopped, on a card now out of play;
  3. remove from save.orders, with their latches, every order whose verb is a TapCard of a card on the lane (each
     logged ended LEFT, "Retired chores", "Logged");
  4. remove from every saved mix each CardShare of a card on the lane; a mix
     left with no component stays, empty (its rule, if any, stays: it runs nothing; an emptied mix keeps the TOWN layer: the leave sets Mix.layer,
     THE TOWN'S MIX, "Layer");
  5. remove from save.pauses the pause of each card on the lane and of each standing action in `stops`: nothing waits
     on that store for them any more, so it is not starved on their account;
  A leave of a lane already left changes nothing.
- Out of play, derived: a card whose lane has LEFT held counts as retired by the one predicate "completed or retired"
  (CARDS, "Retired"), with no RETIRED unlock written: a tap of it is refused (InvalidVerbError from the player; nothing
  from an order, the queue or an Outcome's start), SaveMix refuses it, it makes nothing (and so is no maker, SPENT),
  covers nothing, and holds no entries (ENTRIES HELD WHILE A CARD RUNS). It is in neither View.cards nor
  View.completed: it went with the person. Unlocked(CARD) of it stays true (unlocks grow).
- Never greys: step 4 takes the lane's cards out of every saved mix at the instant they go out of play, so no mix
  holds one. Other lanes' shares are untouched: no share moves to another person (each person's shares are their own
  time), and SPENT's yield never crosses lanes.
- Stops: a standing action named in the `stops` of the Leave of a lane whose LEFT is held is stopped: it reads as a
  locked standing action everywhere (contributes 0 to every rate, no Run in View.standing, explain's share_bp 0, no
  maker), though Unlocked(ACTION) of it stays true. Derived from the rules' Leave and the unlock, never stored; so
  validation allows at most one Leave per lane across all of the rules' outcomes, and a stopped standing stays
  stopped for good.
- Nothing lands on a left lane: an install whose card is on a left lane, a join of a left lane, and a start of a card
  on one change nothing, in the same outcome or any later one. An order a SetOrders sets tapping such a card is
  accepted, as for a retired card, and is passed over (its card cannot act); the page builds none.
- Read model (view.py): View.lanes holds no LaneBar for a left lane; View.standing leaves out stopped standings; the
  joined lanes the town strip draws are those not left. running_town_mix, town_mix_order and the town mix's cards
  read as before over the mix step 4 left.
- Codec: FORMAT unchanged. LEFT is one more UnlockKind value; Outcome gains `leaves` (rules and released outcomes only,
  never a save), left out of the rules encoding while empty (as `joins`), so rules without a leave keep their
  digest; Leave joins the codec's classes. Save's fields are unchanged (app/tests/held/save_fields.json). A save
  written before this part holds no LEFT and decodes as before.
- Rebase: step 4 drops Unlock(LEFT) of a gone lane. Step 6 lands a leave whose trigger is already true at the rebase instant,
  so a save past the moment loads with the person gone: LEFT held, the lane's orders and shares removed (each order
  logged ended LEFT in rebase_logged), its tap ended unpaid.
- Content (the loader): OutcomeEntry gains `leaves`, a list of {lane, stops (default [])}, refused outside a
  trigger's or an answer's outcome; lane a ruled lane, stops standing action ids; ValueError naming the trigger or
  answer and the lane ("trigger {id}: leaves {lane}, which is not a ruled lane"; "trigger {id}: leaves {lane}, which
  another outcome already leaves"). UnlockEntry and ConditionEntry's unlock_kind gain "left", refused in an
  outcome's unlocks and the opening as "joined" is. Combat's gap for people lost by a fight's tally stays open
  (FIGHTS, KNOWN GAPS): this is an outcome, not a fight.
- Answers to the blind tests' questions (leaves):
  - Leave(lane, stops=()), those two fields in that order, a frozen dataclass in engine/cards.py exported from
    ashfall_app.engine beside Join.
  - A SaveMix naming a card of a left lane raises InvalidVerbError (the mix checks' "card {c} is completed": the one
    out-of-play predicate), as for any retired card. A player's TapCard of one raises InvalidVerbError too (a ruled
    lane's card is never the player's to tap).
  - A rate-form task stopped by a leave keeps exactly what its lines had paid at T: the stores read at T as a reading
    without the leave would (each store's segment at integer T, its lump lines' floor(G(T)) as "Rate form" rounds
    them), and nothing after. The rest of the completion's outcome stores are never paid.
  - Validation (InvalidRulesError): "{where}: only a trigger's or an answer's outcome has a person leave ({lane})";
    "{where}: leaves {lane}, which is not a declared ruled lane"; "{where}: leaves {lane}, stopping {id}, which is
    not a standing action"; "lane {lane}: left by two outcomes" (two Leaves of one lane, in one outcome or two);
    LEFT in Outcome.unlocks or Rules.opening as JOINED is (the message names the kind).
  - Loader (ValueError, where as the other refusals name it: "trigger {id}", "answer {id}", "card {id}"): "{where}:
    only a trigger's or an answer's outcome has a person leave"; the two above (the second also for a lane named
    twice in one outcome); "{where}: leaves {lane}, stopping {id}, which is not a standing action"; and a join of the
    lane in the outcome that leaves it, "{where}: joins {lane} in the outcome that leaves it": the engine lands it
    as nothing (leaves come before joins), so content naming both is a mistake. A join of the lane in a later
    outcome is not checked (KNOWN GAPS, the first gap).

INVARIANTS (leaves; numbered on from 147. Invariant 27 now reads "... except one on ANSWERED, ANSWER, JOINED or LEFT,
which is true only once the save holds it"; invariant 49 now reads, for mixes: "mixes equal those of s less the
shares a leave landing at the rebase instant removes".)
148. Others untouched: a leave of L landing at T changes no saved share or order of any other lane. On rules
    where no store that a card on L makes or takes is made or taken by anything else, every Explanation of every
    line not on L at T, after the outcome, equals its Explanation just before T. (Where L's cards made a store others
    take, the others see that store's change by the dry rule and SPENT, as for any maker gone out of play.)
149. No mix greys: no saved mix is greyed at T or later because of the leave; a mix greyed before T stays greyed.
150. Orders gone and logged: from T, save.orders holds no order whose verb is a TapCard of a card on L; the rest keep
    their order and latches; the span's rule_changes hold exactly one RuleChange(T, o, False, level, Ended.LEFT) per
    distinct order removed.
151. Pays nothing mid-task: a tap on L running at T gives no push line, no outcome and no completion at T or later,
    the card's completions stay as at T, and no store moves by that card after T.
152. Out of play: from T every card on L is retired (a player's TapCard raises InvalidVerbError, an order's or an
    Outcome's start changes nothing), is in neither View.cards nor View.completed, and no saved mix holds it; each
    standing action in the Leave's stops contributes 0 to every rate and is absent from View.standing, and its
    Unlocked(ACTION) condition stays true.
153. Once and for good: Unlock(LEFT, L) enters only through Outcome.leaves and never leaves; a second leave of L, a
    join of L, and an install or start on L at T or later change nothing.
154. Rebase: for s past the moment whose trigger holds a Leave of L, rebasing s into rules that add that trigger gives
    the save 148-152 describe, at the save's time, and it loads (decode, advance, view) without error.
155. Codec and validation: decode(encode(s, r), r) == s for s holding LEFT; rules with no leave keep their digest; a
    Leave of a lane that is not a declared ruled lane, a stops id that is not a declared standing action, two Leaves
    of one lane anywhere in the rules, a Leave outside a trigger's or answer's outcome, or LEFT in Outcome.unlocks or
    Rules.opening raises InvalidRulesError.

REJECTED, AND WHY (leaves)
- A lane left idle (the person gone, the lane kept with its rules and shares): a person-shaped hole in the Rules tab
  and the town strip, with no reason on it.
- Greying the town's mix on a retirement (as a hands mix does): every lane stops for one person, until a visit. Chosen
  instead: the retired card's own share stops (Uncapped only).
- Moving the leaver's shares to another person: a person's shares are their own time (THE TOWN'S MIX, "Town share").
- A lane-less stand-in (the person kept as a card with no lane): a person the player never set to work, which content
  cannot always use.
- Paying the task in progress: the person is gone at that instant, and a completion landing for them afterwards is a
  line about someone who left.
- RETIRED unlocks written for each of the lane's cards: one fact (LEFT) already says it; several writers of one set
  for the same thing, and the cards would show in the Completed tab.
- A RETIRED-style unlock per stopped standing action: a new meaning for an id kind (RETIRED ids are cards), renamed
  by another kind at rebase, for what the rules' Leave and LEFT already say.
- Keeping the leaver's shares in saved mixes, skipped when flattened: SaveMix would refuse the player's own mix when
  they next edit it, and the saved split would say something the game no longer does.

KNOWN GAPS (leaves)
- A trigger or offer that taps or installs on a lane after its person left changes nothing; content checks nothing
  for it yet. Decided by: the first chapter whose loader run trips on one.
- Fights on a left lane's cards: their tallies stop with the cards; whole losses that are people stay FIGHTS' gap.
- A town mix the player named in a save from before Mix.layer, if the player empties it with a SaveMix (a leave sets
  the layer), reads as HANDS: nothing had set its layer. Decided by: a save that does it.
