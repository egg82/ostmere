SPELLS (a working that resolves a chain on a group threat: docs/design/week-five-plan.md, item 6; blind tests from this
part and api/contract/spells.txt alone, item 7, against app/tests/engine_contract/spells/MUTANTS_spells.txt). Where this
part and an entry above disagree, this part holds. Examples name ids only; which working, which threat and how far is
content's.

FIT PASS
- A working is a card (Cards: the player taps, mixes, queues or rules it like any card); what it gains is one more
  Component on CardDef, ChainDef, as FIGHTS added FightDef (Nystrom, Component). The chain's fights belong to a standing
  fight that already runs (Combat): the chain reads its tallies and adds to one, and changes nothing else about it.
- Fights are flows, never events (FIGHTS, FIT PASS), so a standing fight has no queue of fights waiting to be fought.
  What it does have, exactly, is its tallies. A threat the content marks as a group (ThreatDef.members >= 2: several
  members, fought one after another) gives "due" an exact meaning from them: the members of the group now being fought
  that are still to be fought. No new state: the tallies are stores the save already holds.
- A chain is a cascade of same-instant firings under the firing cap (docs/systems.md, Spells row; CHOICES "Firing cap"):
  each link is one firing at the working's completion, so a chain is bounded by the cap as every cascade is, and it
  costs no instant of its own (the completion is already one).
- Reach is a third based value with sources, the Store caps' shape (as SideStrength and ThreatStrength are): a base the
  content sets and Modifier entries aimed at it, so a level's pay ("one more") is an outcome's modifier and "why is it
  that number" is explain(). Nothing reads chance: the length is a minimum of three integers the save already gives.
- How games do it: a chain spell (Diablo's chain lightning, Path of Exile's chain count, Slay the Spire's multi-hit
  cards) has a stated count of targets it reaches, shown before the cast, and stops early when the targets run out.
  Here the targets are the group's due members, and "stops early" is min(due, reach).
- What is not new: no Save field, no event kind, no verb, no system. One Component (ChainDef), one Modifier target
  (ChainReach), two authored fields (ThreatDef.members, FightDef.chained).

CHOICES (spells)
- Shapes (exported from the engine package):
  - ThreatDef gains members: int = 1 (>= 1). members >= 2 marks the threat as a group. Nothing else reads it.
  - FightDef gains chained: StoreId | None = None: the tally of this runner's fights that chains resolved, one unit per
    link. Allowed only on a standing fight whose threat is a group; required there when some card's chain names the
    runner.
  - ChainDef(runner: ActionId, reach: int, lines: tuple[LineKey, ...]), a frozen dataclass in engine/cards/cards.py.
    CardDef gains chain: ChainDef | None = None. runner: the standing fight the chain resolves fights of. reach: the
    base reach (>= 1), set by the content from the input the working takes. lines: the link lines, at least one.
  - ChainReach(card: CardId), a Target (Modifiers): the reach of that card's chain.
- Due. For a standing fight R whose FightDef f has a group threat of m members and a fought tally, at instant t:
  F = floor(fought(t) / QUANTITY_SCALE), C = floor(chained(t) / QUANTITY_SCALE) (store values at t, as View.fights
  floors them). R is fighting at t when its fought tally's segment rate at t is above 0 (store_segment: unlocked, not
  stopped by a leave, not paused, not at the tally's cap). Then
      due(R, t) = m - ((F + C) mod m)   if R is fighting at t,   else 0.
  So 1 <= due <= m while R fights: the members of the group now under way, the one being fought included. A fight the
  flow fought, won or lost, counts once in F; a link counts once in C; so the group boundary is where F + C crosses a
  multiple of m, and a chain of `due` links ends its group exactly.
- Reach. reach(card, t) = ChainReach(card)'s value over base_stack at t (the save's modifiers, the shown chips', the held
  entries, the picks'), the Store caps arithmetic: base * max(0, BP + sum(add_bp)) * prod(mul_bp) // BP ** (1 + #muls),
  rounded down once, base = ChainDef.reach. Entries aimed at a ChainReach only raise (add_bp >= 0, mul_bp >= BP), so
  reach >= base >= 1. explain over it is a StrengthExplanation whose target is the ChainReach. Read live, at the
  instant it is used, as a standing fight's odds are: it only rises, so the reach a completion runs is never below the
  reach the card showed when the tap started, and at the completion's instant the reach shown is the reach run. "At
  the completion's instant" is the completion's (kind, sequence) place: a raise from an event sorting after it at the
  same t (a TRIGGER) is read from the next completion on.
- Completion. A card with a chain has a COMPLETION event at every completion, tapped or mixed, capped or not (CardDef:
  "a completion is an event when it has a discrete effect"; the chain is one). For a capped card the completion's own
  parts land first as before (its line, outcome and, at the cap, finished); an uncapped card has none (its tap line is
  still the landing's, at TAP_DONE, after it). Then, in the same handling, the chain resolves.
- Resolving. Let P be the instant's firings before the completion's event is handled (CHOICES "Firing cap"; the
  completion's event is one firing of its own). With the save as it stands after the completion's own parts:
      k = min(due(R, t), reach(card, t), max(0, max_firings_per_instant - P - 1)).
  1. the chained tally gains k * QUANTITY_SCALE, held to its cap (an outcome's store add: one rebuild);
  2. k lines are emitted at t, link i (1 to k) emitting lines[min(i, len(lines)) - 1], each naming the chain's card
     as the line's card, in link order; then any chip on_line the rebuild shows for the first time (as any outcome);
  3. the instant counts k more firings. When they bring its firings to the cap, evaluation is suspended for the rest
     of it and it counts as a cascade, as any instant that reaches the cap does.
  Nothing else moves: R's fought and lost tallies, its odds, its lines and its pauses are as they were; no store of R
  is paid or taken by a link. A link is a won fight: it is in neither lost nor fought. What the working brings it
  brings by its own lines (makes, inputs, outcome), as any card.
  k = 0 (R not fighting, or the cap already reached) emits nothing and changes nothing; the completion is as without a
  chain.
- Same instant. A chain resolves at its card's COMPLETION, in that event's (kind, sequence) place; a second chain
  card completing at the same instant reads the tallies as the first left them, exactly as a completion a moment
  later would: if the first left members due, the second's due is what is left; if the first ended the group, the
  next group is under way and the second finds it whole (m due). No chain alone crosses into the next group.
- Repeating taps. A card with a chain never repeats as a rate (retap.may_repeat is false for it): each landing is an
  event.
- Determinism. k is a function of the save at t and the instant's firing count alone. No draw of any kind: the link
  lines go by link index, never by a bag (rotation.bagged is the chips'), and nothing reads the save's id or a seed.
- Validation (InvalidRulesError):
  - "threat {t}: members must be >= 1".
  - "card {c}: chain names {r}, which is not a standing fight" (r no declared standing action, or one with no FightDef).
  - "card {c}: chain names {r}, whose threat {t} is not a group" (members < 2).
  - "card {c}: chain names {r}, which has no fought or chained tally".
  - "card {c}: chain reach must be >= 1"; "card {c}: a chain needs at least one line".
  - "card {c}: a fight card has no chain" (a CardDef with both).
  - "{where}: chained belongs to a standing fight on a group threat" (on a card's FightDef, or a standing fight on a
    threat with members 1).
  - "{runner}: chained tally store {s} ..." as the fights' tallies: made by no line of any runner (its own included)
    and no outcome, never another fight's lost, fought or chained, and listed once across its runner's stores.
  - Modifiers aimed at a ChainReach: "{where}: a modifier aims at an unknown id" unless the card is declared and has a
    chain; "{where}: a chain's reach only rises" for add_bp < 0 or mul_bp < BP.
- Read model: chain_views(save, rules) -> tuple[ChainView, ...], one per unlocked card with a chain that is neither
  completed nor on a lane whose person has left, in rules.cards order. ChainView(card, runner, reach:
  StrengthExplanation, due: int): reach now (the value the next completion runs if nothing lands before it), and
  due(runner, save.time). FightView gains chained: int, floor(chained / QUANTITY_SCALE) (0 for a runner with none).
  Reading writes nothing.
- Codec: FORMAT unchanged. ChainReach is one more Target tag (a save's modifiers may hold one). ThreatDef.members at 1,
  FightDef.chained at None and CardDef.chain at None are left out of the rules' encoding, so rules that use none keep
  their digest and the saves under them decode as before.
- Rebase: nothing a chain adds is in the save but the chained tally's store and ChainReach entries, which the store and
  modifier rules already carry. A chain added to a released card resolves from its next completion; past completions
  are not replayed (a released card's lines are not replayed either).
- Answers to the blind tests' questions (spells):
  - The fixture shape: a standing action with FightDef(side, threat, lost, fought, per_hour, chained) on a ThreatDef
    with members m >= 2, unlocked; a hands card with ChainDef(runner, reach, lines). A test reaches a known due by
    advancing to a time where floor(fought) is known (per_hour a whole number of fights an hour at full speed: the
    fought tally is exact at whole hours) and C is 0.
  - Odds do not matter to due: lost fights are fought fights. A side of strength 0 (every fight lost) gives the same
    due as a side that wins every fight.
  - A link's line is in the logged lines (advance_logged, decide_logged) at the completion's time, with the chain's
    card, k of them, in link order, after the completion's own line (a capped card's) and before the TAP_DONE line (an
    uncapped tapped card's).
  - The firing room counts the completion's own event: with max_firings_per_instant = c and the completion the first
    event of its instant, k <= c - 1, and k = c - 1 makes the instant a cascade.

INVARIANTS (spells; numbered on from 183)
184. Length: at a completion of a card with a chain on runner R at t, the links emitted number exactly
     min(due(R, t), reach(card, t), max(0, max_firings_per_instant - P - 1)), and R's chained tally rises by exactly that
     many units (less only at its cap).
185. Never past due: over any span, between two instants where F + C is a multiple of m, the links of all chains on R
     number at most m; a chain never makes F + C cross a multiple of m.
186. Only a group: no rules that validate have a chain whose runner's threat has members < 2.
187. Untouched: a chain changes no store but its runner's chained tally (and what the completion itself pays); R's
     fought, lost, odds and pauses equal those of the same save with the chain's links removed.
188. Shown is run: at the completion's instant, the ChainView reach of the card equals reach(card, t) used by rule 184;
     a tap started at t0 runs a reach >= the reach its ChainView showed at t0.
189. No chance: the links, their lines and the save after them depend only on the rules, the save before the instant
     and the instant's firing count; two saves equal but for anything else (an id, a seed, the wall clock) give equal
     results. Timing does not matter (invariant 81 holds for chains: one advance or any slicing gives equal saves and
     equal lines).
190. One line per link: k links emit k lines, never fewer; lines of one chain are never merged into one key or one
     line.
191. Codec: rules with no chain, no chained tally and every threat's members 1 keep their digest; decode(encode(s, r), r)
     == s for a save holding ChainReach entries.

REJECTED, AND WHY (spells)
- A timed bonus on the side (the working raising SideStrength for a while): no chain, nothing runs, and it reads as a
  potion (week-five-plan item 6, Rejected).
- A random chain length, or a chain whose links are each won by a roll: chance would move how far (decisions.md,
  "Chance decides how nice, never how far").
- An event per fight in the engine, so a chain consumes queued fight events: FIGHTS' REJECTED (an instant per fight,
  bag state in the save).
- Due as the fights a flow would fight in the next hour, or since the last cast: it moves with speed and with when the
  player looked, and is not exact against the tallies.
- Links counted into fought: fought is the flow's integral (invariant 84), and a jump in it would break that and fold
  links into the page's fight window, which the page tells by a shuffle bag; a link is told by its own line.
- Paying the runner's makes per link: a standing fight's makes are per hour, not per fight, so a per-link pay would be a
  division the content never wrote; the working's own lines carry what casting it brings.
- A chain bounded only by reach, ignoring the cap: an unbounded cascade (the cap is what makes every cascade safe).
- Reach as a per-level formula on the card's own skill: the pay that raises it may be another skill's level row, and a
  Modifier target already gives sources and explain().
- A chain on any threat, ending at the reach: the content contract says a chain longer than the pack ends at the pack;
  members is the explicit field that says where it ends.

KNOWN GAPS (spells)
- A chain on a fight card (the player's own hunt on a group): due is defined for a standing fight only. Decided by: the
  first fight card on a group threat a working should reach.
- A chained tally at its cap stops counting, and due with it (as FIGHTS' tallies). Content sets its cap at least a year
  of links.
- More than one rule of magic combined (Spells row: rules as data combined by a small interpreter): this part resolves
  one chain; the interpreter stays the loader's (week-five-plan item 5). Decided by: the second group threat.
