CUT-IN (a glowing step tapped while the queue or a rule holds the hands). Where this part and an entry above disagree,
this part holds.

FIT PASS
- An instance of Lanes and the player's tap (Command: TapCard is unchanged) plus the existing interrupt (a tap ends
  where it stands, its work kept). No new state, verb or event: a cut-in is a player's verb, so the forecast (which
  assumes no player) and everything that runs while the player is away are unchanged.
- The page's glow already marks the one obvious next step (CardDef.glows, from the content's `glows`). The defect: a
  short visit finds that step ready while the hands run a queued build or a rule's task, so the step waits a whole
  absence for hands the player's own plan holds.

CHOICES
- decisions.cuts_in(save, rules, card): card glows, is on HANDS, has 0 completions and would start were the hands
  free (ready, speed above 0), and the hands run another card whose task holds (decisions.holds: were the hands free,
  the controlling verb would tap it again). The player's TapCard of such a card interrupts the hands' task
  (work.interrupt) and taps the card; no evaluation follows a player's tap, so the task does not retake the hands.
  When the step lands, evaluation taps the held task again from its kept work. CardView.cuts_in serves it, so the
  page draws the card tappable, with the glow, on a busy lane.
- A glowing step never cuts into a hand tap: the player chose that tap. A cure does (CURE CUT-IN), and the hand tap
  goes to the queue's front.

INVARIANTS (cut-in; numbered on from 108)
108. A player's TapCard of a card with cuts_in True does not raise for the busy lane, leaves the interrupted card's
    work as it stood, and once the step lands the controlling verb taps the interrupted card again. With cuts_in False
    on a busy lane the tap raises InvalidVerbError (invariant 20).

REJECTED, AND WHY (cut-in)
- The queue holding its next start while a glowing step is ready: it helps only a player still on the page when the
  hands free, and an absent player's queued plan stalls for every hour a step sits ready (one never tapped stalls it
  for good, with nothing on screen saying why). Played on the week-one routes it stalled a queue through a night's
  absence and moved one route's B7 hours later.
- Cutting into any task, the player's own taps included: the interrupted tap would sit half done with nothing to take
  it up again.
