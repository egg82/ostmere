SAVES ACROSS RELEASES (FORMAT 4, rebase). Where this part and an entry above disagree, this part
holds.

FIT PASS
- An instance of Saves (docs/systems.md). It replaces that row's "a deploy snapshots every save under the old rules",
  which needs the old code running at deploy time, with a rebase at load, which needs only what the save carries.
- How games do it: a version number in the save and a chain of one-step upgrade functions run on the raw data before
  it is loaded (Minecraft's DataFixerUpper); the save names content by id, never by list position. Here the chain is
  UPGRADES, the one place a save is transformed on purpose (a format change, or a content change worth carrying a save
  across), and the manifest is what turns the save's two positional tuples into ids.
- The engine owns the rebase because it is the one place a save crosses rules, beside decode.

CHOICES (saves across releases)
- Manifest: encode writes, beside FORMAT and the digest, the slice of the rules a later rebase needs, taken from the
  rules it encodes under: stores (id, StoreDef.cap), skills, standing actions, lanes besides HANDS, cards (id, work_s,
  lane, cap), chips and triggers in rules order, and the feature vocabulary (sorted). Plain JSON, untagged, so an
  upgrade edits it as data. Save.chips[i] is the latch of the manifest's chip i, Save.chips_seen[i] whether it has
  shown and how many off lines it has said, and Save.fired[i] the state of its trigger i; every other tuple in the save already names its ids. decode is unchanged in kind: the manifest is part of
  the text it re-encodes and compares. Data, not a flag per change (docs/decisions.md, "Developed live": no machinery).
- FORMAT is 10 (chips_seen counts; 9 Mix without holds, 8 added LaneState.speed, 7 the manifest's offers, 6 took the failed attempt off
  LaneState, 5 added Save.chips_seen, 4 the manifest). OLDEST_FORMAT is 4, the first format a rebase reads. UPGRADES[f] (engine/upgrade.py)
  turns an envelope of format f into one of format f + 1, as JSON, before anything is decoded; a rebase chains them
  from the text's format up to FORMAT. Every format from OLDEST_FORMAT below FORMAT has an entry (a test and the release
  lock both check it). A text of a format below OLDEST_FORMAT or above FORMAT raises StateFormatError: FORMAT 3 texts
  (the preview's tabs before saves) are never rebased, and the service answers them rules_changed one last time.
  UPGRADES[4] adds chips_seen, True for a chip latched or in the save's unlock set: a format 4 save cannot tell a chip
  that showed and cleared from one that never showed, so no on_line plays twice, at the cost of one never playing.
  UPGRADES[5] drops failed and failed_timer from each lane and frees a lane that held a failed attempt; its line,
  still to land, is not said. UPGRADES[8] (FORMAT 9) drops holds from every saved mix (docs/decisions.md, "No hold on a
  line"), so a mix that held a card runs it at its full share; a logged SaveMix whose mix carries an empty holds
  reads back as the same verb without it (verb_from_json), so old decision logs still replay. UPGRADES[9] (FORMAT 10)
  turns each chips_seen flag into a count, 1 for a chip seen and 0 for one not: its off lines start their turns
  from the first.
- Rebase: rebase(text, rules) -> Save, pure (engine/rebase.py); rebase_logged(text, rules) -> Step, its save that same
  Save, its lines those of step 6, all at the save's time, seq from 0, and Step.dropped how many ids of each kind step 3
  found gone (IdKind order, kinds with none left out). rebase, rebase_logged, IdKind, UPGRADES and OLDEST_FORMAT are
  exported by the engine package. When the text's format is FORMAT and its digest is rules_digest(rules), the result is
  decode(text, rules) with no lines and nothing dropped: the save is already under these rules. Otherwise, in order:
  1. upgrade the envelope to FORMAT (above);
  2. decode it loosely: an envelope of exactly format, rules, manifest and save; a well-formed manifest; a Save; the
     text exactly the canonical encoding of that envelope; len(save.chips), len(save.chips_seen) and len(save.fired)
     equal to the manifest's chips and triggers. Anything else raises StateFormatError. The digest is not checked;
  3. find the gone ids: each id the manifest declares (IdKind: stores, skills, standing actions, cards, lanes, chips,
     triggers, features, offers, answers) that the new rules do not, and each answer the new rules put under another
     offer than the manifest did;
  4. drop what names one (engine/dropping.py): an order or queue entry whose condition names a gone id (its latch with
     it), an action naming one (TapCard, AnswerOffer; the order goes when no action is left), an AnswerKind whose kind
     and role no offer of the new rules has, a modifier aimed at one (RateRef runner or store, WorkRef, LaneSpeed,
     StoreCap; the save's and each tap's kept speed entries), a pause of or on one, a mix's share of a gone card (the
     mix stays, its id the player's), a lane's tap of a gone card (the lane freed) and an unlock of one. A runner is
     gone by the kind the manifest declares it. What no manifest declares stays as it is: an order naming a card no
     rules have ("Orders"), a modifier's source (a label), a journal entry or note unlock, a prestige pick (picks count
     the phase's clicks; one on a node the rules lack holds nothing);
  5. build the canonical save under the new rules, at the same time: stores in rules order, each at its value, a new
     store at its initial value, a value above its store's cap under the save's stack (a lowered cap) clamped to it;
     skills in rules order, XP held at or under the phase's ceiling, a new skill at XP 0; chips, chips_seen and fired in
     rules order by id, a new chip unlatched and unseen and a new trigger unfired; lanes HANDS then rules.lanes, a new
     lane free, and a lane holding a tapped card whose CardDef.lane is now another lane freed, as an interrupt: its work
     is kept and nothing lands; every CardState of a card the rules declare kept (raised included), and when the card's
     work_s changed from W to W' its work rescaled with c = work // (W * BP) and rem = work % (W * BP) to c * W' * BP +
     rem * W' // W, so completions are kept and the bar stays at its fraction, rounded down; pauses in runner order;
     the unlock set sorted; mixes, orders, latches, queue, active, modifiers, cascade_count, last_cascade and ended_at
     kept as step 4 left them;
  6. rebuild at that time, then resolve the instant with evaluation (orders and the queue evaluate, as after a
     landing): a new trigger already true fires and a new chip already true latches at the save's time and their lines
     are emitted ("new content just appears"); a trigger fired in the old save stays fired, so no outcome lands twice;
  7. the caller encodes the result under the new rules (new digest, new manifest).
  Only a StateFormatError fails a rebase (a text no encode gave: corruption), and it returns nothing, so a failed
  rebase is never written. A save never fails to load because content moved.
- Forward-only: what the save had at its last write stands; the time away after it runs under the new rules (the first
  advance after a rebase). A change meant to be retroactive, or one that should carry a save's progress across a
  rename, is a step on UPGRADES (run once on the envelope at load), never a flag on saves.
- No release lock and no renames: content may rename, remove or rewrite any id or outcome; a renamed id is a new one,
  and the save's progress under the old id is dropped. Every FORMAT bump needs an UPGRADES entry
  (tests/engine/test_rebase_impl.py). The release number that orders builds for the server's saves is the CI run
  number: api/CONTRACT.txt, "Saves across releases and pods".

INVARIANTS (saves across releases; numbered on from 46. r, r2, r3 valid rules; s a save the engine returned under r;
"kept" ids are those both rules declare, "gone" ids those step 3 finds)
47. Identity: rebase(encode(s, r), r) == s, and rebase_logged's lines and dropped are empty.
48. Idempotent: for s2 = rebase(x, r2), rebase(encode(s2, r2), r2) == s2.
49. Kept ids keep what was earned, for s2 = rebase(encode(s, r), r2): s2.time == s.time; every kept store holds its
    value in s, or its cap in r2 under s2's stack when that is lower; every kept skill's XP is its XP in s, or the
    ceiling of its phase in r2 when that is lower; every kept card's completions and raised equal those in s; s2.unlocked
    holds every unlock of s naming no gone id; every kept trigger fired in s is fired in s2; ended_at equals that of s;
    s2.mixes are s.mixes without shares of gone cards; s2.orders is s.orders as step 4 left them, plus only what a
    RuleInstall landing at the rebase instant adds (invariant 59); and s2.modifiers begins with s.modifiers aimed at
    no gone id (latches, the queue and active are as resolving the instant under r2 leaves them).
50. New content defaults: in s2, a store new in r2 holds its initial value and a skill new in r2 XP 0, unless an
    outcome landing at the rebase instant adds to it; a trigger new in r2 whose condition holds in s2 is fired (its
    outcome landed once, at s.time); a chip new in r2 whose condition holds in s2 is latched.
51. Rescaled work: a kept card whose work_s changed from W to W' has work c * W' * BP + rem * W' // W in s2 (c and rem
    from its work in s), so CardCount is unchanged.
52. Moved lanes: a lane in s whose card's lane differs in r2 is free in s2, and that card's work is kept (rescaled as
    51).
53. Gone ids: no gone id is named anywhere in s2's orders, queue, mixes, pauses, lanes, modifiers' targets or unlock
    set, and rebase_logged counts each under its kind; the rebase never fails for one.
54. Composition: for r -> r2 and r2 -> r3, rebase(encode(s, r), r3) succeeds as rebasing through r2 does, and 49
    holds between s and the result for ids all three declare. The two results need not be equal (the instant
    through r2 resolves under r2, and rescaled bars round twice).
55. Logged: rebase_logged(x, r2).save == rebase(x, r2); every line's and every rule change's time is the save's time
    (rule changes: TOWNSFOLK, INSTALLED RULES AND OFFERS, "Rebase").
56. Formats: UPGRADES has an entry for every format from OLDEST_FORMAT below FORMAT; a text of another format outside
    [OLDEST_FORMAT, FORMAT], or one that is not some envelope's canonical text, raises StateFormatError.

REJECTED, AND WHY (saves across releases)
- A deploy job that snapshots every save under the old rules (the systems row as it stood): it needs the old code at
  deploy time and touches every save at once; the rebase needs only the text, at the save's next load.
- Per-change flags or start dates on saves: docs/decisions.md, "Developed live" (no machinery).
- Failing the rebase on an id the new rules lack, with a table of renames to map it: every content change that moved
  an id needed an entry, and a missed one kept the app from starting. While the game is in alpha a save losing
  progress under a renamed id is accepted ("wait, this is different"); a rename worth carrying a save across is an
  UPGRADES step.
- Resetting fired for a retuned trigger: its outcome would land twice. (A renamed trigger is a new one and fires again.)
- Positional chips and fired with a copy of the old rules in each save: the whole rules tree per save for the few
  facts a rebase reads.
- Work kept as absolute units through a work_s change: a retune would silently change completion counts, granting or
  taking back completions.
- A tapped card keeping the work left in its completion, so its shown time holds across a retune: a lump is paid by
  the bar's fraction (CARDS, "Rate form"), so a longer work_s paid the rest of that completion short (a walk retuned
  7200 -> 14400 s a quarter done paid 0.63 of its item, and the beat waiting on the item never fired). Keeping the time
  as well would mean re-settling every lump store at the rebase, the remainder the rate form exists to avoid.
- Rebasing without evaluation: a new trigger already true would wait for the next instant something else fires, which
  may be days of game time away.
- The full rebase (with evaluation) when the digest is the same: identity would break, since evaluation at load acts
  for the player where a player's tap deliberately does not evaluate.

KNOWN GAPS (saves across releases)
- A card moved off HANDS stays in the saved mixes that hold it (a saved mix is never quietly edited:
  docs/decisions.md, "The mix"), so it runs at its mix share off HANDS until the player edits the mix. Content that moves
  a mixed card off HANDS retires it and adds a new card instead. Decided by: the first release that moves a hands card.
