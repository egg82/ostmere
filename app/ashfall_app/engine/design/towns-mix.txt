THE TOWN'S MIX (people as shares: docs/design/day-three-plan.md, item 3). Where this part and an entry above
disagree, this part holds; it replaces CARDS' "Mixable" and the validation line "Only HANDS cards are mixable until
the town's mix". Examples name ids only; who joins when, and the default tasks, are content's.

FIT PASS
- The town's mix is a Mix (Mixes, docs/systems.md: "one mechanism for both layers: the town's buildings will run
  cards, staff are shares"; docs/decisions.md, "The mix", last line). Nothing about Mix, CardShare, SubMix,
  nesting, flattening, greying, SaveMix or DeleteMix is new; what is new is which time a share is a share of. A
  hands mix's share is basis points of the player's time; a town mix's share is basis points of one person's time,
  the person being the lane the card is on. So the town's mix is one Mix whose cards sit on several people's lanes,
  each lane's shares summing to at most BP, and the whole to at most BP times the people present.
- A person is still a ruled lane (TOWNSFOLK, "Ruled lane"), and the hands lane is already the model for a lane whose
  time a mix runs: a tap on HANDS takes the whole of the player's time and every mix card yields (Lanes). A joined
  person's lane gets exactly that rule: a tap on it (only ever a rule's or an outcome's) takes the whole of that
  person and their shares yield until it lands. So the first person's installed and player-set tap rules keep
  working unchanged after the join, as taps over the mix, which is how "the first person's rules become the town's
  standing orders" holds without converting them.
- Which town mix runs is set the way a ruled lane's task is: by its always rule, Order(AfterTime(0), SwitchMix(m)),
  under the existing Order mix pushdown (CARDS, "Order mix"), read per layer. The town has no player's own mix
  (no save.active for it), as a ruled lane has no player's tap: a new Save field was refused for a lane's default
  task (TOWNSFOLK, Rejected, "A per-lane default task") and is refused again here. Every verb keeps an order: the
  town's switch is an order's, never the player's or the queue's.
- Joining is an Outcome part (Triggers, Cards: "what lands"), landing once like an install, and its fact (this
  lane's person has joined) is an only-growing unlock, as an offer's answer is. A save already past the join under
  new rules gets it at the rebase instant through step 6, the mechanism installs already use.
- How games do it: RimWorld and Dwarf Fortress give each colonist a work-priority table over shared jobs; Banished
  and Township assign people to buildings by count; Idle Awakening's mix is one person's split. The split per person
  over that person's own cards is Idle Awakening's, once per person, edited as one table, which is RimWorld's
  presentation without its pooled jobs (KNOWN GAPS, "Pooled work"). Nystrom: Type Object (a mix is data), Command
  (the join and every switch are verbs or outcome parts in the log), and no new State machine: whether a person runs
  their shares or a tap is the Lanes rule HANDS already follows.

CHOICES (the town's mix)
- Layer: a mix's layer is derived from its flattened cards: HANDS if every card is on HANDS, TOWN if every card is on
  a ruled lane; a mix with no card is TOWN when a join of the rules makes it (RulesIndex.town_mixes) or its
  Mix.layer is TOWN, else HANDS. A mix holding both raises InvalidVerbError at SaveMix; nesting a mix of the other layer
  is the same refusal (checked on the flattened result). plan.mix_layer(mixes, mix) is the one place it is read. Mix.layer
  is set where a mix is emptied: SaveMix of a mix with no component keeps the TOWN the saved mix of that id read, and a
  leave marks each mix it empties that no join made (a verb's own value is replaced; a mix with cards is read from
  them); it is left out of the encoding while HANDS, so no save changes its text and FORMAT stays. Why a field and not the cards alone: read by its cards
  alone, the game's town mix a leave empties (LEAVES, step 4) or a join makes with no share card was HANDS, and the
  town's always rule then took the hands for good (a player's switch set only save.active beneath it); as TOWN, its
  rule runs nothing and the player can still edit it back into use.
- Joined: UnlockKind gains JOINED, id a LaneId. A ruled lane's person is present once Unlock(JOINED, lane) is held;
  present lanes are rules.lanes order filtered by that. Only Outcome.joins adds it (validation refuses it in any
  Outcome.unlocks and in Rules.opening, as ANSWERED), and it is written to save.unlocked whatever rules.opening is,
  as ANSWERED and ANSWER are. Why not a LaneDef flag: who has joined is the story's moment, not the lane's shape (a
  person's lane runs a build before they join), and some ruled lanes are never people (a cure lane whose task an
  installed rule sets).
- Town share: in a TOWN mix a CardShare's share_bp is basis points of the time of the person whose lane the card is
  on. SaveMix of a TOWN mix refuses (InvalidVerbError): a card on a lane not joined, a card with a cap, and any lane
  whose flattened shares sum above BP. A HANDS mix is checked as before (sum <= BP). The engine never moves a share
  between people, nor between cards (Shares: idle is shown, never redistributed).
- Uncapped only: a TOWN mix holds no capped card (refused above), so only retirement takes a card out of it, and a
  retired card stops only its own share: the mix is never greyed (counts.greyed reads a mix's HANDS cards only), the
  card's Run is GREYED (0/1, as in a greyed hands mix), its share idles for that person and is shown, never handed to
  another person or card (Town share), and the mix keeps running for everyone else. A HANDS mix holding a completed
  or retired card still greys whole, unchanged (Greyed mix). A person's one-off is their lane's rule tap (the day-two idiom: an installed first=True always rule),
  which yields their shares while it runs and passes over once done. Why: a town mix greyed by a finished build
  would idle every person in it until a visit, the 2 AM test's failure, for work a tap rule already does; and the
  hands' grey-whole exists to send the player to the editor and falls through to the queue or orders, where the
  town has nothing to fall to.
- Running town mix: plan.running_town_mix_id(save, rules) is the mix of the first order, in sequence, whose latch is
  true, whose verb is a SwitchMix of a TOWN mix (a town mix is never greyed); else None (nobody runs shares). The
  hands' running mix (Order mix) reads only SwitchMix orders of HANDS mixes, then the queue mix, then save.active; so orders of one
  layer never change the other's running mix, and order sequence is priority within a layer only. Everything Order
  mix says of the hands' pushdown holds for the town's: a pure function of latches and greying, rates rebuilt and
  pauses cleared (Running dry) when either running mix changes, a latch change past the firing cap waits (Firing cap).
- Verbs: a player's SwitchMix of a TOWN mix raises InvalidVerbError ("set by rules"), as a player's TapCard on a
  ruled lane does; SetQueue refuses an entry whose verb is a SwitchMix of a TOWN mix (the queue is the hands' plan);
  SetOrders accepts one (it is the town's rule) when the mix is in save.mixes, as for any SwitchMix, so a town mix's
  rule can be set only after its join has created the mix. save.active never names a TOWN mix: DeleteMix and SaveMix
  are as before, and the only writer of active is the player's HANDS switch. A player's SaveMix of the
  running town mix applies at once (rates rebuilt, pauses cleared), as SaveMix of the running hands mix does.
- Reserved ids: a MixId beginning "town:" is content's. SaveMix refuses (InvalidVerbError) one not already in
  save.mixes, so only a join creates it; once created the player edits it as any mix. SaveMix under the id of a saved
  mix keeps that mix's layer (InvalidVerbError otherwise; plan.mix_layer of the saved and of the new). Why: mix ids are
  the player's own text, never renamed, so a join's id must be one no player can already hold; and a name the player
  types for a hands mix (a rename, a save as new) must never overwrite a town mix, nor a town edit a hands mix, which
  would move the rules switching it into the other layer.
- Rates on a joined lane L: while L holds no tap, each card of the flattened running town mix that is on L runs at
  its share, as a hands mix card does on HANDS (Rate form, dry pauses, L's
  LaneSpeed joins its stack, its lumps paid as rates). While L holds a tap, every running town card on L is YIELDED
  (0/1, share kept and shown) and runs again when the tap lands; cards of the same mix on other lanes are not
  affected. A tap on L is started only by an order, an Outcome's start, or nothing (the player never taps a ruled
  lane), and evaluation reads L as free whenever it holds no tap, whatever its shares run (as HANDS is free while
  its mix runs). A card of a TOWN mix on a lane not joined, or unlocked-not, runs nothing (its share idles).
- Dry pauses: TOWN mix cards are mix consumers. A dry store pauses the hands mix's consumers last-in-mix first (as
  before), then the running town mix's last-in-mix first, then other lanes' taps (rules order, as before). Any fixed
  order would do (the hour of hysteresis prevents chatter); this one leaves the hands' pause order exactly as today,
  so no day-one or day-two route moves.
- No XP: a card on a ruled lane carries no skill and no XP line; validation (InvalidRulesError) now refuses it in the
  engine, not only in the loader (docs/decisions.md, "You and the town": townsfolk do not level). Camp make flows
  (CampEntry) are unchanged.
- Join: Outcome.joins, a tuple of Join(lane: LaneId, mix: MixId, card: CardId | None = None). Allowed only in a
  TriggerDef's and an AnswerDef's outcome (once-only, as installs). It lands after `orders` and before `queues`.
  Each join, in tuple order, when Unlock(JOINED, lane) is not yet held:
  1. add Unlock(JOINED, lane);
  2. if save.mixes holds no mix `mix`, append Mix(mix, ()) and, unless save.orders already holds an equal order,
     append the town's always rule Order(AfterTime(0), SwitchMix(mix)) with its latch latched now (as an install);
     append, never insert: a town rule set by the player above it keeps priority;
  3. the share card: the card of the first order on L that is an always rule (AfterTime(0), TapCard) of an uncapped
     card not retired, else `card`, else none;
  4. remove from save.orders (with their latches) every always rule on L whose card is uncapped; always rules of
     capped cards stay (a pending one-off still acts, as a tap yielding the shares; a finished one stays passed over
     as before), and every other rule on L stays where it is;
  5. if there is a share card and mix `mix` holds no CardShare of a card on L, append CardShare(card, BP).
  A join of a lane already joined changes nothing. Orders and the queue evaluate after the outcome, as always. A
  running tap on L at that instant is not cut: with its always rule gone a repeating tap is bounded and lands at its
  next completion (REPEATING TAPS), and L's share yields until then. MAX_MIXES is checked by SaveMix only: a join may
  take save.mixes past it.
- Why the uncapped always rule becomes the share: after the join an always rule on L would tap L at every landing and
  yield L's shares for good; and a repeating card run by its tap and the same card at BP in a mix pay the same
  (invariant 93), so the conversion keeps what L does. A card with inputs or a tap line is not repeat-eligible
  (invariant 78): it goes from a per-completion draw to a rate draw at the join. Accepted: same totals, smoother.
- Lane rules (TOWNSFOLK): LaneRules(L) counts as before (orders on L whose when is not AfterTime(0)); a join removes
  only always rules, so it never changes the count.
- Lane order: LaneBar.order is unchanged (a tap's rule, else None); for shares the view names town_mix_order.
- Read model (view.py): View gains running_town_mix: MixId | None (plan.running_town_mix_id) and town_mix_order: int
  | None (the index into save.orders of the order whose town mix runs; None when none does). CardView for a card of
  the running town mix carries its share and run state exactly as a hands mix card's (by MIX, or YIELDED while its
  lane taps). greyed_mixes lists greyed HANDS mixes only; a retired card of a town mix leaves View.cards and its person's share shows as idle. runs_out_at stays the hands' running mix only. Coverage
  (all_covered) counts a running town card as a running supplier, as a running hands card.
- Codec: FORMAT unchanged. Save's fields are unchanged (app/tests/held/save_fields.json); a TOWN mix is a Mix in
  save.mixes; JOINED is one more UnlockKind value; Outcome gains `joins` (rules and released outcomes only, never a
  save); Join joins the codec's classes. A save written before this part holds no JOINED and no TOWN mix, so it
  decodes as before.
- Rebase: step 4 drops Unlock(JOINED) of a gone lane. Mix ids are never dropped ("town:" ids included). Step 6
  lands a join whose trigger is already true at the rebase instant, as an install: a save past the moment gets
  Unlock(JOINED), the mix and its rule, and its first uncapped always rule as the share, keeping every other rule.
- Content (the loader): OutcomeEntry gains `joins`, a list of {lane, mix, card (optional)}, refused outside a
  trigger's or an answer's outcome; `mix` begins "town:". The loader's own XP check on ruled-lane cards stays as a
  clearer message ahead of the engine's.
- The arrivals count (a gate's `arrivals: n`): a StoreEntry with `tally = true` that outcomes add to and gates read as
  an ordinary at_least on a store; the engine needs nothing new. A trigger on the first arrival's feature adds 1
  (so a save past that arrival gets 1 at rebase, as any new trigger already true fires), and each joining outcome
  adds one per join to a tally store, except a join marked `counted` (the loader's JoinEntry only), whose person a
  tally counted on arrival (the first arrival's). The loader (opening/tally_checks.py) refuses a card or standing
  action that takes a tally store as a cost or an input, and a joining outcome whose tally additions differ from its
  joins not marked counted.

INVARIANTS (the town's mix; numbered on from 89)
90. Shares: in any save reached by play, for every TOWN mix in save.mixes, its flattened shares name only cards on
    joined lanes, none capped, each lane's sum <= BP, so the total <= BP times the joined count. For any running
    town mix and joined lane L with no tap, each of its cards on L runs at share_bp, and the
    unused remainder of L's BP idles; while L taps, each is YIELDED with its share unchanged.
91. Never trains: no rules with a skill or XP line on a ruled-lane card pass validation, so in any save no skill's
    XP rate depends on a town mix, a join or a ruled lane's tap.
92. A rule on the town fires as on a lane: (a) view.running_town_mix is the mix of the first latched
    SwitchMix order of a TOWN mix, else None; view.running_mix is computed with every TOWN-mix order removed, and
    running_town_mix with every HANDS-mix order removed and save.active ignored. (b) A tap order on a joined lane L
    acts at exactly the instants it would if L ran no shares (L free of taps, card ready, speed above 0), and
    invariant 57 holds with TOWN switches added to the verbs that are never applied.
93. Tap equals share: on a joined lane L, a repeat-eligible card c (invariant 78) run by L's always rule, and c at BP
    in the running town mix with no rule tapping L, give equal stores, card work and card counts at every t, for any
    slicing into advances, views and snapshots.
94. Join keeps the work: for s whose lane L runs a repeat-eligible card c by its first uncapped always rule, a join of
    L landing at T gives at every t >= T, with no decision, the stores, card work and counts of the same save
    without the join; its orders differ only by L's removed uncapped always rules and the town's added always rule
    (absent if an equal one was held), its mixes only by `mix` (created or extended), and its unlock set only by
    Unlock(JOINED, L). The rules on L that are not always rules keep their order and their latches.
95. Once: a Join lands at most once per save (it lives only in once-only outcomes and a joined lane's join changes
    nothing); a town always rule the player removed comes back only through a different join whose mix is absent;
    a join never inserts above an existing order.
96. Validation: InvalidRulesError for a Join whose lane is not a declared ruled lane, whose card is not an uncapped
    card on that lane, whose mix does not begin "town:", or that sits outside a trigger's or answer's outcome; and
    for a JOINED unlock in Outcome.unlocks or Rules.opening. InvalidVerbError for a player's SaveMix of a mix mixing
    layers, naming a card on an unjoined lane or a capped ruled-lane card, or summing a lane above BP; of a new
    "town:" id; of a saved mix's id with cards of the other layer; for a player's SwitchMix of a TOWN mix; and for
    SetQueue with one.
97. Rebase: for s past the moment whose trigger holds a Join of L, rebasing s into rules that add that trigger gives
    the save invariant 94 describes, at the save's time; rebasing a save already holding Unlock(JOINED, L) into rules
    that lack L drops that unlock and changes nothing else about the town.

REJECTED, AND WHY (the town's mix)
- A mix per person (each ruled lane its own mix): "one mix whose shares are people's time"
  (docs/design/day-three-plan.md, item 3) and the Mixes row's staff-as-shares both want one table the player edits
  at once; per-person mixes would need a per-lane
  running mix and so a per-lane always rule to switch each, multiplying rules for one decision.
- A pooled town lane (people times BP of anyone's time over shared town cards): content's cards are per person, and
  pooling loses who is doing what, which the scene and the lines draw. Pooled work is KNOWN GAPS.
- The town's own active mix (save.active for the town, set by a player's switch): a new Save field, and a second way
  to say what the always rule says (the same refusal as a lane's default task).
- A static LaneDef.person flag: changes a released lane (the first person's lane shipped in day two), cannot say
  when someone joins, and a cure lane is ruled without being a person.
- Deriving who is present from the town mix's cards: a person dropped from the split would vanish from the strip
  instead of standing idle, which is the honest picture (Shares: idle is shown).
- Converting the first person's tap rules into SwitchMix orders: it invents a mix per rule; taps that yield are the
  hands' rule and keep the first person's rules working as they were set.
- Taps on a joined lane running beside its shares: a person would work more than one person's time, and "shares sum
  to the people present" would be false.
- Capped cards in a TOWN mix: one finished build greys the whole town mix and idles everyone until a visit; a rule tap
  already does a person's one-off.
- A FORMAT bump: nothing in the codec changes shape; the content release already keeps two builds off one save.
- A new event at the join: a join lands inside an outcome, at an instant that already evaluates.

KNOWN GAPS (the town's mix)
- A person dying by a fight's tally stays FIGHTS' gap ("Whole losses that are people"). A person leaving by an
  outcome is LEAVES, below.
- Pooled work (a building any townsperson can staff, wide expeditions as several people on one trip): a card on no
  one person's lane needs a share unit of "people", not one person's BP. Decided by: the first staffed building or
  the first wide expedition (BANKED TIME, KNOWN GAPS, "Wide expeditions": a trip card on one person's lane at a
  share already works; several people on one trip is this gap).
- An always rule the player writes on a joined lane through the full editor: the engine accepts it and it yields that
  person's shares for good. The page builds none for a joined lane (api/contract/the-towns-mix.txt, "The town's mix"). Decided by:
  the first save seen holding one.
- Joint projects (one card both layers work on, docs/decisions.md, "You and the town"): a card is on one lane.
  Decided by: the first joint project.
