GROUPS (pooled lanes: many people who do the same work under one split; blind tests from this part alone). Where this part and an entry above disagree, this part holds. Examples
name ids only; who is in a group, who keeps their own split and when is content's.

FIT PASS
- A group is a Type Object over people (Nystrom, Type Object): the group owns the cards its members can do and one
  split; a person points at their group. The group never changes for a person in play (LaneDef.group is authored), so
  there is no "can the type change" machinery, and no parent groups: nothing to share yet. What does change, the mark
  (a person keeps their own split or not), is State on the person, not part of their type (Type Object's See Also:
  State).
- It extends Lanes and THE TOWN'S MIX, no new system: a group is a lane of a new kind (pooled), its split is
  CardShares in the town mix that already holds each person's, and the one reader that turns people into runs is
  engine/mixes/mixruns.py _town_entries. It is the share unit of "people" that THE TOWN'S MIX's KNOWN GAPS, "Pooled work",
  asked for.
- Nystrom's near-death attack is the typed object selectively overriding the type's behaviour, which is the mark.

CHOICES (groups)
- The data:
  - LaneDef.pooled: bool = False. A pooled lane is ruled, is never a person, never joins or leaves, never taps, and
    holds only uncapped cards with no XP.
  - LaneDef.group: LaneId | None = None. On a person's ruled lane, the pooled lane that is their group. One value, so
    one group per person. A pooled lane has no group.
  - Join.own_split: bool = True: the person's mark as they join. True keeps every released join meaning what it did.
  - Outcome.marks: tuple[Mark, ...] = (), Mark(lane, own_split: bool, card: CardId | None = None): sets or clears a
    joined person's mark; allowed in a trigger's and an answer's outcome only; lands after `joins`, before `queues`.
    `card`, as Join's, is an uncapped card given BP when the town mix holds no share on the lane it is on: setting the
    mark, a card on the person's own lane; clearing it, a card on their group (as an unmarked join's is).
  - LaneState.own_split: bool = True: the mark itself, on the lane's own state ("not a Save field": the
    PhaseState-on-SkillState precedent in PRESTIGE PHASES). Written only by a join and a Mark.
  - Every field is left out of the codec and the rules digest while at its default. "General" is content, not an
    engine case: content declares a pooled lane for people with no specialty and sets `group` to it.
- How a group's split runs (engine/mixes/mixruns.py, _town_entries):
  - Members of pooled lane G at instant t: person lanes L with LaneDef.group == G, JOINED, not LEFT, LaneState.own_split
    False. Free members: members whose lane holds no tap.
  - A CardShare of a card on G is basis points of EACH member's time. Its card runs at effective share
    share_bp x free_members. Pauses, waiting, spent gains and greying are worked at the per-member share on G as a lane
    (spent.yielded already groups by lane), and the crew factor multiplies last. No members: the share idles and is shown,
    as a share on an unjoined lane is. Members but none free: YIELDED.
  - The multiplier sits on the share, not on Run: Run requires 0 <= num <= den, while modified_rate takes the share as a
    plain factor, so a share above BP is arithmetic the formula already does.
  - Speed: a group card's work and lines read the pooled lane's own LaneSpeed(G); a member's LaneSpeed(L) applies to
    their own cards only. One stack per card, as today: a weighted sum of members' speeds would make every member's
    modifiers an input to another lane's rate.
  - A marked person (own_split True) runs their CardShares on their own lane exactly as today and adds nothing to G. An
    unmarked person's own-lane shares stay in the town mix, idle and kept; re-marked, the split they had comes back. The
    engine never deletes a person's shares on a mark change ("a saved mix never quietly starts doing something else").
  - A tap on a member's own lane (a one-off from a rule or an outcome) takes the whole person: free_members drops by one
    until it lands and the group card keeps running on the rest. This is "a tap yields that person's shares" applied to
    pooled time.
  - Rates change only at instants that already rebuild rates: a join, a leave, a mark (an outcome instant), a tap start
    and a tap landing. No new event kind.
- Join (engine/people/joins.py): a join with own_split False sets LaneState.own_split False, still removes the lane's uncapped
  always rules (they would yield the person for good), and gives the join's share to G instead of L: if the town mix
  holds no share of a card on G, append CardShare(card, BP), `card` being the join's, which must be on G. So the first
  member starts the group at a whole split, and every later member adds output under the split already set.
- Mark (engine/people/marks.py): set LaneState.own_split; when the town mix holds no share on L (setting it True) or on G
  (clearing it), append CardShare(card, BP) if the mark names a card, which is on L or on G respectively. A mark on a lane not joined, or left, changes nothing (as a join of a
  joined lane does).
- Verbs (engine/core/verbs.py, plan checks): SaveMix of a TOWN mix treats G as a lane: G's shares sum to at most BP, no
  capped card. A card on G is accepted whether or not G has members. SetOrders, installs, starts and the queue refuse
  any TapCard of a card on a pooled lane.
- View (engine/read/view.py, viewparts.py): LaneBar gains own_split (person lanes only); CardView of a group card gains crew
  (free_members) beside its share and run, so the page can say "x3" and the ledger's rate is the total.
  While crew is above 0, the card's share_bp and `gained` are the totals over the free members (the card runs at
  them: saved share x crew), and crew is 0 with no free member (members all tapped, or none): then the share is the
  per-member one, a full run is YIELDED with members and the card is absent from the running mix with none. crew is
  None on a card of no pooled lane.
- Validation (InvalidRulesError; one message per rule): "lane {id}: a pooled lane is a ruled lane"; "lane {id}: a
  pooled lane has no group"; "lane {id}: only a ruled lane has a group"; "lane {id}: group {g} is not a pooled lane";
  "card {id}: a card on a pooled lane has no cap" (a pooled card with XP is refused by "a card on a ruled lane trains no
  skill"); "{where}: taps {card}, a card on a pooled lane" for a start, an install or a queue of one; "{where}: leaves
  {lane}, a pooled lane, which is never a person"; "{where}: only a trigger's or an answer's outcome marks a person";
  "{where}: marks {lane}, which is not a declared person's lane"; "{where}: unmarks {lane}, which has no group";
  "{where}: marks {lane} with {card}, which is not an uncapped card on the lane" (on its group, for an unmarking); and "{where}: a join is not valid" for
  a join of a pooled lane, or an unmarked join whose lane has no group or whose card is not an uncapped card on that
  group.
- Codec: FORMAT unchanged. The fields above are left out while default (LaneDef.pooled, LaneDef.group, Join.own_split,
  LaneState.own_split, and Outcome.marks while empty); Mark joins the codec's outcome classes. Save's fields are
  unchanged (app/tests/held/save_fields.json). A save written before this part decodes with every own_split True.
- Save storage: the group's split is CardShares in the town mix (save.mixes); the mark is LaneState.own_split
  (save.lanes). No Save field.
- Rebase: step 6 lands a Mark whose trigger is
  already true at the rebase instant, as it lands joins and installs. A person whose `group` content later changes
  contributes to the new group from the rebase instant. No migration: every existing LaneState decodes with own_split
  True, every released join with own_split True, no released lane is pooled.
- Content (the loader): LaneEntry gains `pooled` and `group`; a pooled lane has a label_key (the group's name on
  screen) and no name_key, group or told ("lane {id}: a pooled lane is ruled and has no name_key, group or told"); a
  group names a pooled lane. JoinEntry gains `own_split`, required on every join (the released joins say `true`): the
  mark is authored per person, never defaulted by arrival order or a count. OutcomeEntry gains `marks`, a list of
  {lane, own_split, card?}, refused outside a trigger's and an answer's outcome. A join with own_split false, or a mark
  clearing it, needs its lane to have a `group`, and the loader adds the group's cards to that outcome's unlocks (unlocks
  only grow, so a second member's copy is a no-op): the loader expanding a lane's cards where it is joined, as town.py
  does for person lanes.
- Who sets and clears the mark: only content, through Join.own_split (as a person arrives) and Outcome.marks on triggers
  and answers (the story's later moments). Never arrival order, never a headcount, never the player.

INVARIANTS (groups; numbered on from 178)
179. Crew equals people: k unmarked members of G with split S give, at every t and any slicing, the same stores and card
    work as k marked people each running S on identical own cards. (The pooled form of 93, "Tap equals share".)
180. A tap on one member lowers G's cards to (k-1)/k and restores them at its landing; no other lane changes.
181. A mark change never removes or changes a CardShare: clearing then setting a mark with no decision between gives
    the save it started from, the latch and lane state aside.
182. Byte identity: a save and rules with no pooled lane, no Mark and every own_split True encode, rebase and advance
    exactly as before this part (proved by the committed saves' hashes, the rules digest and the golden saves).
183. Validation: InvalidRulesError for each rule listed under Validation, and for no valid rules.

REJECTED, AND WHY (groups)
- Job tags on per-person cards (each card names a job; a group's split gives each member's card of that job the share):
  content must author one card per person per job, the copy chore this part removes, and loader-made per-person card ids
  would stay in the content for ever.
- A mix per group (each group its own TOWN Mix and always rule): THE TOWN'S MIX turned down a mix per person for the same
  reason; the town mix stays the one container.
- The mark as an unlock: unlocks only grow, and the mark is set and cleared.
- The mark as the notable tier: notables never die (a promise to the player); the mark is given and taken by the story.
  Fusing them would expose a person to death on losing the mark. The two fields stay separate.
- Ability per person inside a group (an exception list on every group card): ability lives on the group, so a person
  who cannot do a general thing belongs to a group without it.
- Pooling "loses who is doing what" (THE TOWN'S MIX's REJECTED): the scene and lines still know who is in a group and
  what it runs, so each member can be drawn at the group's cards in proportion. Derived for display; no rate needs it.

KNOWN GAPS (groups)
- A marked person's share of a group card (they keep their own split yet do their group's everyday work): not built; a
  marked person runs only the cards written for them. Decided by: the owner's answer.
- A group with several LaneSpeed aims on its members' lanes: a member's speed never reaches a group card. Decided by:
  the first content that aims a LaneSpeed at a person lane.
