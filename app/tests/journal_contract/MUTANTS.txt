Wrong implementations of ashfall_app/saves/journal.py to plant one at a time; the [bracketed] test_journal_<name>.py
modules must go red (HYPOTHESIS_PROFILE=ci where a property needs depth). A kill is a FAILED line naming a test, never
an exception from a malformed plant; a timeout counts as red. The script that plants these is committed beside this
list with the implementation, and results follow the rows. Equivalent mutants close the list, each with why no test
can tell it apart.

The roll and determinism
 1 salted hash   the roll from Python's hash() of the key tuple, or random.Random seeded by it.  [roll, forward]
 2 one draw      the roll ignores `purpose` (one draw serves write, late, misremember and order); or ignores the
                 day, or the save.                                             [roll, late, misremember, rates, order]
 3 bytes         the last eight bytes, little-endian, over 2**64 - 1, or the key joined without "journal" or with
                 another separator.                                                                         [roll]
 4 set order     notes or candidates gathered in a set of subject ids and emitted in its iteration order, so the
                 order depends on PYTHONHASHSEED.                                                 [roll, properties]

 6 counter rng   draws from a running counter or one Random per call, so a new subject shifts every later draw.
                                                                                            [isolation, roll, care]
