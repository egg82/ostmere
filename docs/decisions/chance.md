# Chance

What randomness may decide.

## Chance decides how nice, never how far

Costs, yields and pacing are deterministic. Randomness picks flourishes, cosmetic rarities and which mini-story turns
up; it never moves progress. (RNG that affects progress is mid in an idle game. Same rule as habits.)

**Anything that happens by chance shows itself the moment it's earned**. Incremental games often
unlock a chance effect (a crit, a proc, a rare find) and then leave it to turn up whenever, so the player buys it and
is left guessing whether it works. Here the first draw after an unlock is that thing, on screen at once; after that it
comes at its rate. The shuffle bag makes this free: it already decides which draw is the rare one.
