Ashfall Writing Test

39 short pieces, shuffled. Some are by published human writers and games, some are mine. Rate each by one question: would you want this in Ashfall? 1 is "no", 5 is "exactly this".

You'll probably spot mine by their world, a valley where the sun stopped rising (made up so nothing spoils Ashfall). Rate the writing anyway. The reveal at the end shows the averages by author.
