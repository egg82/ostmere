# Venues for a browser incremental game

Where a browser incremental game can be listed or shared, what each accepts, its rule on AI, and how to put early
builds in front of players. It follows galaxy.click's decline until the game is nearly complete (`galaxy-review.md`).
Sources are in `venues/notes.jsonl`, one row each with its URL and whether it was read in full or only as a snippet.
Evidence is marked in a word: **policy** (the venue's own text), **practitioner**, **opinion**, **snippet** (seen only
in a search result). Venue rules change; each carries what the notes record as its date, and the rest are undated.
As of 2026-10-21.

## 1. Venues

- **galaxy.click** (policy, read in full; undated). Common rejection reasons: "heavy use of generative AI material",
  "being _very_ short", not English, a non-differing mod, paid ads, microtransactions. The page says nothing on
  completeness; the full criteria are in a linked handbook that was not read. Three staff votes decide, and deleted
  games can usually resubmit after fixing. The operator (a post from about January 2025) does not ban AI-made games
  outright, labels them, plans higher acceptance requirements for vibe-coded ones, and says that "over half of the
  time when people very blatantly did use it they say they did not". Commenters favour mandatory disclosure.
- **itch.io** (policy, read in full; undated). Asks creators to tag generative AI accurately; games are listed on an
  AI Assisted browse page. Enforcement is strict for asset pages only, and press reports disagree on game scope
  (November 2024, snippet). Algorithmic game AI and procedural generation need no tag. Draft and Restricted pages are
  shareable and stay out of browse and New Releases. The first public publish puts the page on Most Recent and cannot
  be redone.
- **r/incremental_games** (snippet; reddit could not be fetched). Second-hand: 30 days between new self-posts, posts
  must refer to playable content. Feedback Friday is the sanctioned weekly thread for works in progress (policy,
  snapshot of 2025-12-06). No AI rule found. A claim that a developer's posts vanished after an AI-art Steam removal is
  one commenter's speculation.
- **Steam** (policy, read in full; undated). Early Access needs a playable game, no specific promises about future
  events, and sells the current state. AI use goes in a Content Survey, split into pre-generated and live-generated;
  disclosure is required, not a ban (press summary, January 2024, snippet; the Steamworks page was not read).
- **Poki** (policy, read in full; undated). "AI should empower, not shortcut." Be ready to share tools, prompts and
  iterations; heavy template or AI reliance may be declined; buggy games are declined. Review of 1-2 weeks is
  third-party.
- **CrazyGames** (policy, read in full; undated). Two-step launch, Basic then Full, which must land directly in
  gameplay. No AI rule on the page; a third-party FAQ agrees (snippet).
- **Newgrounds** (opinion, snippet; September 2022): AI art is banned from the Art Portal. No Games Portal policy was
  found. **Kongregate** appears closed to submissions (fan wiki, undated, unverified); no Armor Games rules found.

## 2. Sharing early builds

**Finding: the practice of established incremental developers was not found.** Searches on Antimatter Dimensions,
Trimps, Melvor, Idle Slayer and Synergism histories returned nothing usable. What was found: Kittens Game was online in
2014 and updated continuously, with a subreddit and wiki by 2015; one browser incremental dev opened Discord after a
jam version for live feedback; generic devlog guides stress regular cadence (practitioner, snippets).

## What it means here

Proposals inferred from the above; no source showed them for this game.

- Share early builds on a hidden itch.io Draft or Restricted page and in Feedback Friday, not by publishing the page
  early. Disclose AI use on itch.io and wherever a form asks.
- Resubmit to galaxy.click when the game is near complete; its AI stance makes the human-written text matter.

**Proposal to test:** an itch.io Restricted page plus a Feedback Friday post as the first public build.
