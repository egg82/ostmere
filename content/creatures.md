# Creatures

Principles: `docs/research/creature-design.md`. Premise: `world.md`. Each region asks a different question of the
survivor, by **kind** not size (R1 hunger, R2 stealth and silence, R3 heat and machines, R4 the hollowed and the
Unpaid, R5 cold and the unknowable).

**The three rules that make the bestiary one fauna, not a zoo:**
1. **The Taking**: eat ash-fed things long enough and you take traits from what you ate; eat the dead (heavier in
   sense) and it's fast. Ordinary animals turned wrong by what they ate.
2. **Seam life**: where the burn heats rock, life runs on heat and minerals like vent life, with no sun.
3. **Made things**: Chequer constructs and self-made Unpaid, built for a purpose that outlived its makers. Their
   bodies show the maker's intent, not local pressure.

Each creature is marked **evolved**, **taken** (evolved, bent by the Taking) or **made**. Ordinary animals are
hunted, skinned and fought plainly; nothing is cruel on screen. Taken and made creatures are monsters.

Silhouette line first (one sentence, for the sketch), then the biology.

## Food web, region by region

```
R1  ashroot, lichen, fungus ─► ashrats, ash-deer ─► wolves ─► ash-bear
                         dead (past) ─► crows (tallymen), greycoats
R2  bracket fungus ─► bracket-elk, hush-moths, ash-boar ─► mourners ─► howlet
                fungus ─► pilgrims (fungus moving animal carcasses toward heat)
R3  vent crust ─► crawlers ─► glass-eels, salamanders ─► seam-worm
                keepers (made; eat nothing; seam-heat powered)
R4  archive paper ─► paper wasps ─► city cats ─► (nothing: the apex eats sense)
                clerks ◄─ the Unpaid (feed on memory)
R5  scarp lichen ─► tahr ─► ounces          strays, Leaners, the dune thing: outside the web
```

## The out-of-place rule

One rare per region (above), drawn by the threat schedule at a rate set in code (`rare_threat_rate`), bounded to a
few per save. Each comes from a deeper region and previews it; each is a story moment, not a farm.
