# The first hour, as you'd play it

Two layers. **Screen** is what you do and see, with a running clock [m:ss] and bar lengths. Quoted lines are the log, as the game shows them. Close the app at any point and whatever is mid-bar finishes while you're away.

**[0:00] Screen.** Black, then a ruin in brown light, ash hanging still. No numbers. One button: **Stand up**, a 3 s bar. Each press moves a small figure on the ground a little higher.

> you get an arm under you. it gives.<br>knees under you this time. then not.<br>up. the wall holds you there until the ground stops tilting.

**[0:15] Screen.** **Look around**, 4 s. When it finishes, a pale blue chip, **cold**, appears at the top of the screen and stays.

> walls, mostly. a roof over one corner. grey every way you look, nothing moving in it. your hands have been cold a long while.

**[0:25] Screen.** Four buttons fade in: **Search your coat**, **Gather dead wood**, **Scavenge** (*the ruin, by hand*) and **Clear a spot**. You search your coat, 3 s. A book icon drops into the corner and pulses once; it opens on the torn stubs, then blank pages. At [0:40] you gather dead wood: 5 s an armful, three armfuls, and the counter reads *wood (damp) 3*.

> inside the coat, a book with its first pages gone at the stitching. wrapped with it, a charcoal stick worn short.<br>dead birch, snapped off by hand. wet through, like everything here.

**[1:00] Screen.** **Lay it to dry**, 3 s. The panel splits in two: the wood's own bar on the left (*drying, 3:00*) and your hands on the right. A banner across the split reads **Two things at once: the wood dries while you work.** From then on the split stays, with an empty third slot.

**[1:10] Screen.** Scavenge, 4 s a bar, each one tapped. Scrap and stones trickle in; a stone counter appears beside **Clear a spot**: *stones 0/6*. At [1:50], Clear a spot: two pushes of 3 s.

> under a fallen beam, a bent nail. you keep it.<br>you scrape the grey back to stone with the side of your boot.

**[2:45] Screen.** Six stones in, so **Ring it with stones**, 3 s, and a ring is drawn on the cleared stone. You scavenge on. At [3:10] Scavenging reaches level 3 with a burst, and *flint* lands in the pile.

> a grey stone that sparks against the nail. you carry it back in your fist.

**[3:15] Screen.** **Strike a fire** lights up. The drying bar beside it still shows *0:55*. You strike anyway, 4 s. The bar drains grey and nothing is spent but the seconds. Under the button: *needs dry wood (0:50)*. You fill the wait with three more armfuls of damp wood and a few scavenges.

> you strike at wet wood for a good while. it stays wet.

**[4:10] Screen.** The drying bar settles: *wood (dry) 3*. You strike again. A fire is drawn in the ring, moving, the screen's grade warms a shade, and the **cold** chip fades out.

> it takes. you sit close enough to smell your sleeves.

**[4:20] Screen.** A **ledger** slides up under the bars with one red, falling line: *dry wood 2, out in 20 min*. Under it, in grey, *damp wood 3*. Tap the red line and a card opens with one sentence already filled in: **When dry wood is below 2, lay damp wood to dry.** Tap Set: the line turns white, and the drying slot refills itself.

> fire lit. it eats a stick every ten minutes.

**[4:45] Screen.** **Craft** opens with two recipes, each 3 dry wood, and you have 2. Each card shows its weight:
- **Stool**: rest recovers half again as fast. The open fire keeps eating a stick every 10 min.
- **Fireback** (stacked stone behind the fire): the fire eats a stick every 15 min. Rest stays slow.

**[5:00] Screen.** While the wood dries you scavenge for a second flint, 4 s bars, found at [5:40]. **Knap an edge**: three pushes of 3 s. At [6:00] a new skill bursts open: **Woodcutting**, marked *flint edge*, 5 s a bar. Gathering by hand stays beside it at half the speed.

> you chip a flint until it has an edge. so do two of your fingers.<br>with the edge, a birch comes down in four strokes.

**[6:40] Screen.** An amber chip, **hungry**, and **Forage** (*by hand*): roots, 5 s a bar. Then **Roast them in the coals**, 4 s, and the chip clears.

> roots under the scrub, bitter and full of grit.<br>roasted, they go soft enough to eat. you eat three.

**[7:50] Screen.** Your bars run at half speed and a grey chip says **tired**; **Rest** appears, 10 s. Dry wood reaches 3, and both Craft cards light. You pick the stool, 10 s.

> the fireback can wait. one leg is short. you sit on it anyway.

**[8:20] Screen.** *Stool (wobbly, birch)* lands in your stores, and its icon rocks when you touch it. Rest now fills faster, and the fuel line shows the fire still eating every 10 min. The third slot of the split is now in use: drying, Woodcutting and Rest share your time. Drag the dividers to change the shares, and the ledger beside each says what that does: *dry wood +1 per 6 min*. From [8:30] to [13:00] you work toward the fireback: cut, dry, rest, scavenge, about a tap every 5 s.

> a cup with no handle. it holds water if you hold it level.

**[13:10] Screen.** Dry wood reaches 3 again. **Fireback**, 10 s: stones stacked behind the fire, which is drawn a little brighter, and the fuel line now shows a stick every 15 min. At [13:40] the roots line goes red: *roots 1, gone in 30 min*. Tap it, and the card's sentence fills itself: **When roots are below 3, forage.** Set. Every ledger line is now white.

> stones stacked at the fire's back. its heat comes off them onto your face.

**[14:30] Screen. First leave-it-running moment.** The light moves from brown toward grey. A line appears over the ledger: **Everything here keeps itself going. Come back whenever.** The bars keep moving if you close the app; each one says what it will have done by the time you're back.

> no wind. nothing stirs. far off, a stone rolls, and stops.<br>a fire, a stool, an empty book. it gets darker. the count stays the same.
