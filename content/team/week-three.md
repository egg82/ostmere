## What week three is

The Clear: the first sun in the player's memory, frost, stars, and a valley that looks bigger with the veil thin.
Week two closed the camp; week three makes it a town. The hall gets everyone under one roof and at one table, and the
table shows, as a ledger line, that the Hollow cannot feed what it draws. People come anyway, to the light and the
pot. The answer to the shortfall is the road out, and the road takes one of the people it was cut for.

What the player feels, in order: wonder (the sun), the cold (frost on everything past the warm ground), pride (the
hall), worry (the line falling), a new kind of power that is smaller than it looks (Spellcraft opens on a failure),
a fuller town, something wrong at the table (B16), the size of the world (the far hills), and a loss with no body.

## Decided here: what week two left to week three

**1. A share on a spent pool (D-125).** The bloom runs dry a few hours into the Clear on typical (34.8 left at 336 h,
measured) and about a day in on sharp (204.7). Today its share pauses dry and idles until the bloom refills, which is
the next Drift's turn, three weeks on. Two rules forbid that: a re-tune is only ever set off by something the player
chose, and nothing finishes into idleness; and the babysitter re-tunes at 336 h exactly while the whenever player
re-tunes a day later, which is the 2 AM test failing. The engine never redistributes a share by hand-wave
(`DESIGN.txt`: "a hidden redistribution is a number with no source"), so the rule is a visible one: **a card paused dry
on a store that no unlocked card or standing can make (a pool, or a store whose every maker is itself spent) is spent;
its share goes to the rest of its mix in proportion, each receiving line's explanation naming the spent card as a
source; an outcome that refills the store takes the share back at that instant.** The player's own mix, as saved, is
what runs whenever the season's things exist; when they don't, the time goes where the player already put the rest.
Rejected: leaving it idle (above); greying the mix (a saved mix stopping because a season ended is worse); retiring the
pool's cards (the next Drift brings them back, and a mix holding a retired card greys). Plan item 1.

**2. The Drift's chores (D-126).** The turn to the Clear removes every rule whose task is a cure the Drift's chores
named (`clear_roofs`, `n2_clear_roofs`, `peg_flaps`, `n1_peg_flaps`), whoever wrote it, and the Rules tab's Lately
block says so. The order cards come back by themselves: they are offered while their chip shows and no rule covers it,
and the next Drift's ash shows the chip. Roof ash stands at 16 at 336 h on typical (measured), the chip's threshold, so
B13 also opens `roof_wind`, a standing drain on roof ash (the cold wind off the roofs: the visitor's idiom, drawing its
own pool the turn fills) that empties it within the hour. `wind_torn` sits at 2.8 (measured) under its chip's 12 and
nothing makes it after 336 h: left alone. Plan item 2.

**3. The stores without a sink at night 14.** Smoked goes to the table (B14, a standing), pelt to the hall's doors and
benches and to the frost covers (the Clear's chore). Measured cap loss at night 14: smoked fails on whenever, pelt on
sharp; the check at day fifteen's end is the proof.
