- Canon 2: C-beat-1 ruin.
- Rules 2: clean.
- Overwrought 1: "kept... gave up" is a mild arch flourish.
- Changed N ("kept its walls" is a static description). Free win N. Brighter N. Turn stated N.

## Weakest three
1. **P5** (flint): free win, brighter than planned, and stated; the only pick that fails three Y/N rows.
2. **P6** (feed it): the rule is stated, which is the draft's lens showing through instead of a turn.
3. **P2** (snow): a quip that opens a dread page; the same personifying shape as P1, P5 and P6.

Strongest: **P3**, which changes the situation and leaves the feeling to the reader.

## Swaps
- Swap P6 for candidate 13 ("no one is here to say don't. you sit closer."): the turn is an unstated cost and it is plainer than P6. Risk: "say don't" hints at someone who would, so check it against the must_not on a past self before taking it.
- Candidate 3 ("the sky is up there somewhere. it isn't saying.") serves better than P2: it fits C-world-12 without the quip.
- Candidate 9 is a safe fallback for night (C-world-13) if P8 is cut for creature-hint risk.
- Candidate 14 repeats the "which was the rule" shape; leave it out.

## Sequence
It does turn, but late. Lines 1-6 are all scene-setting or a flat success (ruin, ash, name, scrap, fire, fire rule), with two fire lines back to back. The change comes at P7 (leaving the fire) and P8 (the quiet), so the situation changes only in the last two lines. P5 and P6 should be one beat. Nearly every line is a personification (kept, never got the news, has to move over, it leaves, keeping), a monotone register the flat-story note would flag. The set ends on dread rather than a clean win, which is right for the brief.
