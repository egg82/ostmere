# Regions

Five regions, five acts. Five holds: the spine's reveals fall into five clean questions (how to survive; how big is
the world; what is the burn and what am I; why did it break; what can be done), and ~2.5 weeks each matches the
strawman. Fewer would make each act too long to keep four beats a week from one region's material; more would split
R4/R5, whose reveals depend on each other.

Every region: new land, new resources, **new methods for existing skills** (no new skills), a threat roster that ramps
with time spent there (never with defences), outpost sites, joint projects, and one question it answers. Creatures in
detail: `creatures.md`. Day numbers are the typical route.

Each region also has one **linked-group creature** so cascades (rule 3, a jump along kin-links) have a target worth
building for: R1 wolf packs, R2 moth swarms, R3 crawler colonies, R4 wasp hives and lich-linked clerks, R5 strays
(all linked to the burn).

## R1, the Hollow (opens day 1; act days 1–21)

**Land.** A bowl valley on a warm seam. Scrub birch and thorn, ash-grey; a warm stream; old fields under ash; the
ruins (which are layered previous towns, `spine.md` B36). The camp sits on the keel's capstone, a flat warm stone.

**Question it answers:** can you live? (Yes, slowly, then less slowly.)

**Resources:** scrap, flint, deadwood, ashroot, seam-warm clay, wolf pelts and meat, crow feathers, tallow.

**Methods opened:** Scavenging (ruins, cellars), Woodcutting (birch, deadwood), Foraging (ashroot, lichen),
Cooking (stew, smoking: "spending" meat), Smithing (scrap iron), Crafting (stool, palisade stakes, sand-charcoal
filters), Arcana (heat moved from warm stone), Melee/Defence.

**Threats (ramp with days in R1):**

| Days in R1 | Roster |
|---|---|
| 1–7 | Ashrats in the stores; lone hungry wolves; crows at the drying racks |
| 8–14 | Wolf packs; greycoats (wolves that took from the dead) start appearing; Drift-only ash-bear |
| 15+ | Greycoat packs as the main threat; a greycoat leader (one per save, seeded); bandits from broken groups (light-tier human threat) |
| Rare, out of place | A ledger-hound (Chequer construct, R3-made) wanders in; sits at your feet, goes for whoever is beside you |

**Outposts:** none: the town grows in place here.
**Joint projects:** P0 camp (personal), P1 palisade, P2 hall, P3 ash road (opens R2). Later town projects built in
R1 with other regions' inputs: P7 forge, P8 scriptorium, P14 hearth.

**Reveals:** you're alone; the town (B3); magic as wonder (B6); the margin note (B9); the stool in the cellar (B12);
the sun (B13).

## R2, the Ashwood (opens day 20; act days 22–35)

**Land.** A dead forest of grey standing trunks killed in the first years, now a fungus forest: shelf brackets like
staircases, ash-caps, pale lattices. Warm patches where seams near the surface; Chequer road markers half-buried;
a waystation on a hill.

**Question:** how big is the world, and how odd are you? (Bigger, with a network; quite.)

**Resources:** charwood (dead hardwood that burns hot), resin, bracket fungus, ash-caps, spores (alchemy), elk hide,
moth-dust (silences), Chequer road-iron.

**Methods opened:** Woodcutting (charwood), Foraging (fungus by season), Cooking (drying, fungus preserving),
Crafting (resin, hide), Alchemy (spore tinctures, moth-dust), Arcana (rule 3: reagents as links), Wayfinding
(road markers, scouting the network), Ranged (silent hunting in hush).

**Threats:**

| Days in R2 | Roster |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Bracket-elk (dangerous in rut); hush-moth swarms (Drift); ash-boar |
| 6–12 | Mourners (latch-cats) start following supply lines; boar sounders |
| 13+ | Mourners raiding the logging line; the howlet (apex owl) in the Still |
| Rare | A stray (a loose working, a wandering flame that moves heat at random), drawn up from R5 along a seam |

**Outpost site:** the logging post (P4) on a warm patch by the old road. Raided by mourners; improved by a road and a
latched store.
**Joint projects:** P4 logging post (outpost), P5 waystation (Chequer ward woken: town labour and timber, your
Arcana), P6 lift (re-keying a Chequer lift down into the seams: rope, timber, your Arcana and Wayfinding).

**Reveals:** pilgrims (B18), N3 (B19), you're kept (B20), the faith (B21), like calls like (B23), the network map
and your camp on it (B24), the ward that opens to a Chequer seal, yours or a charcoal copy once (B25).

## R3, the Seams (opens day 36; act days 38–55)

**Land.** Underground and half-underground: cracks, galleries, hot pools, glowing rock; Chequer works (lifts, pipes
of glass, keel chambers). Warm, wet, lit red. The Warm live in a long gallery by a hot spring.

**Question:** what is the burn, and what are you? (The thing that keeps everyone alive; a holder.)

**Resources:** ore (iron, copper, a heat-holding red ore, "ruddle"), glass sand, mineral salts, sulphur, vent
crust, crawler chitin, eel oil (lamp oil that burns clean), Chequer glass.

**Methods opened:** Mining (all ores), Smithing (seam-heat forging, ruddle), Alchemy (salts, sulphur), Crafting
(glass, lenses), Cooking (eel, salt-curing), Spellcraft (hold shapes; heat bound into objects), Defence (chitin
armour), Scavenging (Chequer works).

**Threats:**

| Days in R3 | Roster |
|---|---|
| 1–6 | Seam crawler colonies (swarm on light); glass-eels in pools; keepers (constructs) guarding keel galleries: they step round you, file you, and fight your companions |
| 7–14 | Warm raiders (a faction split; human threat, tradeable with otherwise); kiln-salamanders (animals that move heat) |
| 15+ | The seam-worm (apex, burrows in hot rock); keeper patrols on the supply line |
| Rare | One of the Unpaid, lost, wandering up out of R4's undercroft |

**Outpost site:** the mining camp (P9) on a gallery by the Warm's spring. Trade line with the Warm.
**Joint projects:** P7 forge (seam heat piped to town: town stone and labour, your heat-binding), P8 scriptorium
(town building; books and the ability to read Chequer script; your Arcana), P9 mining camp (outpost), P10 causeway
(R3 stone over R4's drowned flats).

**Reveals:** vent-life, and the Warm's names scratched on a crack's lip and said into it, without faces (B27); the
keepers file you (B28); N6 (B29); the keel chamber (B30); the rules of holding (B31); the seam you hold shut and the
outpost it freezes (B32); N9 and the snare knot (B34); "the Hold's", and the Warm's custom taking a porter's wife's
face (B35, SB-face, O-price); the layered ruins (B36).

## R4, the Chequer seat (opens day 55; act days 57–70)

**Land.** Across drowned ash flats (a lake of grey slurry, a causeway), the ruin of a Chequer city on a hill:
the Tallyhouse (archive and registry), towers, a square of dead fountains. Wards still hold in places; some streets
are warm because the Unpaid draw heat there.

**Question:** why did it break? (A holder said no; the machine was built on people who wouldn't.)

**Resources:** books and records, Chequer tools and lenses, clockwork, fine glass, ward-stones (pre-made anchors),
wasp paper, silver.

**Methods opened:** Scavenging (archives: records as a resource), Arcana (reading Chequer workings; sense as a
reagent, rule 5, theory only), Crafting (clockwork, lenses), Smithing (silver; ward-inlay), Spellcraft (anchored
wards on stone), Wayfinding (city mapping), Ranged (wasp-hive smoke tactics).

**Threats:**

| Days in R4 | Roster |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Paper wasp hives; feral city cats; hollowed clerks (mostly harmless, some still guarding doors) |
| 6–12 | The Unpaid's warm streets: lich-linked clerks in groups; the first of the Unpaid |
| 13+ | Older Unpaid (each a named encounter, killed by finding and breaking its anchor); the Tallyhouse's held ward |
| Rare | A Leaner at the city's edge (R5), seen, not fought |

**Outpost site:** the Tallyhouse watch (P11) in a fortified gatehouse; reading room for the scribes.
**Joint projects:** P11 Tallyhouse watch (outpost), P12 cold road (a line of held lamps out to R5: ruddle, eel
oil, Chequer lenses, your hold-shaped spells; the town's labour sets them).

**Reveals:** the dead city and the Unpaid (B38), N6's daybook and its struck line (B39), the registry (B40), the keepers' orders (B41), the
Breaking's record and N3 (B42), N6's plan (B44).

## R5, Caldrigg (opens day 70; act days 71–84)

**Land.** Past the last lamp: cold first, a frozen grey waste where heat was taken at the Essart, with things
stilled mid-step; then rising warmth and a red glow on the horizon that is never reached. Frost-grey scarps with
lichen; dunes of old ash. The refuser's keel.

**Question:** what is it, and what can be done? (Unanswerable; something smaller.)

**Resources:** cauk (a pale stone that holds workings very well: a near-perfect
anchor), live cinders (strays caught in lamps), scarp lichen, stilled things' remains (alchemy), frost-glass.

**Methods opened:** Mining (cauk), Alchemy (cinders), Spellcraft (anchors of cauk, the keel grammar),
Crafting (cold gear), Wayfinding (navigating by the glow), Cooking (lichen, goat), Defence (cold).

**Threats:**

| Days in R5 | Roster |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Cold itself (gear decides); tahr (harmless, hunted); strays in swarms |
| 5–10 | Ounces (apex, live in the hollows under the scarps); Leaners (stand and lean; do not fight unless approached; never explained) |
| 11+ | Old Unpaid who walked out here toward the heat and are still walking |
| Rare | Something the size of a hill moving in the dunes at night. Never fought. Seen twice at most. |

**Outpost site:** the last lamp (P13), a held lamp-house at the cold's edge.
**Joint project:** P13 last lamp (outpost). The final project P14, the Hearth, is built **at home** in the Hollow
with inputs from every region (R1 stone and labour, R2 charwood, R3 ruddle, R4 records and the chronicle, R5
cauk; your knowledge of the keel grammar). Under E-d its act is the roll: the households' names entered at N8's
table (B51).

**Reveals:** the cold that turns the first push back (B46), the even draw (B47), the refuser's keel (B48), the torn
pages and town 3's failed design (B49).

## Death (the player's own)

From B20: a death returns you to the warm stone at camp, the Grim Dawn ceiling. It costs no progress, levels or stores;
it comes only from a fight you chose or a story beat; the attempt is wasted as with any lost fight. Each return adds
one journal margin line of a small, trivial forgetting (flavour; never a recipe, an odd, a name of a notable).
Progression from death happens once, as B20. No one in town finds it remarkable.
