# Cast

Two kinds of notable:
- **Fixed** (authored, the same person every save): the spine needs who they are. N1, N3, N4, N6, N9.
- **Risen** (an authored role, cast per save from whoever is in town when its gate opens: the light-tier person with
  the best-fitting traits and history). Their soul is the save's; their arc beats are authored. N2, N5, N7, N8, N10.

All notables **never die** and **never betray you** (betrayal stays on the light tier). They may be in danger and
scrape through. Each rises from the light tier through something that happens, never by title.

## Notables

### N1, Lucy (fixed): the first one
- **Archetype:** the hard practical survivor; the voice of "somebody has to".
- **Arrives:** B3, alone, the last of five (lost before she arrived; never shown, and canon only: "the last of five"
  never reaches player text).
- **Wants:** for the fire to still be lit tomorrow. Later: for somebody else to say no for once.
- **Rises:** at B14 with the hall, as the one who brings you most offers. Most "you said yes" lines are her looking
  at you.
- **Arc:** from survival to the town's conscience. Late (after B40) she offers you a job and visibly hopes you'll
  decline; whatever the player does, she says nothing about it. Epilogue: she runs the work, and keeps asking.
- **Trope:** the first follower who stays. Pays off as promised.

### N2, the cook (risen at B14)
- **Role:** keeper of the hall's fire and pot; the town's gossip and heart; chatter centre.
- **Cast from:** whoever has cooked most for others among arrivals of the survival tier.
- **Arc:** feeds whoever comes; a feud over rations (thin weeks) tests them; becomes the belonging tier's face. Under
  E-d, they're first in the queue at N8's table (B51).

### N3, Sorrel (fixed): the one who says no
- **Archetype:** the gentle, damaged stranger (trope pays off as a turn, `world.md`).
- **Truth:** the holder of the first keel, who refused and let go, causing the Breaking ~40 years ago. They are
  sealed, as every holder was. When they let go, the keel's load cascaded away along the links, but the part of the
  working that holds its holder had nothing left to hold, so it keeps itself on N3's own memory, one day at a time:
  they are unageing and **forget each day**. They've walked for forty years and remember this morning.
- **Arrives:** B19, at the Ashwood's edge. Townsfolk name them (seeded from a list of plants; "Sorrel" is the default).
- **Tell:** **they decline anything that would keep or bind them** (a place in a house, a hand, a rope to hold, a
  name), gently, and can't say why. They accept what asks nothing back: food, a blanket, later the book. Their sleeves
  stay down, so their wrist seal goes unseen until B33, when they copy its letters to learn them. No flinch.
- **Wants:** to stop waking frightened.
- **Arc:** O-book (B19): a book to write in. If accepted, their pages become a daily diary in the game's journal
  style. It contradicts itself from one day to the next, and they don't notice. B42 lays the keel name they copied
  beside the refuser's; B43 offers O-tell. If told, they take it quietly, and the next morning have forgotten; the
  book remembers for them. E-c: they say yes for the first time, their first binding yes.
- **Never:** a villain. The refusal was right and broke the world anyway.

### N4 (fixed): Old Warmth's words
- **Role word:** bedesman (not "faith-keeper"); N4 for short.
- **Archetype:** the hedge-priest; faith that is partly right.
- **Arrives:** B21; walks from seam to seam laying hands on warm stones.
- **Wants:** the dead burned with the right words, and named. Keeps the faith's one taboo, *never thank the warmth*,
  and says the names of the dead at every burning, asking for the names nobody gave.
- **Rises:** on arrival, through the burnings (they know the words, and the town has no one else who does).
- **Arc:** the only one who finds your return (B20) worth a second look, and decides it's a blessing. Their faith is
  shaken by B32 (the warmth is a fire someone lit) and B47 (the even draw), and doesn't break; it changes. Under E-d
  they read the roll's new names at the Hearth.
- **Rule:** faith is never a mechanic; they are not a "priest" building or role bonus.

### N5, the tracker (risen at B22)
- **Role:** first tracker (Trackers town skill); scouts supply lines; turns unknown into known with you.
- **Cast from:** a forager or hunter with the most expeditions.
- **Arc:** loves the Ashwood, hates the Seams; the one who marks the Rim's edge at B51.

### N6, Thompson (fixed): the stronger mage
- **Archetype:** the old mentor with a secret. Trope pays off as promised: he teaches, his secret is a confession.
- **Truth:** a Chequer tallier (accounting-mage of the keels) who signed holders' papers, yours among them. To
  survive the Breaking he moved his years into a ledger he carries: a held working, rule 4, which he has been paying
  for with his own memories. He's a little of what the Unpaid became, stopped short.
- **Arrives:** B29, drawn by the forge (esteem tier: the skilled arrive). Looks sixty; is past ninety.
- **Wants:** to be useful before he forgets why he's sorry.
- **The daybook:** he did the costing the keels were built on. It priced the upkeep from *remembrance of the
  deceased* and struck the line, *rejected, supply irregular*; beside it, in his hand, *requires the leave of every
  household; not obtainable*. That is his premise, and it is wrong: leave can be given per name.
- **Arc:** looks at your wrist (B29), says nothing; teaches the rules of holding (B31); at B39 the player finds the
  daybook first, he sees that it's been read, and he doesn't deny it: he stays and teaches harder. He proposes the
  Chequer way (B44) because it works, and because his own note says there is no other. Helps with whichever the player
  chooses; under E-b he rebinds you. Stronger than you at magic throughout; never the hero.

### N7, the smith (risen at B29)
- **Role:** master of the forge; the esteem tier's face.
- **Cast from:** the arrival whose history includes metalwork, or the most-used crafter.
- **Arc:** competitive with you, then fond; never manages to fix the stool, and it bothers them for the rest of the
  game.

### N8, the scribe (risen at B33)
- **Role:** keeper of the chronicle; reads Chequer script.
- **Cast from:** a light-tier person who came for food and shelter and is sharp and curious (the decisions doc's
  example of rising).
- **Arc:** reads wrist seals once the script is readable (B33); reads the registry with you (B40); writes the town's
  history down. Under E-d they keep the roll: the climax is the queue at their table (B51), and every later death is
  entered there.

### N9, Margaret (fixed): the girl you looked after
- **Archetype:** the witness who remembers you.
- **Truth:** at eight she lived in town 1, your first town after the Breaking; you taught her to set snares. She's in
  her late forties now and walks with a stick.
- **Arrives:** B34, at belonging tier, looking for a hall and people.
- **Arc:** recognises the snare knot before she recognises you (B34), ties it back and says nothing, and works near
  you from then on; knows where the old town's hall stood (directs B36's dig); at B40 works out the binding date's
  arithmetic and says nothing. Helps N8 write. Under E-d she loses her mother's face (B52). Epilogue: teaches a child
  to set snares, the way you did.

### N10, the cellar boss (risen, emergent)
- **Role:** the leader of a clique that hardens into a gang (`decisions.md`, "People have social lives").
- **Cast from:** the social system: whoever leads the clique when it forms (thin rations, the Drift, the Intact
  versus the Bereaved).
- **Arc:** an emergent quest decides it: broken up, absorbed into the guard, or grown. Never dies, never betrays you;
  can betray light-tier townsfolk. S-factions reads the outcome.

## Arrivals by tier

Player text names each tier by what draws people, never by the tier word: fire, wall, table, forge, books.

| Tier | Draws (*player-facing name*: what you built) | Who comes | Mini-story ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | *fire*: fire, food to spare | The lone and desperate; broken groups; deserters from nobody's army; scavengers who know the ruins | Who they lost on the road (past, off screen); what they carried |
| Safety | *wall*: palisade, guards, roofs | Families, always missing someone; the old; the sick | The Intact (rare): spared families who know the horrors, and families who somehow have no idea; their effect on a town of the bereaved |
| Belonging | *table*: hall, shared meals | Widowers, musicians, a choir of three, people walking toward any light | Feuds over who sits where; first weddings; the first child born in town |
| Esteem | *forge*: forge, workshops | Masons, a glassblower, a tanner, a Chequer-trained clerk who keeps asking for forms | Guild habits from before; the clerk forming a committee |
| Self-actualization | *books*: scriptorium, the Chequer records | Scholars, a painter who paints only the Clear, a hedge-mage weaker than you, people wanting to know why | Arguments about what happened; the Ledger clique |

Groups arrive broken (deaths decided on arrival, `decisions.md`). Losses of the young are only ever past or off
screen, sad, never for shock; for runtime lines the rate is a code number (`young_loss_rate`, start at most one line
in a few weeks of play), never a frequency word.
